Houston and Toronto each hold 329 of 1,000 combinations for the No. 1 pick on Sunday, Nov. 15. Houston's most likely result is No. 3, at 38.3 percent. The team with the most combinations won the No. 1 pick in 10 of the 16 lotteries where a WNBA.com page states the answer.

## Chance of each 2027 lottery pick

Key finding: Houston's most likely pick is No. 3 (38.3 percent), not No. 1 (32.9 percent). Toronto's most likely pick is No. 1 (32.9 percent). No. 3 is next, at 29.5 percent.

Houston is one of the two drawn picks 61.7 percent of the time. The other 38.3 percent, Houston is the worst record left and picks third. Toronto is also one of the two drawn picks 61.7 percent of the time. Toronto picks third when Houston is drawn and Toronto is not (29.5 percent). Toronto picks fourth when neither of them is drawn (8.8 percent). In that 8.8 percent, Houston is still third.

The same arithmetic sets a floor for every row. Washington cannot fall past fifth (2.2 percent there). Seattle cannot land on third. Portland cannot land on third or fourth. Los Angeles's own pick cannot land on third, fourth, or fifth. The Phoenix ticket, if it is not drawn first or second, is seventh every time (97.2 percent).

The two Los Angeles rows are separate tickets. Added together they are 36 combinations, 3.6 percent, at No. 1. They cannot both be the No. 1 pick.

Winning percentage on the release is .250 for Houston and for Toronto. The other percentages, from the records in that table, are Chicago .295 (26-62), Seattle .352 (31-57), Portland .386 (17-27), Los Angeles .420 (37-51), and Phoenix .489 (43-45).

## When it is, and where to watch

The [Oct. 28, 2026 release](https://www.wnba.com/news/wnba-draft-lottery-2027-sunday-november-15) sets the 26th annual lottery for Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, from 7:30 to 8:00 p.m. ET, on ESPN, after UConn vs. Louisville. WNBA head of league operations [Bethany Donaphin](https://www.wnba.com/news/wnba-draft-lottery-2027-sunday-november-15) reveals the results. Ernst & Young oversees the drawing. TSN carries it in Canada. The same release says this is the 15th straight year an ESPN network has carried the lottery. The draft is Monday, April 12, 2027. The sponsor line on the release is State Street Investment Management SPY.

TV: ESPN, 7:30-8:00 p.m. ET, after UConn vs. Louisville. Streaming: the Oct. 28 release does not name a U.S. app. In Canada, TSN.

On Nov. 8, 2026, the [@WNBA](https://x.com/WNBA/status/2119459932399649169) account posted "ONE WEEK OUT" and the same date and time, 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN. The post's timestamp is 16:00 UTC.

Six clubs take part: Houston, Los Angeles, Portland, Seattle, Toronto, and Washington. Phoenix traded its first-round pick to Los Angeles. Chicago traded its first-round selection to Washington. The seven non-playoff records are Chicago, Houston (previously the Connecticut Sun), Los Angeles, Phoenix, and Seattle on the combined 2025 and 2026 records, and Portland and Toronto on 2026 only. Portland and Toronto are the 2026 expansion teams. Their rosters and the move are on the [expansion page](/news/wnba-expansion-teams/).

## Where the two traded picks came from

Washington's row is Chicago's record, 26-62, and Chicago's 161 combinations. The [Sky release](https://www.wnba.com/news/chicago-sky-acquire-ariel-atkins) of Feb. 23, 2025, says Chicago acquired [Ariel Atkins](/wnba/ariel-atkins/) and Washington acquired the 2025 No. 3 pick, Chicago's 2027 second-round pick, and the right to swap 2027 first-round picks. The [Mystics release](https://mystics.wnba.com/news/washington-mystics-acquire-third-overall-picki-in-the-wnba-draft) the same day says the swap is Washington's own 2027 first-round pick with Chicago's own. The Oct. 28 lottery release says Chicago traded its own 2027 first-round pick to Washington for Atkins, and it assigns the 161 combinations to Washington.

Washington is not in the lottery on its own record. The release lists Washington only through Chicago. Chicago's pick is one of the seven lottery slots. A swap of Washington's own pick for Chicago's own pick puts that slot with Washington, which is the assignment the lottery release prints.

The lottery release says Phoenix traded its own 2027 first-round pick and [Monique Akoa Makani](/wnba/monique-akoa-makani/) to Los Angeles for [Kelsey Plum](/wnba/kelsey-plum/) on Aug. 2, 2026. ## What the release does not say about the draft class

The lottery release does not name a player. It assigns picks, not prospects.

[USC's 2026-27 roster](https://usctrojans.com/sports/womens-basketball/roster/juju-watkins/18627) lists JuJu Watkins as a senior, a guard, and says she redshirted the 2025-26 season because of injury. The personal line on that page says she was born July 15, 2005, so she turns 22 in 2027.

Article XIII, Section 1(b) of the [WNBPA collective bargaining agreement](https://www.wnbpa.com/_files/ugd/8121a3_675911316ca142178932adc1e56519ac.pdf) says a player is eligible for the draft if she will be at least 22 during the calendar year of the draft, or if she has graduated or her original class is graduating within three months after the draft, and if she has no remaining college eligibility or renounces it in writing at least 10 days before the draft. A shorter window applies after her last NCAA game. This page has the birth date and the senior listing. It does not have a renunciation letter, and it does not have an NCAA eligibility ledger. The redshirt year means this page cannot say she is out of college eligibility. It does not say she will be in the April 12, 2027 draft.
