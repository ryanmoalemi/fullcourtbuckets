The [Golden State Valkyries](/wnba/teams/golden-state-valkyries/) beat the [Atlanta Dream](/wnba/teams/atlanta-dream/) 68-63 in Game 7 on Saturday at Chase Center and won the 2026 WNBA Finals, 4-3. [Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/) scored 30 points, 15 of them in the third quarter.

## A 9-point hole, then 15 points from Williams

The Dream led 30-24 at halftime and 37-28 with 5:59 left in the third, after [DeWanna Bonner](/wnba/dewanna-bonner/)'s layup. That was Atlanta's largest lead. Golden State then scored 17 points to Atlanta's 7 and led 45-44 at 1:05 of the third. Williams scored 15 of Golden State's 21 third-quarter points. After the score reached 37-34, the Valkyries' last 11 points of the quarter were hers: threes at 2:58, 2:22 and 2:00, then a turnaround jumper at 1:05. Those three threes came 58 seconds apart, from the first to the third.

Williams finished with 30 points on 10 of 23 shooting, 4 of 8 from three and 6 of 6 at the line, with 3 rebounds, 2 assists, 2 steals, 4 blocks and a plus-7. She had 2 points at halftime. Watch the sequence on the [Game 7 play-by-play](/wnba/finals/2026/game-7/play-by-play/).

The fourth quarter was tied at 57 with 2:41 left, after [Rhyne Howard](/wnba/rhyne-howard/)'s layup. Williams' 31-foot three at 2:18, assisted by [Veronica Burton](/wnba/veronica-burton/), made it 57-60. Golden State led the rest of the way. Williams made two free throws at 1:40 for her 30th point and a 62-57 lead. [Kiah Stokes](/wnba/kiah-stokes/) tipped in a miss with 53.8 seconds left to make it 64-57. [Allisha Gray](/wnba/allisha-gray/) answered with a three at 34.2 seconds, assisted by Howard, to make it 64-60. Burton made two free throws with 5.1 seconds left for the final margin, 68-63.

## The crowd, the series and where it was shown

Game 7 drew 18,064, a sellout, at Chase Center ([WNBA.com game page](https://www.wnba.com/game/atl-vs-gsv-1042600307)). That is the same attendance figure on the official pages for Games 1, 2 and 5 at Chase Center. The [FCB Finals attendance file](/news/2026-wnba-finals-ratings-tracker/) now has 123,553 fans through seven games.

Saturday was the first seventh game since the WNBA made the Finals a best-of-seven in 2025 ([WNBA announcement](https://www.wnba.com/news/wnba-finals-format-expansion-2025)). The Las Vegas Aces swept Phoenix in four games in that first series ([WNBA](https://www.wnba.com/news/las-vegas-aces-finals-accolades-2025)).
