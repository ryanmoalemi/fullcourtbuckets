The [Golden State Valkyries](/wnba/teams/golden-state-valkyries/) have scored 9 points in the third quarter of both WNBA Finals games. The [Atlanta Dream](/wnba/teams/atlanta-dream/) won those two quarters 38-18. Golden State won the other six quarters 115-105.

That split is the series so far. Golden State took Game 1 68-64 at Chase Center, and Atlanta took Game 2 79-65 to tie it 1-1. Game 3 is Wednesday in Atlanta.

## Finals Game 3 at a glance

- When: Wednesday, Oct. 21, at 5 p.m. PT
- Where: State Farm Arena, Atlanta
- TV and streaming: USA Network and CNBC, streaming on Peacock ([Valkyries schedule update](https://valkyries.wnba.com/news/wnba-finals-schedule-updates-20261013))
- Series: Tied 1-1
- Next: Game 4 is Saturday, Oct. 24, at 10 a.m. PT at State Farm Arena, on NBC and Peacock ([Valkyries schedule update](https://valkyries.wnba.com/news/wnba-finals-schedule-updates-20261013))

Game 3 was moved up from Thursday, Oct. 22, and out of the Dream's usual home arena to State Farm Arena. It is the first WNBA Finals game in the Atlanta market since 2013, the league said when it announced the move.

## Injury report

The league has not posted a Game 3 injury report yet. Its last report, from Oct. 19 at 5:30 p.m. PT, listed one player across both teams: Atlanta's [Brionna Jones](/wnba/brionna-jones/) (left leg), out ([WNBA injury report](https://ak-static.cms.nba.com/referee/wnba_injury/Injury-Report_2026-10-19_05_30PM.pdf)).

[Tiffany Hayes](/wnba/tiffany-hayes/) was not listed for Game 2 and scored 14 points in 25 minutes. [Iliana Rupert](/wnba/iliana-rupert/) has been away from Golden State all season for a non-injury reason.

## What happened in Game 2

Atlanta won 79-65 behind [Angel Reese](/wnba/angel-reese/), who had 30 points and 16 rebounds on 10-of-16 shooting. A 19-2 Atlanta run that started late in the third quarter turned a 45-44 game into a 64-46 lead. [Rhyne Howard](/wnba/rhyne-howard/) had 6 steals, a Finals record, and Atlanta had 14 as a team ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/dream-valkyries-score-wnba-finals-game-2-8561417b17cc6fb3e3e890e331fb6525)). It was the franchise's first Finals win. Atlanta had gone 0-10 in its three previous Finals trips. Our [Game 2 recap](/news/dream-valkyries-finals-game-2-recap/) has the full sequence.

"For me, I think I let my team down last game not really contributing as much as I could in the second half," Reese said ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/dream-valkyries-score-wnba-finals-game-2-8561417b17cc6fb3e3e890e331fb6525)).

## The free-throw line

Both games had the same shape from the field. In Game 2 each team made 21 field goals and 7 threes. The difference was the line: Atlanta went 30 of 35, Golden State 16 of 19.

Across both games, Atlanta has made 48 of 56 free throws and Golden State 27 of 35. Golden State has committed 36 fouls to Atlanta's 32, including 23 in Game 2.

## The possession count flipped back

Before Monday, Golden State had come out ahead on turnovers plus offensive rebounds in all seven of its playoff games, including Game 1, when Atlanta finished minus-9. In Game 2 Atlanta was plus-6: Golden State committed 18 turnovers to Atlanta's 14, and Atlanta won the offensive glass 8-6.

These are player turnovers from FCB's game files, which leave out team turnovers.

[Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/), who scored 26 in Game 1, had 14 on 3-of-15 shooting in Game 2. Golden State had 10 assists, down from 15 in Game 1.

## My pick

My pick is Atlanta to win Game 3. The Dream are at home, they have won both third quarters by a combined 20 points, and they won the possession count on Monday for the first time this series. Two games is a small sample, so this is a lean, not a lock.
