Atlanta has shot 43 of 151 threes (28.5%) and Golden State 43 of 163 (26.4%). Atlanta has also outscored Golden State 437-404, a 33-point edge, with the series tied.

## A 2-0 lead, then a 13-0 run

The WNBA moved the Game 6 tip from 8 p.m. ET to 12:30 p.m. ET so it could be played at State Farm Arena. The Hawks moved their game that night to 8 p.m. ET to make room ([WNBA announcement, Oct. 26](https://www.wnba.com/news/2026-wnba-finals-game-6-location-announcement)).

[Veronica Burton](/wnba/veronica-burton/) made a step-back with 9:19 left in the first quarter to give Golden State a 2-0 lead, the only lead the Valkyries held. [Allisha Gray](/wnba/allisha-gray/) answered with a three at 9:04, and the [official WNBA.com box score](https://www.wnba.com/game/gsv-vs-atl-1042600306/box-score) has Golden State leading for 15 seconds. The game was never tied.

[Naz Hillmon](/wnba/naz-hillmon/) was called for a defensive three-second technical, and [Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/) made the free throw to make it 6-5. Atlanta then scored 13 straight points to go up 19-5 before Williams hit a three at 3:49. The Dream led 32-15 after the quarter. Those 32 points are the most by either team in any quarter of this series, and the 17-point margin is the largest end-of-quarter lead. Atlanta led 46-26 at halftime. Golden State made 9 of 34 shots in that half, with three blocked shots counted as attempts on the [Game 6 play-by-play](/wnba/finals/2026/game-6/play-by-play/).

## Gray, Howard and a flagrant call

Atlanta shot 13 of 30 from three (43.3%) and 27 of 62 overall (43.5%). Golden State shot 7 of 27 from three (25.9%) and 22 of 70 overall (31.4%). Gray made 8 of 12 shots and 5 of 7 threes for 23 points, with 6 rebounds, 3 assists, 2 steals and 3 blocks. On Friday, Oct. 23, with Atlanta up 2-1, she told the AP, "I am literally playing with eight fingers" ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/gray-dream-hand-ca7cd78a091d37cb1210b4f29391d1a2)). [Rhyne Howard](/wnba/rhyne-howard/) scored 21 on 6 of 11 shooting, 4 of 6 from three and 5 of 5 at the line, with 4 assists. She picked up her sixth foul, a shooting foul, with 1:15 left.

With 7:23 left in the third, Gray stole a [Tiffany Hayes](/wnba/tiffany-hayes/) pass. Burton was called for a flagrant-1 on the possession after officials reviewed the play and ruled she made an unnecessary windup and contact with Howard's face as Howard drove to the basket ([ESPN](https://www.espn.com/wnba/story/_/id/50320645/allisha-gray-dream-beat-valkyries-force-game-7-wnba-finals)). The play log marks that review as a call overturned, and Howard made both free throws. Atlanta led 53-28. Golden State won the third quarter 21-17 but still trailed 63-47 entering the fourth. [Kaila Charles](/wnba/kaila-charles/) made threes at 1:06 and with 1.2 seconds left in the quarter, her only 6 points of the game.

[Angel Reese](/wnba/angel-reese/) finished with 15 points, 14 rebounds and 6 assists, and 6 turnovers, on 5 of 18 shooting and 2 of 8 from three. This was her fourth points-and-rebounds double-double of the series, following Games 1, 2 and 3. She has 78 rebounds in the six games: 12, 16, 12, 9, 15 and 14, the most by any player in a single WNBA Finals ([Sporting News](https://www.sportingnews.com/us/wnba/atlanta-dream/news/dreams-angel-reese-sets-finals-record-ensure-wnbas-first-game-7/b3133eb960f686a79a1bef84)). The previous mark was Sylvia Fowles' 75 rebounds (15.0 per game) in the five-game 2017 Finals ([WNBA](https://www.wnba.com/news/fowles-finishes-perfect-season-finals-redemption)). "The job is not done, and I'm looking forward to it. One more. Job's not done," Reese said after the game ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/oct/29/atlanta-dream-golden-state-valkyries-game-6-wnba-finals)). Atlanta's bench scored 4 points, all from [Isobel Borlase](/wnba/isobel-borlase/). [DeWanna Bonner](/wnba/dewanna-bonner/) was 0 of 2.

## Williams scored 26 but finished minus-15

Williams scored 26 points on 8 of 22 shooting and 7 of 8 free throws, added 3 steals and finished minus-15. Twenty-two of her 26 points came after halftime ([Game 6 play-by-play](/wnba/finals/2026/game-6/play-by-play/)). She matched her Game 1 performance of 26 points on 8-of-22 shooting and 7-of-8 free throws, though she was plus-16 in that 68-64 Valkyries win. Her three at 4:06 of the fourth, then a running layup and free throw at 1:15, got Golden State to 77-69. [Laeticia Amihere](/wnba/laeticia-amihere/) had 10 rebounds and was plus-14 in 20 minutes.

Gray answered both of Golden State's early fourth-quarter pushes. Her three at 9:03, assisted by Howard, made it 66-47, and after the Valkyries cut the lead to 68-55, her three at 6:33, again from Howard, made it 71-55.

Atlanta led 74-55 with 5:43 left after Reese's second three. Golden State scored 9 of the next 10 points, and the lead was 75-64 with 2:36 left. Gray's fifth three, assisted by Reese with 56 seconds left, made it 80-69.

[Kiah Stokes](/wnba/kiah-stokes/) scored 0 points on 0 of 2 shooting, with 7 rebounds, 0 blocks and a minus-25. She had at least 3 blocks in each of the first five games and 17 blocks through Game 5. Burton scored 8 points on 2 of 13 shooting and 0 of 6 from three, made all 4 free throws, and had 0 turnovers and 3 steals. Hayes, who played her first 10 WNBA seasons with the Dream ([Valkyries](https://valkyries.wnba.com/news/gameday-recap-20261029)), scored 15 on 6 of 10 shooting. [Cecilia Zandalasini](/wnba/cecilia-zandalasini/) scored 9 off the bench on 3 of 12. [Jordin Canada](/wnba/jordin-canada/) scored 10, and Hillmon had 7 points and was plus-15.

## The crowd and Game 7

Game 6 drew 17,099, a sellout, matching Games 3 and 4 at State Farm Arena. The [FCB Finals attendance file](/news/2026-wnba-finals-ratings-tracker/) now has 105,489 fans through six games. That is more than the 92,599 at the 2024 Finals, the highest series total in that file from 2002 through 2025. Game 7 has not been played, so the series total is not finished.

Game 7 is Saturday, Oct. 31, at 5 p.m. PT (8 p.m. ET) at Chase Center in San Francisco, Dream at Valkyries ([WNBA.com game page](https://www.wnba.com/game/atl-vs-gsv-1042600307)). Oct. 31 is Halloween. TV: USA Network and CNBC. Streaming: Peacock.

Saturday will mark the first Game 7 in WNBA Finals history. In 2025 the league replaced the best-of-five Finals with a best-of-seven ([WNBA announcement](https://www.wnba.com/news/wnba-finals-format-expansion-2025)). The Las Vegas Aces swept Phoenix in four games in that first series ([WNBA](https://www.wnba.com/news/las-vegas-aces-finals-accolades-2025)). The [FCB Finals file](/news/2026-wnba-finals-ratings-tracker/) from 2002 through 2025 includes no seventh game, as the 2025 series ended in four.
