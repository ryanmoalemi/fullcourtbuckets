The [Atlanta Dream](/wnba/teams/atlanta-dream/) beat the [Golden State Valkyries](/wnba/teams/golden-state-valkyries/) 79-65 in Game 2 of the WNBA Finals on Monday at Chase Center, tying the series 1-1. [Angel Reese](/wnba/angel-reese/) had 30 points and 16 rebounds, and a 19-2 Atlanta run that started late in the third quarter turned a one-point game into a 64-46 lead.

Both teams made exactly 21 field goals and exactly 7 threes. The whole 14-point margin came at the free-throw line, where Atlanta went 30 of 35 and Golden State went 16 of 19.

## The third quarter, again

Golden State scored 9 points in the third quarter of Game 1. It scored 9 again on Monday.

[Veronica Burton](/wnba/veronica-burton/) hit a 26-foot three at 9:31 of the third for a 40-39 lead, Golden State's last lead of the night. [Kaitlyn Chen](/wnba/kaitlyn-chen/) still had the Valkyries within 45-44 at 3:52.

From there it was all Atlanta. [DeWanna Bonner](/wnba/dewanna-bonner/) scored on a running layup off a Reese assist at 3:06. [Allisha Gray](/wnba/allisha-gray/), Reese and Naz Hillmon all scored at the line, Reese added a layup and two more free throws, and [Jordin Canada](/wnba/jordin-canada/) hit a 32-foot three with 0.8 seconds left for a 60-46 lead after three.

Canada and Bonner each made two free throws in the first minute of the fourth, and by 9:00 it was 64-46. Atlanta outscored Golden State 19-2 over that 6:52 stretch.

## How the first half went

Chen's 5-footer with 2.0 seconds left gave Golden State a 21-18 lead after one, helped by two threes from [Kaila Charles](/wnba/kaila-charles/).

In the second quarter, the Valkyries scored 7 straight points at the line between 7:04 and 4:14, 5 of them from [Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/), to lead 33-27. Atlanta answered with a 10-0 run, capped by Gray's 25-foot running jumper at 1:31. [Tiffany Hayes](/wnba/tiffany-hayes/) tied it with two driving layups, and Bonner's 18-foot pull-up with 2.5 seconds left made it 39-37 Atlanta at the half.

## The fourth quarter

Atlanta's lead hit 21 at 69-48 on two Canada free throws at 6:06. Williams hit a 26-foot three and Charles a 23-footer at 4:32 to make it 71-55. On the next trip, Reese made a 24-foot three off a Canada pass at 4:15, her only three-point try of the night. Burton's 24-foot three at 1:30 made it 78-63, and the Valkyries never got closer than the final 14.

## The numbers that decided it

Our Game 2 preview focused on the turnover battle. Atlanta had won it in all five of its earlier playoff games, then lost it 12-8 in Game 1. On Monday it flipped back.

- **Turnovers.** Golden State turned it over 19 times and Atlanta 14. The Dream scored 24 points off those turnovers, the Valkyries 13.
- **Steals.** Atlanta had 14 steals, up from 5 in Game 1. [Rhyne Howard](/wnba/rhyne-howard/) had 6 and Bonner 4.
- **Fast breaks.** Atlanta outscored Golden State 19-0 in fast-break points.
- **Free throws.** Atlanta took 35 free throws after taking 21 in Game 1.

The preview also added the turnover margin and the offensive-rebound margin together. Golden State had won that combined count in all seven of its playoff games. Atlanta won it by 7 on Monday: by 5 on turnovers and 8-6 on the offensive glass. It's one game, but it's the first time this postseason the Valkyries lost that count.

Reese made 10 of 16 shots and 9 of 11 free throws and was +13. Canada had 13 points, 10 assists and 7 rebounds. Gray scored 18 and was +20, the best mark on either team.

Williams and Hayes scored 14 each for Golden State, but Williams went 3 of 15. [Kiah Stokes](/wnba/kiah-stokes/) had 11 rebounds and 4 blocks without attempting a shot, and the Valkyries had 10 assists on 21 baskets.

## What's next

My preview picked Golden State to win Game 2, and that missed. The betting lean, the under 152.5, hit with 144 total points.

Game 3 is Wednesday, Oct. 21, at 5 p.m. PT at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, on USA Network (simulcast on CNBC) and Peacock.

My pick is Atlanta in Game 3. Through two games, the Dream have outscored Golden State 38-18 in third quarters.
