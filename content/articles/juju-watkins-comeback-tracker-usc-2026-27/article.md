## The injury, in USC's words

On March 24, 2025, USC beat Mississippi State 96-59 in the NCAA second round. The [game recap](https://usctrojans.com/news/2025/3/24/womens-basketball-no-1-seed-usc-womens-basketball-scores-sweet-sixteen-spot-with-96-59-win-over-bulldogs) says Watkins sustained a season-ending injury in the first quarter, went to the floor, and did not return. The [March 26 release](https://usctrojans.com/news/2025/3/26/womens-basketball-usc-all-american-juju-watkins-sustains-season-ending-injury) says she would undergo surgery and miss the rest of that NCAA tournament. Neither release names the injury.

On Sept. 28, 2025, USC published her statement that she would sit out 2025-26. The sentence she is quoted as saying, in that [release](https://usctrojans.com/news/2025/9/28/womens-basketball-uscs-juju-watkins-to-miss-2025-26-season-to-focus-on-recovery): "Because you've been with me every step of the way, I wanted you to hear it from me directly that, following the advice of my doctors and trainers, I will sit out this season and fully focus on continuing to recover so I can come back to the game I love."

The [roster bio](https://usctrojans.com/sports/womens-basketball/roster/juju-watkins/18627) lists her as a senior guard and says she redshirted 2025-26 due to injury. The same bio gives her birthday as July 15, 2005, and describes her as a senior at Sierra Canyon who was co-MVP of the 2023 McDonald's All-American Game.

## Sunday, the Gamecocks, and the draft

The Charleston recap says USC plays No. 1 South Carolina on Sunday, Nov. 15, at Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, at 1 p.m. ET and 10 a.m. PT, on ABC. USC's [schedule](https://usctrojans.com/sports/womens-basketball/schedule/2026-27) lists the same day, 10:00 a.m., ABC, that arena, and radio on USCTrojans.com/Listen and the USC Gameday App. Neither listing names a streaming service.

The same evening, the [WNBA lottery FAQ](https://www.wnba.com/news/2027-wnba-draft-lottery-faq) says the 2027 WNBA Draft Lottery is on ESPN at 7:30 p.m. ET. The [Oct. 28 league release](https://www.wnba.com/news/wnba-draft-lottery-2027-sunday-november-15) says the special runs 7:30 to 8:00 p.m. ET and that the draft is Monday, April 12, 2027. Full Court Buckets' team odds are on the [2027 draft lottery page](/wnba/draft-lottery-2027/).

On age, two WNBA pages do not use the same test. The [league FAQ](https://www.wnba.com/faq) says domestic entrants have to be at least 22 during the draft year, and have no remaining college eligibility or renounce it. The [2026 draft FAQ](https://www.wnba.com/draft/2026/faq) says collegiate players turning 22 in 2025 could renounce remaining eligibility for the 2026 draft, and that seniors who had exhausted eligibility did not have to renounce. Watkins turns 22 on July 15, 2027, which is after April 12, 2027. This page does not treat either FAQ sentence as a ruling on the 2027 draft. It also did not find a statement from her, on the USC releases it read, about entering that draft.

Another college number from the same week is [Sarah Strong's 39 for UConn](/news/sarah-strong-39-points-uconn-record/).
