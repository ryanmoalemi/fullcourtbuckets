Every club plays 14 games, and every pairing is scheduled twice. Breeze is in six of the eight tour cities. Rose is in two, Los Angeles and San Francisco. Unrivaled posted the clubs on Nov. 6, on dates that were already on sale. Who plays in her own city is in the homecoming table. The player list is the [Season 3 rosters](/news/unrivaled-season-3-rosters/).

## Opening night, and the rematch

The [Nov. 6 release](https://www.unrivaled.basketball/news/unrivaled-reveals-season-three-matchups-and-clubs-competing-in-tour-stops-ftugcxnvnpa7) puts the first game at TD Garden at 7 p.m. ET. [Veronica Burton](/wnba/veronica-burton/) and Mist play [Tiffany Hayes](/wnba/tiffany-hayes/) and Phantom, a rematch of last season's final. Mist beat Phantom 80-74 on March 4, 2026, in the [league recap](https://www.unrivaled.basketball/game/jx1ocoygd5a4/recap). At 8:30 p.m. ET, [Paige Bueckers](/wnba/paige-bueckers/) and Breeze play [Flau'jae Johnson](/wnba/flaujae-johnson/) and Vinyl. The release calls that club Breeze BC presented by Thorne.

The same night at Intuit Dome, Rose plays Lunar Owls at 7 p.m. PT, which is 10 p.m. ET. The release calls [Napheesa Collier](/wnba/napheesa-collier/) and [Chelsea Gray](/wnba/chelsea-gray/) league MVPs. At 8:30 p.m. PT, Hive plays Laces. The release says four Notre Dame alums are in that game: [Sonia Citron](/wnba/sonia-citron/) and [Olivia Miles](/wnba/olivia-miles/) for Hive, [Jackie Young](/wnba/jackie-young/) and [Jessica Shepard](/wnba/jessica-shepard/) for Laces. Miles' college on the rosters file is TCU. The release calls her a Notre Dame alum in this game. [Cameron Brink](/wnba/cameron-brink/) and [Rae Burrell](/wnba/rae-burrell/) are the other Hive players the release names for Los Angeles. The release calls Hive "Hive BC presented by Chegg."

## The other tour stops

The Detroit stop, presented by Ally, is Jan. 11 at Little Caesars Arena. At 7 p.m. ET, Bueckers and [Kaitlyn Chen](/wnba/kaitlyn-chen/) on Breeze play [Breanna Stewart](/wnba/breanna-stewart/) and Mist. The release calls Bueckers and Chen 2025 NCAA champions and UConn alums, and it calls Chen "a 2026 WNBA" without the rest of that title. Chen is marked a 2026 champion on the rosters file because she is on the [Valkyries roster page](https://valkyries.wnba.com/roster). At 8:30 p.m. ET, [Kelsey Plum](/wnba/kelsey-plum/) and Phantom play [Rhyne Howard](/wnba/rhyne-howard/), [Allisha Gray](/wnba/allisha-gray/), and [Jordin Canada](/wnba/jordin-canada/) of Vinyl. The release says all three are coming off the 2026 WNBA Finals.

San Francisco the same day is Hive against Rose at 7 p.m. PT, then Laces against Lunar Owls at 8:30 p.m. PT. The release says Brink, a 2021 NCAA champion at Stanford, returns to the Bay Area to face [Lexie Hull](/wnba/lexie-hull/) and [Kayla Thornton](/wnba/kayla-thornton/) of Rose. It says [Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/), the 2026 Finals MVP, returns to Chase Center with Laces, against [Sydney Taylor](/wnba/sydney-taylor/), [Skylar Diggins](/wnba/skylar-diggins/), and Lunar Owls. Her 30-point Game 7 is in the [Game 7 recap](/news/dream-valkyries-finals-game-7-recap/).

The New York stop, presented by Maker's Mark, is Jan. 22 at Barclays Center, 7:30 and 9 p.m. ET. Lunar Owls play Breeze, then Hive plays Mist. The release calls [Marina Mabrey](/wnba/marina-mabrey/) a New Jersey native, Taylor a Long Island native, Miles a New Jersey native, and Citron a New York native. Those hometowns are not rows in the homecoming table. That table uses a bio that names the town, or a WNBA home arena. Miles is on the table for Target Center, not for New Jersey. The release also says Stewart, Burton, and [Arike Ogunbowale](/wnba/arike-ogunbowale/) return to the court where Mist reached the 2026 championship game.

Minneapolis is Jan. 24 at Target Center. Mist plays Lunar Owls at 1 p.m. CT. Breeze plays Hive at 2:30 p.m. CT. The release says co-founders Collier and Stewart play each other, and that Miles and Bueckers are the two most recent WNBA Rookies of the Year.

Houston is Feb. 12 at Toyota Center. Phantom plays Breeze at 6:30 p.m. CT. Vinyl plays Laces at 8 p.m. CT. ## How the rows were counted

Club games are the 56 games with two clubs. Each club has 14, and each of the 28 pairings is on the schedule twice. Tour-stop games are the 16 games at the eight road arenas, two per stop. Miami games with a set tip are the rest, except the four March 5 games, which are counted in the flex column and in the 14. The 1-on-1 nights are on the game file and not in the club totals.
