## Who can be cored, and who cannot

Article VI, Section 7(a) lets each team designate one veteran free agent as a core player. A player who is still under a contract she signed while designated counts against that one spot. Section 7(d) says the designation ends when the original term ends, or when she is traded, the contract ends, or she retires.

Section 7(b) limits designations made before the 2026 season: a player who had already played two seasons on a core contract could not be designated again. For 2027, the same section says only a player with six or fewer years of service may be designated. The example in the text is that a player may not be designated after the season in which she accrues a seventh year.

On that 2027 test, Collier, Young, Mitchell and Plum cannot be cored. The same is true of anyone else with seven or more seasons in the player file. Four seasons or fewer is not a core candidate in the ordinary case, because Section 7(a) applies the designation to a player who would otherwise be unrestricted or restricted. Reserved players are not in that sentence.

[Exhibit 4](https://www.wnbpa.com/_files/ugd/8121a3_675911316ca142178932adc1e56519ac.pdf) lists core service after the 2025 season, including Kelsey Mitchell in 2025, Kelsey Plum in 2025, and Breanna Stewart in 2024 and 2025. It does not say which of those contracts, if any, still run in 2027. This page does not mark any team's 2027 core spot as already used.

## What this file does not say

It does not say the contract is over. A player with eight seasons can be unrestricted and still be signed. The only 2026 term confirmed here from a team document is Collier's one-year deal in the Lynx notes. The February list is the one that will match names to statuses after the qualifying offers go out.

Salaries are not in the file. Developmental contracts are not marked, and Section 8 also reserves a player who finished the season on one of those if the team sends the offer. Players who did not appear in the 2026 regular-season file, including anyone whose contract expired while suspended, are not in the table. Article VI, Section 3 keeps a player who withheld services off the free-agent list unless the old team agrees.
