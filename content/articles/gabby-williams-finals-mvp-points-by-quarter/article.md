[Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/) was named 2026 WNBA Finals MVP after the [Golden State Valkyries](/wnba/teams/golden-state-valkyries/) beat the [Atlanta Dream](/wnba/teams/atlanta-dream/) 68-63 in Game 7 on Oct. 31. Across the seven games, she scored 137 points, with 85 coming after halftime.

## Gabby Williams' 2026 Finals points, by quarter

Williams scored 51 fourth-quarter points in the Finals, 22 more than any other player in the series. In Games 6 and 7, 50 of her 56 points came in the second half.

## Williams' record half in Game 7

The Dream led 30-24 at halftime of Game 7, when Williams had 2 points on 1-of-4 shooting. Through the first six games, the team leading at halftime had won every game, according to the [AP recap on WNBA.com](https://www.wnba.com/news/2026-finals-valkyries-dream-game-7-recap).

Williams then scored 28 points in the second half. The Valkyries [said](https://valkyries.wnba.com/news/gameday-recap-20261031) that was the most points by any player in one half of a WNBA postseason game. She scored 15 in the third quarter and 13 in the fourth. She made 4 of 6 threes after halftime, after making 10 of 46 in the series up to that point.

"She told me to stay patient, I trust her with my life," Williams said of coach Natalie Nakase's halftime message, [per the AP](https://www.wnba.com/news/2026-finals-valkyries-dream-game-7-recap).

With the game tied at 57 and 2:41 left, Williams hit a 3-pointer, then made two free throws with 1:40 to go. [Veronica Burton](/wnba/veronica-burton/) sealed it with two free throws with 5.1 seconds left. The full sequence is on FCB's [Game 7 play-by-play page](/wnba/finals/2026/game-7/play-by-play/), and the [Game 7 recap](/news/dream-valkyries-finals-game-7-recap/) has the series trend table.

"Give Gabby all the credit," Dream coach Karl Smesko said, [per The Athletic on WNBA.com](https://www.wnba.com/news/the-athletic-2026-gabby-williams). "She made us pay, and she made us pay repeatedly."

## Records the team confirmed

The Valkyries' [Game 7 release](https://valkyries.wnba.com/news/gameday-recap-20261031) lists these records and milestones:

- Her 18 steals broke Napheesa Collier's record for a Finals series.
- She joined Penny Taylor (2007) as the only players to score 30 or more in a winner-take-all Finals game.
- Her 30 points, 4 blocks and 2 steals in Game 7 made her the first player to post 30, 4 and 2 in a Finals game.
- She had nine playoff games with at least two steals, tied with Tamika Catchings and Chelsea Gray for the most in one postseason.
- [Kiah Stokes](/wnba/kiah-stokes/) finished with 19 blocks, a Finals series record.

## How Williams got here

The Chicago Sky drafted Williams No. 4 in 2018. Chicago traded her to the Los Angeles Sparks in 2021, and the Sparks sent her to the Seattle Storm the next year, [according to The Athletic](https://www.wnba.com/news/the-athletic-2026-gabby-williams). In her first seven WNBA seasons, she played 12 playoff games, the same number she played this postseason.

In 2025 with Seattle, she led the league with 99 steals and made her first All-Star team. She then signed a multi-year deal with Golden State, [the league announced](https://www.wnba.com/news/valkyries-sign-gabby-williams).

The Finals MVP award carries its own bonus under the 2026 labor deal. FCB's [Finals bonus breakdown](/news/wnba-finals-bonus-2026-champions-60000/) explains how a Finals MVP on the winning team could collect $90,000.
