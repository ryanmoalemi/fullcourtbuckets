Each eligible player on the [Golden State Valkyries](/wnba/teams/golden-state-valkyries/), who won the 2026 WNBA Finals 4-3, is in line for a $60,000 bonus, up from $22,908 per player in 2025. Eligible players on the runner-up [Atlanta Dream](/wnba/teams/atlanta-dream/) are in line for $20,000 each. [Gabby Williams](/wnba/gabby-williams/), named Finals MVP after 30 points in the 68-63 Game 7, is in line for a separate $30,000 award, six times the $5,000 paid in 2025. The dollar figures are the bonus schedule in the agreement the league and the players' union signed in March. The team site is the source for the series result and the MVP. The agreement does not say the awards have been paid.

A year ago, winning the midseason Commissioner's Cup paid more than winning the title. Players on the 2025 Cup champion Indiana Fever could earn up to $30,000 each, according to Sportico. The new deal keeps the Cup at $30,000 per player and pays twice that for the championship.

## 2025 vs. 2026 playoff bonuses

Every playoff bonus at least doubled under the new deal, and the Finals MVP award grew sixfold. Here's each one, 2025 vs. 2026:

| Bonus | 2025 | 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| WNBA champion, per player | $22,908 | $60,000 | 2.6x |
| Finals runner-up, per player | $8,521 | $20,000 | 2.3x |
| Lost in semifinals, per player | $3,435 | $10,000 | 2.9x |
| Lost in first round, per player | $1,778 | $5,000 | 2.8x |
| Finals MVP | $5,000 | $30,000 | 6x |

Sources: 2025 figures from the league's March 2026 summary of the deal; 2026 figures from the bonus table in the signed agreement. Methods: Change is the 2026 figure divided by the 2025 figure, rounded to one decimal.

## What each Finals roster stands to earn

The agreement's bonus table counts 12 players per team. On that basis:

- **The champion's roster:** $720,000 in title bonuses (12 x $60,000).
- **The runner-up's roster:** $240,000 (12 x $20,000).
- **The gap between winning and losing:** $40,000 per player, or $480,000 across a 12-player roster.

The Finals MVP award is listed as its own line in the agreement, separate from the team bonuses, and the table does not say one replaces the other. Under that reading, Finals MVP Gabby Williams would collect $90,000 in bonuses ($60,000 plus $30,000) if she also meets the team-bonus roster test. The table does not say how the award is handled if the MVP comes from the losing team, so that case is left out here. Nothing on the team site or in the agreement says either award has been paid.

Not every player who wore the jersey this season is automatically in line for a share. To earn a team bonus, a player must be on the team's regular-season roster on the last day of the regular season. If a player was on a playoff team's roster for two-thirds of its regular-season games but not on any roster on the final day, she gets $5,000 instead, as long as she did not earn a playoff team bonus with another team. The exact headcount on each Finals roster that qualifies has not been published.

## Why neither Finals team gets Cup money

The 2026 Commissioner's Cup went to the [New York Liberty](/wnba/teams/new-york-liberty/), who beat the [Las Vegas Aces](/wnba/teams/las-vegas-aces/) 93-85 in the Cup final on June 30 at Barclays Center. [Breanna Stewart](/wnba/breanna-stewart/) was named the game's MVP. Under the agreement, that win was worth $30,000 per player for 12 Liberty players. The Aces got $10,000 each as the Cup runner-up, and Stewart also earned the separate $10,000 Cup MVP award.

Neither the Valkyries nor the Dream reached the Cup final, so neither team adds Cup team money to its playoff bonus this season. Using the agreement's 12-player basis, the Liberty's Cup pool was $360,000, half of the $720,000 the champion's roster is in line for.

## Is "nearly tripled" right?

Close, but it rounds up. The AP described the $60,000 title bonus as "nearly triple" last year's amount. The actual ratio is 2.6 times. The larger multipliers are lower in the table: semifinal bonuses are up 2.9 times, first-round bonuses 2.8 times, and the Finals MVP award is up six times.

Last year's numbers were already a raise. The 2024 champion New York Liberty got $20,825 per player. When the Finals moved from best-of-five to best-of-seven in 2025, the league raised the pool, and each Las Vegas Aces player got $22,908, Sportico reported. Some outlets still list $20,825 as the 2025 figure. That is the 2024 amount.

## How the bonus compares to salaries

The agreement sets the 2026 minimum salary at $270,000 to $300,000, depending on years of service, and the league projects an average salary of $583,000. A $60,000 title bonus is 20% to 22% of a minimum contract and about 10% of the average.

Playoff bonuses are paid in addition to salary. Starting in 2027, every amount in the bonus table rises or falls at the same rate as the salary cap. The full 2026 schedule, including the Commissioner's Cup and individual awards like MVP and All-WNBA, totals $3 million.
