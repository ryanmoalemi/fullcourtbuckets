[Napheesa Collier](/wnba/napheesa-collier/) and [Kelsey Mitchell](/wnba/kelsey-mitchell/) each made $1,400,000 in 2026, the most in the 2027 unrestricted class. Re-signing can pay either up to $1,582,000 in 2027. Any other team is capped at $1,344,700, a $237,300 gap per season.

## Leaving is a pay cut for Collier and Mitchell

Under the new CBA, only a player's prior team can offer the supermax, and only if she has five or more years of service and played for that team in both of the last two seasons, or moved only by trade or waiver claim. Collier played both seasons for the [Minnesota Lynx](/wnba/teams/minnesota-lynx/) and Mitchell both for the [Indiana Fever](/wnba/teams/indiana-fever/).

Any other team can offer at most the standard maximum. At the highest possible 2027 cap that is $1,344,700, which is $55,300 less than each made in 2026. Staying could pay up to $1,582,000, a raise of $182,000. A lower cap shrinks both numbers, and the cost of leaving grows. Here is the first-year maximum at three possible 2027 caps:

| 2027 cap | Supermax (re-signing) | Standard max (any team) | Gap | Change from $1.4M if she re-signs | Change from $1.4M if she leaves |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $6,090,000 (lowest allowed) | $1,218,000 | $1,035,300 | $182,700 | -$182,000 | -$364,700 |
| $7,000,000 (unchanged) | $1,400,000 | $1,190,000 | $210,000 | $0 | -$210,000 |
| $7,910,000 (highest allowed) | $1,582,000 | $1,344,700 | $237,300 | +$182,000 | -$55,300 |

Methods: the CBA limits the 2027 cap to 13% above or below the 2026 cap of $7,000,000 (Article VII, Section 1), so it must land between $6,090,000 and $7,910,000. ## What else the data shows

- **10 of 31.** Collier, Mitchell, [Jackie Young](/wnba/jackie-young/), [Kelsey Plum](/wnba/kelsey-plum/), [Kayla Thornton](/wnba/kayla-thornton/), [Sophie Cunningham](/wnba/sophie-cunningham/), [Tiffany Hayes](/wnba/tiffany-hayes/), [Rachel Banham](/wnba/rachel-banham/), [Cheyenne Parker-Tyus](/wnba/cheyenne-parker-tyus/) and [Emma Cannon](/wnba/emma-cannon/) have a clear supermax path with their 2026 teams. For each of them, re-signing at the maximum would pay $237,300 more than any other team could.
- **$582,001 for Plum.** The [Los Angeles Sparks](/wnba/teams/los-angeles-sparks/) traded Plum to the [Phoenix Mercury](/wnba/teams/phoenix-mercury/) on Aug. 2 ([WNBA.com](https://www.wnba.com/news/rapid-reactions-trade-deadline-2026)). The CBA counts a trade as an assignment, so the trade kept her path intact and Phoenix can offer up to $1,582,000, which is $582,001 above her $999,999 in 2026.
- **No supermax path for Griner.** [Brittney Griner](/wnba/brittney-griner/) made the 2026 standard maximum, $1,190,000, but she signed with the [Connecticut Sun](/wnba/teams/connecticut-sun/) as a free agent after playing 2025 in Atlanta. Starting in 2027, only a player with six or fewer years of service can be named a Core Player (Article VI, Section 7), and she is listed at 12. Her ceiling anywhere is $1,344,700.
- **25 of 31 are 30 or older.** The median age is 33. Their 2026 pay adds up to $18,887,499.
- **Mitchell led the class at 24.7 points per game.** Next were Plum (21.5 in 17 games), Young (18.9), [Nneka Ogwumike](/wnba/nneka-ogwumike/) (17.3) and Collier (16.8 in 16 games).
- **Ogwumike is not expected back.** She announced on Aug. 19 that she would retire from the WNBA after the 2026 season ([Sparks release on WNBA.com](https://www.wnba.com/news/nneka-ogwumike-announces-wnba-retirement)). She stays in the table because her 2026 contract ended.

## Key dates and caveats

Teams can send qualifying offers from Jan. 30 through Feb. 3, 2027. Negotiations open Feb. 4 and new contracts can be signed starting Feb. 16 ([CBA](https://www.wnbpa.com/_files/ugd/8121a3_675911316ca142178932adc1e56519ac.pdf)). This table will be refreshed when the league announces the 2027 cap and as players sign after Feb. 16.

- **The cap is not set.** All 2027 maximums are ceilings at $7,910,000, the highest cap the CBA allows.
- **Supermax paths are Full Court Buckets' reading of the CBA,** not a league ruling. A Core Player designation can also open the supermax, but in 2027 only for players with six or fewer years of service. Seven players are listed at six or fewer: Cunningham, [Kennedy Burke](/wnba/kennedy-burke/), [DiJonai Carrington](/wnba/dijonai-carrington/), [Tyasha Harris](/wnba/tyasha-harris/), [Temi Fagbenle](/wnba/temi-fagbenle/), [Kierstan Bell](/wnba/kierstan-bell/) and [Awak Kuier](/wnba/awak-kuier/).
- **Laney-Hamilton is unclear.** Washington was awarded [Betnijah Laney-Hamilton](/wnba/betnijah-laney-hamilton/) off waivers from New York on Aug. 8, 2026 ([WNBA.com transactions](https://www.wnba.com/draft/2026/teams/washington-mystics)). A waiver claim counts as an assignment, but she missed the 2025 season, and the CBA test looks at contracts covering both of the last two seasons.
- **Listed years are not CBA years of service.** The 2027 minimum column is an estimate. Kuier (3), Fagbenle (4) and Bell (4) are listed below the CBA's five-year line for unrestricted free agency, so their status depends on service the public list does not show.
- **Eight veterans are listed at $277,500,** below the CBA's 2026 minimum for players with four or more years of service. Those figures are published as listed.
