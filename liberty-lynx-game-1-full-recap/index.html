Liberty Stun No. 1 Lynx 91-75: Game 1 Full Recap

LIBERTY 91, LYNX 75. STEWART GOES FOR 34, FIEBICH HITS FOUR THREES AND NEW YORK STEALS HOME COURT.

The No. 8 seed isn't supposed to win at the No. 1 seed's building. The New York Liberty did it anyway. Behind 34 points and 12 rebounds from Breanna Stewart, the Liberty beat the Minnesota Lynx 91-75 on Sunday afternoon at Target Center to take a 1-0 lead in their best-of-three first-round series.

We already broke down how the Ionescu-to-Stewart connection won this game in our earlier story. This is the full picture: the shooting, the fouls and the supporting cast that turned a close game into a 16-point road win.

How it happened

Minnesota played well in the first half. The Lynx won the second quarter 26-15 and led 44-40 at the break. Nia Brodie gave them a big lift off the bench with 16 points in just 19 minutes.

The third quarter flipped it. New York tied it at 44 in the first minute and went back and forth with Minnesota until Ionescu's step-back three put the Liberty ahead to stay at 49-46. Then Leonie Fiebich started firing. Her threes at 5:17 and 4:08 pushed the lead to 57-50. Kayla McBride kept Minnesota close with two late threes that cut it to 63-60, but Stewart made two free throws with 7 seconds left and the Liberty led 65-60 after three.

The fourth quarter was all New York, 26-15. Fiebich opened it with a jumper, Han Xu hit a three for a 72-62 lead, and Fiebich buried another three for 75-64. A late 8-0 burst from Stewart and Sabrina Ionescu made it 87-68, and that was the ballgame.

The difference

  • Threes: The Liberty made 13 of 25 from deep. The Lynx made 9 of 26.
  • Free throws: Stewart alone went 10-for-12. "We can't give her 12 free throws," Lynx coach Cheryl Reeve said, per the AP.
  • Foul trouble: Napheesa Collier and Natasha Howard each finished with five fouls, and Howard played just 18 minutes.
  • Rebounds: New York won the glass 38-23.

Top performers

  • Breanna Stewart, Liberty: 34 points, 12 rebounds, 6 assists, 2 steals, 2 blocks, 11-18 FG.
  • Leonie Fiebich, Liberty: 16 points, 6-7 FG, 4-5 from three, 8 rebounds.
  • Sabrina Ionescu, Liberty: 13 points, 6 assists.
  • Pauline Astier, Liberty: 7 points, 8 rebounds, 5 assists.
  • Jonquel Jones, Liberty: 8 points and 5 rebounds while playing through an ankle injury, per the AP.
  • Nia Brodie, Lynx: 16 points on 6-7 shooting, 4-5 from three.
  • Kayla McBride, Lynx: 14 points, 6 assists.
  • Napheesa Collier, Lynx: 12 points on 6-14 shooting.
  • Olivia Miles, Lynx: 11 points and 8 rebounds on 3-12 shooting in her first playoff game.

What's next

New York leads 1-0. Game 2 is Tuesday, September 29 at 8:30 p.m. ET at Barclays Center in Brooklyn on USA Network and CNBC. A Liberty win ends the series. A Lynx win sends it back to Minneapolis for Game 3 on Thursday.

Stewart isn't taking anything for granted. The Liberty lost in the first round last year, and she had a short message for her teammates after this one: "This doesn't mean relax."
