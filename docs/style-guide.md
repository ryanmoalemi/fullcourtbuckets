# Full Court Buckets style guide

## List articles

A numbered list reads straight through. The reader sees item 1 right after the title and the lead image, scrolls through the list, and that is the body.

Use this order:

1. Title (H1)
2. Byline
3. Lead image
4. At most one or two short intro sentences
5. Items 1 through 10. Each item is the heading, then the card image, then one price line, then that item's text.
6. After the last item, only a short bottom line. The research note, the how-made note, and image credits follow as small fine print.

Do not put a ranking table, a checklist, or a sales grid in the body of a list article.
