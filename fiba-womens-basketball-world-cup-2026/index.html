This Is the Olympics of the WNBA

The WNBA season stops after August 30 and does not resume until September 17 as many of the league’s top players head to Berlin for the 2026 FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup.

Team USA’s roster has 58 combined WNBA All-Star selections, 16 WNBA championships and seven Rookie of the Year awards. The roster includes Caitlin Clark, Angel Reese, Paige Bueckers, A’ja Wilson, Breanna Stewart, Napheesa Collier, Kelsey Plum, Rhyne Howard, Chelsea Gray, Jackie Young, Aliyah Boston and Kahleah Copper.

Clark and Reese Will Play Together for the First Time

Caitlin Clark and Angel Reese will be teammates for the first time. Clark can hit Reese running the floor before the defense gets set, find her on rolls to the basket and draw help defenders away from the paint. Reese gives Clark an elite offensive rebounder who can recover missed shots and generate second-chance possessions.

Clark creates space with her shooting range and passing. Reese attacks that space and the offensive glass.

Kara Lawson Will Coach Team USA

Kara Lawson will coach Team USA. She is the head coach at Duke and won an Olympic gold medal as a player in 2008. Her assistants are Golden State Valkyries head coach Natalie Nakase, Phoenix Mercury head coach Nate Tibbetts and Indiana Fever head coach Stephanie White.

Team USA Opens Against China

Team USA opens group play against China on September 4, then plays Italy on September 6 and Czechia on September 7. Those three games make up the group stage before the tournament moves into elimination rounds.

The World Cup champion earns automatic qualification for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. The United States is already qualified because it is the host nation.

Team USA Is Chasing a Fifth Straight World Cup

The United States is trying to become the first country to win five consecutive Women’s World Cups. Team USA has won the last four, has not lost a World Cup game since 2006 and enters Berlin on a 30-game World Cup winning streak.

Like Steve Kerr said in Paris about the men’s Team USA, expectations are so high that anything short of gold is a disappointment. For the women at the 2026 World Cup, the standard is the same. Silver will be a disappointment.

How to Watch

All 36 World Cup games will stream live on HBO Max. Team USA’s group games against China, Italy and Czechia will also air on TNT, with the China and Czechia games also on truTV.
