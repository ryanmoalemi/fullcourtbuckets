The Complete Homeowner's Guide To Building An ADU In San Diego

Building an accessory dwelling unit in San Diego requires decisions across planning, design, permit approvals, utility coordination, and construction sequencing. This guide is designed to help homeowners avoid the most common cost and timeline mistakes before they begin formal submittals.

How To Use This Guide

  • Start with feasibility and site-fit to confirm whether your lot supports your ADU goals.
  • Use permit sequencing to map the order of design, engineering, and city review steps.
  • Apply budget guardrails early so scope decisions match realistic construction pricing.
  • Complete the owner readiness checklist before moving to full design and permit work.

Top ADU Planning Priorities For San Diego Homeowners

  • Site constraints and utility routes
  • Permit package completeness
  • Budget ranges including sitework and contingencies
  • Design choices that impact construction schedule and cost
  • Execution timeline from concept to permit-ready plans
