Player FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Diana Taurasi married?

Yes. Taurasi married former Mercury teammate Penny Taylor. They have children together.

How old is Diana Taurasi?

She was born June 11, 1982 in Glendale, California, U.S. As of late 2026 that puts her at 44 years old.

What is Diana Taurasi's nationality?

Diana Taurasi is described as American former in major biographical profiles.

Did Diana Taurasi retire?

Taurasi's long Mercury career concluded around the mid-2020s; she is widely described as a former player and all-time great rather than a current roster staple.

Where is Diana Taurasi from?

She is from Glendale, California, U.S., and played college basketball at UConn.

How many championships does Diana Taurasi have?

Taurasi won three WNBA titles with Phoenix (2007, 2009, 2014) and is the league's all-time leading scorer, with a record haul of Olympic gold medals for Team USA.

Who is Diana Taurasi?

Diana Lorena Taurasi is an American former professional basketball player.

Who is Diana Taurasi dating?

Taylor at one time was the Phoenix Mercury Director of Player Development and Performance, After eight years of dating, Taurasi wed Taylor on May 13, 2017.

What team does Diana Taurasi play for?

Her most recent WNBA team on record is the Phoenix Mercury.
