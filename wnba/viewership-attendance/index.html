    How many fans attended WNBA games?

    The WNBA's 2026 regular season drew 3.79 million fans across 330 games, a league record.

    Are average crowds growing?

    Total attendance

    Totals use a separate panel because a bigger schedule and a bigger crowd answer different questions. Years without a sourced total are left blank.

    How much of the total comes from games versus average crowds?

    Provisional

    This is an accounting split of the change in total attendance. It is not a cause of demand. The 2025 game count is calculated, and the 2025 average used here is calculated from the rounded total.

    Extra games: 44 x (3,150,000 / 286) = 484,615 (76%). Average crowd: 330 x (11,474 - 3,150,000 / 286) = 151,805 (24%). Together that is 3,786,420 minus 3,150,000, or 636,420. 330 times 11,474 is 3,786,420. Sports Business Journal rounds the 2026 total to 3.79 million, which is a separate rounded figure.

    How this page is built

    • Viewership. Nielsen estimates reported by the league, the networks, or Sports Media Watch. Nielsen's Big Data + Panel, used more widely from 2025, may affect comparisons with earlier years. One adjustment is not applied to every figure.
    • Attendance. Figures are what the league, a team release, or Sports Business Journal's box-score count reports. They are not automatically paid attendance or unique fans.
    • Conflicts. When a printed average does not match the total divided by the game count, the cell is labeled unresolved and the printed figure stays next to its source.
    • No guessing. A blank cell is not zero. Spot an error? Email Ryan.
