    WNBA viewership and attendance stats 2026: How many people watch the WNBA?

    Last updated: Oct. 13, 2026.

    By Ryan Moalemi

    The WNBA drew a record 3.79 million fans to regular-season games in 2026, and its most-watched regular-season game since 1997 pulled 3.31 million TV and streaming viewers.

    Key WNBA stats

    • 2026 regular-season attendance hit a record 3.79 million.
    • WNBA games averaged a record 11,474 fans per game in 2026.
    • Fever at Sky on Aug. 23, 2026 drew 3.31 million viewers on NBC and Peacock, the most for a regular-season game since 1997.
    • The 2024 Finals averaged 1.6 million viewers, the most-watched Finals in 25 years.
    • The 2025 Finals averaged 1.5 million viewers over a four-game sweep.
    • The single-game attendance record is 20,711, set Sept. 19, 2024 (as of the league's 2024 release).

    How this page is built

    • Viewership: Nielsen estimates reported by the league, the networks or Sports Media Watch. Nielsen added smart-TV and set-top-box data ("Big Data + Panel") in 2025, which gives 2025 and later numbers a built-in boost over earlier years.
    • Attendance: paid attendance from the league, or Sports Business Journal's count of official box scores.
