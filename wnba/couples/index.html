How this page was built

Empty fields are skipped. A chip stays only when its source URL still resolves. Photos are Wikimedia Commons files, after the license on the file page was checked. A partner without a photo is shown with initials. Both-WNBA couples come first.

Questions people ask

Which WNBA players are married?

These marriages are confirmed on this page: Breanna Stewart and Marta Xargay Casademont, Courtney Vandersloot and Allie Quigley, Diana Taurasi and Penny Taylor, Sabrina Ionescu and Hroniss Grasu, Napheesa Collier and Alex Bazzell, Candace Parker and Anna Petrakova, Arike Ogunbowale and Lala Ronay, Katie Lou Samuelson and Devin Cannady, Betnijah Laney-Hamilton and Jordan Hamilton, and Megan Gustafson (DiLeo) and David DiLeo.

Which WNBA players are married to each other?

These married couples are both WNBA players: Breanna Stewart and Marta Xargay Casademont, Courtney Vandersloot and Allie Quigley, and Diana Taurasi and Penny Taylor.

Are Paige Bueckers and Azzi Fudd dating?

Yes. Paige Bueckers and Azzi Fudd are dating. They went public in July 2025, when Bueckers named Fudd as her girlfriend in a WAG Talk interview. Both spoke about the relationship in the 2026 docuseries The Dynasty: UConn Huskies.

Is A'ja Wilson dating Bam Adebayo?

Yes. A'ja Wilson and Bam Adebayo are dating. They met at the 2021 Tokyo Olympics. They have been together since 2021, and Wilson confirmed it publicly in February 2025.

Which WNBA players are married to NBA players?

Betnijah Laney-Hamilton is married to Jordan Hamilton, a former NBA player, now in Japan's B.League. A'ja Wilson is dating Bam Adebayo, a Miami Heat center (NBA). That relationship is not listed as a marriage.

Who is Caitlin Clark dating?

Caitlin Clark is dating Connor McCaffery, a former Iowa basketball player and former Butler assistant coach. They met as Iowa Hawkeyes. They have been together since April 2023.

Is Sabrina Ionescu married?

Yes. Sabrina Ionescu is married to Hroniss Grasu, an NFL center. They went public in 2021. They got engaged on January 20, 2023. They married on March 10, 2024, in California.
