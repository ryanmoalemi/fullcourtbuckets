Player FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How tall is A'ja Wilson?

A'ja Wilson is listed at 6 feet 4 inches (1.93 m) by the WNBA. That length helps her finish inside and contest shots as the Las Vegas Aces' starting center.

How old is A'ja Wilson?

She was born August 8, 1996, in Columbia, South Carolina. As of late 2026 that puts her at 30 years old.

How much does A'ja Wilson make?

In April 2026 she signed a three-year package with the Las Vegas Aces reported as the richest deal in WNBA history, about $5 million guaranteed, starting near $1.4 million for the 2026 season. Endorsements such as her Nike agreement are separate from that team salary.

What is A'ja Wilson's contract?

Wilson's current Aces contract is a three-year, fully guaranteed supermax-style deal signed in April 2026. Public reporting puts the total near $5 million, with 2026 base pay around $1.4 million and raises in later years if the salary cap rises as allowed.

Is A'ja Wilson married?

There is no public record of her being married. She has openly confirmed a romantic relationship with NBA center Bam Adebayo and has described him as her partner in interviews, without announcing a wedding.

Who is A'ja Wilson dating?

Wilson is dating Bam Adebayo of the Miami Heat. Rumors built around the 2024 Olympics, and she later confirmed the relationship in 2025 media interviews while the two have continued to support each other publicly at games.

How many MVPs does A'ja Wilson have?

She has won the WNBA Most Valuable Player award four times: 2020, 2022, 2024, and 2025, the first player to reach four league MVPs.

How many championships does A'ja Wilson have?

Wilson has three WNBA titles with the Las Vegas Aces, won in 2022, 2023, and 2025. She was also named Finals MVP in 2023 and 2025.

What awards has A'ja Wilson won?

Beyond four MVPs and three championships, her résumé includes Rookie of the Year (2018), multiple Defensive Player of the Year honors (2022, 2023, and co-DPOY in 2025), two Finals MVPs, and repeated All-Star and All-WNBA First Team selections.

Does A'ja Wilson have a signature shoe?

Yes. Nike released her first signature sneaker, the A'One, in May 2025 (retail around $110), along with matching apparel. She is a longtime Nike athlete with a multi-year signature deal.

What are A'ja Wilson's stats / points per game?

In the 2026 regular season, A'ja Wilson averaged 26.2 points, 9.4 rebounds, and 3.2 assists in 41 games for the Las Vegas Aces. Her full season-by-season numbers are on this page.

What team does A'ja Wilson play for?

She plays for the Las Vegas Aces and wears No. 22 at center. The Aces drafted her No. 1 overall in 2018, and she has remained with the franchise since.

Who is A'ja Wilson?

A'ja Wilson is a South Carolina product and the face of the Las Vegas Aces, a four-time WNBA MVP and three-time champion who anchors the paint on both ends.

Where is A'ja Wilson from?

She is from Columbia, South Carolina, and starred at the University of South Carolina before entering the WNBA.
