    2026 season

    Season summary

    After the 2026 regular season, the Dallas Wings are 27-17, 4th in the Western Conference, and 6 games back. Those results put the Dallas Wings at the 7th playoff seed. The Dallas Wings were home 16-6 and road 11-11, a last-10 of 7-3, and a streak of W1. For the 2026 regular season, the Dallas Wings leaders are Paige Bueckers in scoring at 20.9 points per game, Jessica Shepard in rebounds at 11.1 rebounds per game, and Paige Bueckers in assists at 5.9 assists per game. Paige Bueckers was the minutes leader for the Dallas Wings at 32.8 per game. The Dallas Wings lost Game 1 to the Valkyries 104-80, won Game 2 108-100 in overtime, and lost Game 3 77-73. Golden State took the series 2-1. Related playoff stories: Game 1, Game 2, Game 3.
