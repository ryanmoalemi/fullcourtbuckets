    2026 season

    Season summary

    As the 2026 regular season ended, the Atlanta Dream are 30-14, 1st in the Eastern Conference, and 3 games back. That record was the 4th playoff seed for the Atlanta Dream. Splits for the Atlanta Dream were home 15-7 and road 15-7, a last-10 of 9-1, and a streak of W5. On the current Atlanta Dream roster, the 2026 regular-season leaders are Allisha Gray in scoring at 19.0 points per game, Angel Reese in rebounds at 12.1 rebounds per game, and Jordin Canada in assists at 7.3 assists per game. Rhyne Howard played the most minutes on the Atlanta Dream at 34.2 per game. The Atlanta Dream beat the Mystics, then swept the Liberty 3-0 in the semifinals. The Dream lost Game 5 64-55 on Oct. 26. They now face a 2-3 deficit. Next: Game 6, Oct. 29. Playoff and series coverage: Mystics Game 1, Liberty Game 1, Liberty Game 2, Liberty Game 3.
