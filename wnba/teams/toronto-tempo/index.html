    Season summary

    The Toronto Tempo are 11-33, 6th in the Eastern Conference, and 22 games back in its first WNBA season. Eight playoff seeds were assigned, and the Toronto Tempo were not among them. Venue by venue, the Toronto Tempo had home 7-15 and road 4-18, a last-10 of 1-9, and a streak of L8. The 2026 season for the Toronto Tempo was led by Marina Mabrey in scoring at 20.8 points per game, Aneesah Morrow in rebounds at 8.3 rebounds per game, and Julie Allemand in assists at 5.5 assists per game. The busiest minutes average on the Toronto Tempo belonged to Marina Mabrey at 29.5 per game. An expansion club, the Toronto Tempo played its first games in May 2026, went 11-33, and missed the playoffs, per the expansion story. How the series has gone: expansion story.
