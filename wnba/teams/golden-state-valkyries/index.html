    Season summary

    At the end of the 2026 regular season, the Golden State Valkyries are 32-12, 2nd in the Western Conference, and 1 game back. The Golden State Valkyries were the 2nd seed when the playoffs began. Beside the record, the Golden State Valkyries had home 17-5 and road 15-7, a last-10 of 7-3, and a streak of W1. The Golden State Valkyries were led in the 2026 regular season by Gabby Williams in scoring at 14.4 points per game, Kiah Stokes in rebounds at 5.8 rebounds per game, and Veronica Burton in assists at 5.7 assists per game. Most minutes on the Golden State Valkyries went to Veronica Burton, at 28.6 per game. The Golden State Valkyries opened the first round with a 104-80 win, dropped Game 2 108-100 in overtime, and closed the Wings out 77-73 to win the series 2-1. In the semifinals they beat the Aces 71-60 and 83-81 and lead 2-0 heading into Game 3.
