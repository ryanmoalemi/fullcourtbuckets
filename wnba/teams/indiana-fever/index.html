    Season summary

    The Indiana Fever are 28-16, 3rd in the Eastern Conference, and 5 games back for the 2026 season. Playoff seeding placed the Indiana Fever 6th. For the Indiana Fever, the splits were home 15-7 and road 13-9, a last-10 of 6-4, and a streak of L1. Leaders for the Indiana Fever across the 2026 regular season are Kelsey Mitchell in scoring at 24.7 points per game, Aliyah Boston in rebounds at 8.0 rebounds per game, and Caitlin Clark in assists at 8.3 assists per game. Kelsey Mitchell carried the heaviest minutes for the Indiana Fever at 32.5 per game. The Indiana Fever lost Game 1 to the Aces 102-85, won Game 2 99-89, and lost Game 3 94-83.
