    Season summary

    The Washington Mystics are 28-16, 2nd in the Eastern Conference, and 5 games back with the regular season complete. The Washington Mystics held the 5th seed in the playoff field. The finer marks for the Washington Mystics were home 15-7 and road 13-9, a last-10 of 8-2, and a streak of W4. The 2026 Washington Mystics regular-season leaders were Shakira Austin in scoring at 17.0 points per game, Kiki Iriafen in rebounds at 9.6 rebounds per game, and Sonia Citron in assists at 4.4 assists per game. Sonia Citron had the highest minutes average on the Washington Mystics at 33.0 per game. The Washington Mystics lost Game 1 to the Dream, 92-77. The playoff path: Game 1 recap.
