    Season summary

    By October 1, 2026, the Las Vegas Aces are 31-13, 3rd in the Western Conference, and 2 games back. The Las Vegas Aces took the 3rd seed into the postseason. The rest of the Las Vegas Aces record shows home 15-7 and road 16-6, a last-10 of 8-2, and a streak of W7. In the 2026 regular season the Las Vegas Aces leaders are A'ja Wilson in scoring at 26.2 points per game, A'ja Wilson in rebounds at 9.4 rebounds per game, and Jackie Young in assists at 6.8 assists per game. Chelsea Gray averaged the most minutes for the Las Vegas Aces, at 32.7 a game. The Las Vegas Aces beat the Fever 102-85, lost Game 2 99-89, and closed the series 94-83 for a 2-1 win. In the semifinals the Valkyries beat Las Vegas 71-60 and 83-81, so the Aces trail 0-2 going into Game 3.
