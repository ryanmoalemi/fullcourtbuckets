    Career

    Career summary

    Crystal Dangerfield averaged 16.2 points and 3.6 assists over 21 games with the Minnesota Lynx in 2020.

    She played for the Minnesota Lynx from 2020 through 2021 after going 16th overall out of UConn in the 2020 draft.

    From there, she played for the Indiana Fever in 2022, the New York Liberty in 2022, the Dallas Wings in 2023, the Atlanta Dream in 2024, and the Los Angeles Sparks in 2024.
