    How the drawing works

    The league puts 14 balls, numbered 1 through 14, in the machine and draws four. That is 1,001 combinations. One combination is left unassigned, so 1,000 are in play. The slot that owns the combination gets pick 1. The four balls go back in, and the same draw sets pick 2. If the machine hits the unassigned combination, or a combination owned by the slot that already won, it draws again.

    Picks 3 through 7 are not drawn. They follow the pre-lottery order among the five slots still waiting. That order, worst record first, is Houston, Toronto, Washington (Chicago's pick), Seattle, Portland, Los Angeles (own pick), Los Angeles (Phoenix's pick).

    Five of the records are two seasons added together, 2025 and 2026: Chicago 26-62, Houston 22-66, Los Angeles 37-51, Phoenix 43-45, and Seattle 31-57. Portland and Toronto were expansion teams in 2026, so their odds use 2026 only: Portland 17-27 and Toronto 11-33. Houston and Toronto both land at .250, so each receives 329 combinations.

    The tie

    The Oct. 28, 2026 league release uses the 2026 head-to-head as the first tiebreaker. Houston, then the Connecticut Sun, went 1-2 against Toronto. The three games are in the site schedule file, which was taken from the WNBA schedule: Toronto 106, Connecticut 102 on June 10; Toronto 101, Connecticut 97 on June 19; Connecticut 95, Toronto 81 on Sept. 24. Houston therefore sits ahead of Toronto. Houston's latest possible pick is No. 3. Toronto's latest possible pick is No. 4. Those floors also follow the drawing rules in that release: Houston cannot land past No. 3, and Toronto cannot land past No. 4.

    The traded picks

    Washington holds Chicago's pick. The Oct. 28 footnote says Chicago traded its own 2027 first-round pick to Washington for Ariel Atkins on Feb. 23, 2025. The team releases that day describe the first-round piece as the right to swap Washington's own 2027 first with Chicago's own 2027 first, plus Chicago's 2027 second-round pick. Those are the Mystics release and the league release on the Sky deal. This page follows the Oct. 28 lottery assignment for the combinations. It does not assign Washington's own first-rounder.

    Los Angeles holds Phoenix's pick. The league's trade-deadline note says Los Angeles sent Kelsey Plum to Phoenix for Monique Akoa Makani, a 2027 first-round pick, and a 2028 second-round pick. The lottery footnote dates that deal Aug. 2, 2026.

    Houston is the relocated Connecticut Sun. The May 13, 2026 sale release says the club stays in Connecticut for 2026 and moves to Houston for 2027, under Tilman J. Fertitta. The team URL on this site is still /wnba/teams/connecticut-sun/.

    Questions

    When is the 2027 WNBA Draft Lottery?

    Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026, from 7:30 to 8:00 p.m. ET (4:30 to 5:00 p.m. PT). It is the 26th annual WNBA Draft Lottery, presented by State Street Investment Management SPY.

    When is the 2027 WNBA Draft?

    Monday, April 12, 2027. The lottery sets picks 1 and 2 only. The expansion draft for Portland and Toronto was April 3, 2026. That draft already happened.

    How does the WNBA draft lottery work?

    Fourteen balls numbered 1 through 14 go into the machine, and four are drawn. There are 1,001 four-ball combinations, and 1,000 of them are assigned. The slot that holds the combination gets the first pick. The balls go back in, and the draw is repeated for the second pick. An unassigned combination, or one that belongs to the slot that already won, is redrawn. The five slots that were not drawn then take picks 3 through 7 in pre-lottery order.

    Who has the best odds?

    Houston and Toronto. Each has 329 of 1,000 combinations, a 32.9% chance at the first pick and a 61.7% chance at a top-two pick. Los Angeles, counting both of its picks, has a 3.6% chance at No. 1.

    What does the Houston and Toronto tie mean?

    Both clubs are at .250. Houston is 22-66 across 2025 and 2026. Toronto, an expansion team, is 11-33 in 2026 only. The first tiebreaker is the 2026 head-to-head. The Sun went 1-2 against the Tempo: Toronto won 106-102 on June 10 and 101-97 on June 19, and Connecticut won 95-81 on Sept. 24. Houston is first in the pre-lottery order, so the latest pick Houston can land is No. 3. Toronto is second, so the latest pick Toronto can land is No. 4.

    Where can I watch the lottery?

    ESPN in the United States, after UConn vs. Louisville, and TSN in Canada. The Oct. 28 release does not name a separate streaming service. Bethany Donaphin, head of league operations, reveals the results. Ernst & Young oversees the drawing.

    Will JuJu Watkins be the No. 1 pick?

    She is the consensus No. 1 on the Ultimate Big Board snapshot of Oct. 29, 2026: 8 of 8 mocks, weighted average 1.0. That is a projection, not the draft. She is on the 2026-27 USC roster. Sports-Reference had one game posted by Nov. 9, 2026. She can stay in school. This page does not say she will be the first pick.
