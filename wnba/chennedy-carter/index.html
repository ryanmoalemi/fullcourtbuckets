    Career

    Career summary

    Chennedy Carter was drafted fourth overall out of Texas A&M and played for the Atlanta Dream from 2020 through 2021.

    Her strongest WNBA season with the Chicago Sky in 2024 was 17.5 points, 3.5 rebounds, and 3.1 assists over 33 games.

    Her WNBA career also included the Los Angeles Sparks in 2022 and the Las Vegas Aces in 2026.
