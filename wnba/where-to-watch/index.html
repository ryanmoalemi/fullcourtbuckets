    How we build this list

    • A venue is listed only when an official team watch-party page, the venue's own WNBA page, a named local news report, or a written reply to our staff shows WNBA screenings.
    • "Confirmed with staff" means the venue replied to us in writing on that date. We keep those replies on file.
    • Reddit, aggregators and third-party directories are not used as evidence.
    • Venues that are closed, not yet open, or have no current address stay off the page until checked.
    • Directions links are built from the street address. No rankings, ratings, tiers or badges. Listing here is not an endorsement or an official team or league partnership.

    FAQ

    Do these bars show every WNBA game?

    Not always. Contact the venue to check your game.

    What does "Confirmed with staff" mean?

    The venue told us in writing, on the date listed, that it shows WNBA games.

    My bar shows WNBA games. How do I get listed?

    Email ryan@fullcourtbuckets.com with your bar name, address and a link to your WNBA listing or event page.

    A listing is wrong or a bar closed. What should I do?

    Email ryan@fullcourtbuckets.com and we will recheck it.
