Ionescu to Stewart Sinks the No. 1 Lynx in Game 1

LIBERTY 91, LYNX 75. FIVE ASSISTS, 11 POINTS AND A 1-0 SERIES LEAD.

The Minnesota Lynx were the No. 1 seed for a reason. On Sunday at Target Center, the No. 8 New York Liberty beat them anyway, 91-75, to take Game 1 of their first-round series. The biggest reason was a connection Liberty fans know by heart: Sabrina Ionescu finding Breanna Stewart.

Ionescu assisted Stewart five times. Those five buckets were worth 11 points, and almost every one landed at a moment when the game was up for grabs. Stewart finished with 34 points, 12 rebounds and 6 assists on 11-of-18 shooting. Ionescu added 13 points, 6 assists and 2 steals. Together they scored 47 of New York's 91 points.

How the connection took over

The first one came right before halftime. With 43.6 seconds left in the second quarter and New York down 38-36, Ionescu found Stewart for a 5-foot turnaround jumper to tie it at 38. Stewart was fouled on the play but missed the and-one free throw, and Minnesota still took a 44-40 lead into the break.

Then the third quarter started and the game flipped. The Liberty opened the half on an 11-2 run, and Ionescu and Stewart had a hand in all 11 points:

  • 9:44: Stewart set up Pauline Astier for a driving layup.
  • 9:09: Ionescu hit Stewart for a 4-foot layup to tie it 44-44.
  • 8:44: Stewart made both free throws for a 46-44 lead.
  • 7:27: Ionescu drilled a 27-foot step-back three.
  • 6:38: Ionescu found Stewart on a driving layup for a 51-46 lead, and the Lynx burned a timeout.

Minnesota didn't fold. Kayla McBride scored 10 points in the last four minutes of the third quarter and cut it to 63-60. Stewart answered by making two free throws with 7 seconds left, and New York took a 65-60 lead into the fourth.

The fourth quarter belonged to the Liberty, 26-15. With New York up 79-68, the duo ended any comeback talk in about 80 seconds. At 4:07, Ionescu kicked it to Stewart for a 23-foot three. At 3:36, she found her again for an 8-foot floater. At 2:49, Ionescu hit a 26-foot three herself. That was an 8-0 burst, all Ionescu and Stewart, and it pushed the lead to 87-68, New York's biggest of the day.

The numbers

  • Final: Liberty 91, Lynx 75. New York leads the best-of-three series 1-0.
  • Ionescu to Stewart: 5 assists, 11 points (1 in the second quarter, 2 in the third, 2 in the fourth).
  • Breanna Stewart: 34 points, 11-18 FG, 2-3 from three, 10-12 FT, 12 rebounds, 6 assists, 2 steals, 2 blocks in 38 minutes.
  • Sabrina Ionescu: 13 points, 4-14 FG, 3-8 from three, 6 assists, 2 steals in 28 minutes.
  • The duo: 47 of the Liberty's 91 points.
  • Leonie Fiebich: 16 points on 6-7 shooting, 4-5 from three. Stewart assisted four of her buckets.
  • From three: Liberty 13-25, Lynx 9-26.
  • Lynx: Nia Brodie 16 (6-7 FG), McBride 14, Napheesa Collier 12 (6-14 FG), Olivia Miles 11.

Why this one matters

This is a rematch of the 2024 WNBA Finals, when the Liberty beat the Lynx 3-2 to win the franchise's first title. New York also won the season series this year, 2-1. So the 8 vs. 1 matchup never looked like a normal 8 vs. 1.

The bigger question coming in was Stewart's health. She was listed day-to-day with a sore knee heading into the playoffs. She played 38 minutes, went 10-for-12 at the line and grabbed 11 defensive rebounds. If the knee was bothering her, it didn't show.

Ionescu's shot has come and gone from deep all season, and Sunday wasn't a clean shooting day either at 4-for-14. But she made the threes that mattered, the step-back during the third-quarter run and the 26-footer that made it 87-68, and she kept feeding Stewart when the Lynx needed stops. On a day her jumper was streaky, her passing won the game.

What it means for Game 2

Game 2 is Tuesday, September 29 at 8:30 p.m. ET at Barclays Center. A Liberty win ends the series. A Lynx win sends it back to Minneapolis for Game 3.

Minnesota has a real problem to solve. If the Lynx send help at Stewart, she has shown she will make the right pass, and Fiebich made them pay from three. If they stay home, Ionescu keeps finding Stewart in the spots she likes. The Lynx also need more from Collier, who shot 6-for-14 and finished with five fouls. Minnesota earned the No. 1 seed, so expect adjustments. The question is whether any of them can split up Ionescu and Stewart.
