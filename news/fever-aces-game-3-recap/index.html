Wilson Scores 36, Aces Close Out the Fever 94-83

By Ryan Moalemi

Aces 94, Fever 83. Wilson goes 11-of-18 and Las Vegas takes the series 2-1.

The Fever had it within two. With 3:00 left at Michelob ULTRA Arena, Kelsey Mitchell stepped back from 25 feet and made it 82-80. Then Las Vegas lived at the line. Chelsea Gray made two free throws and a pull-up, and A'ja Wilson, Gray, and Jackie Young scored 12 of the last 15 points, 10 of them from the stripe. The final was 94-83. The best-of-three is over. The Aces win it 2-1.

The first quarter was a tie that opened as a Fever burst. Mitchell's 26-foot three started the scoring. Caitlin Clark hit a 24-footer and a step-back, Mitchell added a jumper, and it was 10-2. That eight-point margin was Indiana's largest lead. Wilson's three cut it to 22-20, the running layup tied it at 22, and the and-one free throw put the Aces ahead 23-22. Clark's 25-foot running jumper made it 29-25 Fever. Sophie Cunningham's 13-footer with 1.5 seconds left, right after Gray's driving layup, knotted it at 31.

The half turned late in the second. The game was tied at 40, then tied again at 42, and Wilson scored the next 6. Young's 25-foot three made it 51-43 Aces. Jewell Loyd's 23-foot three with 11.4 seconds left made it 54-46 before Clark's two free throws. Halftime was 54-48, Aces.

The third was Las Vegas's quiet quarter and Indiana's best chance. Wilson's pull-up early made it 58-48 Aces. Gray's 25-foot three, off a Young assist with 4:32 left, made it 67-55. That was the Aces' largest lead, 12. Indiana scored 10 of the next 13. Aliyah Boston hit a 22-foot three, Myisha Hines-Allen scored inside and at the line, and Clark finished a running layup plus a free throw. The Aces scored 16 in the quarter and led 70-65 after three.

Cunningham's 27-foot three at 8:50 of the fourth, assisted by Lexie Hull, cut it to 70-68. Young scored at the rim, Wilson scored inside, Gray converted an and-one, and free throws pushed the lead to 80-70. Boston's 26-foot three cut it to 80-73. Gray's step-back made it 82-73. Mitchell scored the next 7 Indiana points herself: two free throws, a jumper, and the 25-foot step-back that made it 82-80. The Aces closed on a 12-3 run. Gray's pull-up was the only field goal in those 12 points. Clark's 7-foot basket and one Mitchell free throw were the rest of Indiana's night. Attendance was 10,388.

Indiana won the paint 36-22 and the glass 38-34, with 9 offensive rebounds to 2. In Game 2 the paint was 58-26 Fever, and Indiana shot 37-of-71 (52 percent) with 8-of-21 from three (38 percent). Thursday the Fever shot 30-of-78 (38 percent) and 9-of-32 from three (28 percent). The Aces shot 29-of-64 (45 percent) and 8-of-20 from three (40 percent). The line separated them: Las Vegas 28-of-35 (80 percent), Indiana 14-of-20 (70 percent), after Indiana was called for 27 fouls and Las Vegas 19. Turnovers were 10 apiece, and each side scored 14 points off them. The Aces led for 71 percent of the game, with seven lead changes.

How this series swung

  • A'ja Wilson, Aces: 36 points, 10 rebounds, 2 blocks, 11-of-18, 2-of-3 from three, 12-of-15 at the line, 37 minutes. In Game 1 she had 38 on 15-of-19 with 16 rebounds. In Game 2 she had 22 on 5-of-11.
  • Chelsea Gray, Aces: 23 points, 5 assists, 4 rebounds, 8-of-15, 6-of-6 at the line, all 40 minutes.
  • Jackie Young, Aces: 18 points, 8 rebounds, 7 assists, all 40 minutes. She had 31 in Game 2.
  • Jewell Loyd, Aces: 11 points, 3-of-10, 3-of-6 from three.
  • Kelsey Mitchell, Fever: 23 points, 7-of-23, 3-of-11 from three, plus the 7-point push that made it 82-80. She took 23 shots after 16 in Game 2, when she scored 26.
  • Aliyah Boston, Fever: 19 points, 10 rebounds, 8-of-16, 2-of-4 from three.
  • Caitlin Clark, Fever: 18 points, 4 assists, 6 turnovers, 2 steals, 4 blocks, 6-of-18, 3-of-12 from three, 37 minutes. The 4 blocks were her first of the series. She had none in Game 1 or Game 2. On Tuesday she had 27 points and 15 assists on 5-of-11 from three.
  • Myisha Hines-Allen, Fever: 12 points and 12 rebounds off the bench, 5-of-9 in 20 minutes.

There is no Game 4. The first round is a best-of-three, and the series is complete: Las Vegas 2, Indiana 1. A Fever win was the only way to keep playing. They were within 82-80 with three minutes left. The Aces made 10 free throws over the last 2:15, and the season ended 94-83.
