    JuJu Watkins is scoring 38.9 points per 40 minutes in her return

    By Ryan MoalemiNovember 11, 2026

    No. 3 USC is 3-0. JuJu Watkins is averaging 24.3 points in 25.0 minutes, beside 23.9 points in 33.9 minutes in 2024-25. Per 40 minutes that is 38.9, against 29.8 for 2023-24 and 2024-25 combined.

    The sheet's 75 minutes are the box minutes added up: 25, then 27, then 23. The 73 points are 21, 32, and 20. Her field-goal line on the same sheet is 26-for-54.

    Nov. 2 was at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas. The box lists 2,031 in attendance and a 100-68 final, quarters 29-23-31-17 for USC and 15-24-10-19 for UNLV. The UNLV recap headline also says 100-68. One sentence in that recap says 100-66, and the same sentence says 21 points in 21 minutes. The box minute column is 25.     Nov. 8 was at Galen Center. The box lists 7,807 in attendance and a 118-40 final. The Utah State recap says the 118 tied for the third-highest total in program history, matching Missouri on Dec. 27, 1985, and that the 78-point margin ranks second, behind the 124-39 win over CSUN on Nov. 12, 2024. The same recap says she had a game-best plus-minus of +61. The box page does not print a plus-minus column. It does print the 4-12 rebound split, the 10-for-19, the 4-for-7 from three, and the 8-for-9 at the line, in 27 minutes.

    Nov. 10 was at Galen Center. The box lists 3,870 in attendance and a 105-44 final, with Watkins in 23 minutes. The Charleston recap says 22 minutes. The box is the row in the table. That recap says USC has scored 100 or more in three straight games for the second time in program history, matching a stretch in December 1980, and that this is the first time the Women of Troy have done it to open a season. The three boxes are 100, 118, and 105.

    The week of awards, through two games

    The national awards were announced before Charleston. USC's AP release on Nov. 10 says the Associated Press named her national player of the week, her sixth career selection, and that USC still had Charleston that night. The AP's own note is the award. USC's USBWA release the same day says the U.S. Basketball Writers Association gave her an eighth Ann Meyers Drysdale National Player of the Week honor, and it prints a two-game average of 26.5 points, 10.5 rebounds, 4.0 assists, 3.5 steals, and 1.0 block. That is 21 and 32 points, 5 and 16 rebounds. Charleston is not in it. After Charleston, the cumulative sheet is 24.3 points a game.

    USC's AP release also says the week included an eighth Big Ten Player of the Week award. The Big Ten's Nov. 10 note says Watkins and Michigan's Te'Yala Delfosse were the conference's co-players of the week.

    The injury, in USC's words

    On March 24, 2025, USC beat Mississippi State 96-59 in the NCAA second round. The game recap says Watkins sustained a season-ending injury in the first quarter, went to the floor, and did not return. The March 26 release says she would undergo surgery and miss the rest of that NCAA tournament. Neither release names the injury.

    On Sept. 28, 2025, USC published her statement that she would sit out 2025-26. The sentence she is quoted as saying, in that release: "Because you've been with me every step of the way, I wanted you to hear it from me directly that, following the advice of my doctors and trainers, I will sit out this season and fully focus on continuing to recover so I can come back to the game I love."

    The roster bio lists her as a senior guard and says she redshirted 2025-26 due to injury. The same bio gives her birthday as July 15, 2005, and describes her as a senior at Sierra Canyon who was co-MVP of the 2023 McDonald's All-American Game.

    Sunday, the Gamecocks, and the draft

    The Charleston recap says USC plays No. 1 South Carolina on Sunday, Nov. 15, at Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, at 1 p.m. ET and 10 a.m. PT, on ABC. USC's schedule lists the same day, 10:00 a.m., ABC, that arena, and radio on USCTrojans.com/Listen and the USC Gameday App. Neither listing names a streaming service.

    The same evening, the WNBA lottery FAQ says the 2027 WNBA Draft Lottery is on ESPN at 7:30 p.m. ET. The Oct. 28 league release says the special runs 7:30 to 8:00 p.m. ET and that the draft is Monday, April 12, 2027. Full Court Buckets' team odds are on the 2027 draft lottery page.

    On age, two WNBA pages do not use the same test. The league FAQ says domestic entrants have to be at least 22 during the draft year, and have no remaining college eligibility or renounce it. The 2026 draft FAQ says collegiate players turning 22 in 2025 could renounce remaining eligibility for the 2026 draft, and that seniors who had exhausted eligibility did not have to renounce. Watkins turns 22 on July 15, 2027, which is after April 12, 2027. This page does not treat either FAQ sentence as a ruling on the 2027 draft. It also did not find a statement from her, on the USC releases it read, about entering that draft.

    Another college number from the same week is Sarah Strong's 39 for UConn.
