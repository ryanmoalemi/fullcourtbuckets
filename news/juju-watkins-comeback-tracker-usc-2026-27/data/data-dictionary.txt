Files
- juju-watkins-season-rates.csv and the seasons array in juju-watkins-comeback-tracker.json: one row per season on USC's official cumulative sheet, plus one combined row for 2023-24 and 2024-25.
- juju-watkins-2026-27-games.csv: one row per Watkins game in 2026-27.
- juju-watkins-first-three-games.csv: the first three games of 2023-24, of 2024-25, and of 2026-27. Nine rows.
- juju-watkins-comeback-tracker.json: the same rows, plus the combined pre-injury totals.

What per 40 means
pts_per_40 = points / minutes * 40, rounded half up to one decimal.
It uses the minute figure on the source, which is a whole number on these USC sheets and boxes. It is not points per game, and it is not a per-possession rate.
The single-season mpg and ppg cells are the figures printed on the cumulative sheet. The combined row is calculated on this page because the sheet does not print one: minutes and points are added, then divided by games or by minutes.

Column notes
orb and drb are the two sides of the box column labeled orb-drb. reb is the box rebound total. 4-12 on Nov. 8 is 4 offensive and 12 defensive, and 4+12=16.
attendance is the box "Attendance" line.
home_away_neutral is from the site on the box: T-Mobile Arena and Adidas Arena are neutral, Galen Center is home.
The 22nd career 30-point game and Cheryl Miller's program record of 25 are USC's count in the Nov. 8, 2026 recap. This file does not recount those games.

How to add the next game
1. Add one row to juju-watkins-2026-27-games.csv and to the games array from the official USC box.
2. Replace the 2026-27 season row with the next USC cumulative PDF, and recompute pts_per_40 from that sheet's points and minutes.
3. If the new game is still inside the first three, update the 2026-27 rows in juju-watkins-first-three-games.csv. Later games stay in the season file only.
4. Redraw images/articles/juju-watkins-comeback-tracker-usc-2026-27/per-40.webp from the new per-40 figures. The share image is the 1200x630 file saved over og-1200.jpg after publish, because a fresh hero build writes a crop of the lead photo into that filename.
5. Change the data-through date on the page and in this file.
