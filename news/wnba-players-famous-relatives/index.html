    Steph Curry, Donovan McNabb and JaVale McGee top our list of 36 WNBA players' famous relatives

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 19, 2026

    Steph Curry is Cameron Brink's godbrother, Donovan McNabb is Kia Nurse's uncle by marriage, and JaVale McGee is Imani McGee-Stafford's older half-brother. Angel Reese's younger brother, Julian, signed with the New Orleans Pelicans on Sept. 24, 2026. Full Court Buckets has checked 36 famous relatives of 27 current and former WNBA players against team, league and school records. The NBA accounts for 14 of them, and 18 of the players are on 2026 rosters.

    Updated October 19, 2026 · 27 WNBA players · 36 famous relatives · New family ties are added as they are verified.

    The two 2026 WNBA Finals teams have 9 of the players: 5 with the Atlanta Dream and 4 with the Golden State Valkyries.

    Cameron Brink and Stephen Curry

    Cameron Brink, Los Angeles Sparks

    Stephen Curry, godbrother. Four-time NBA champion and two-time NBA MVP with the Golden State Warriors. Not a blood relative. Curry's parents, Dell and Sonya Curry, are Brink's godparents, and Brink's parents are Stephen's godparents. Brink's mother, Michelle Bain-Brink, was Sonya Curry's college roommate at Virginia Tech, and her father, Greg Brink, was Dell Curry's teammate there.

    Sources: Stanford Athletics bio: Cameron Brink; Stanford Athletics feature, March 15, 2022; NBA.com bio: Stephen Curry

    Angel Reese: Julian Reese and Jordan Hawkins

    Angel Reese, Atlanta Dream

    Julian Reese, younger brother. NBA forward. Signed a two-way contract with the New Orleans Pelicans on Sept. 24, 2026, after starting 2026 with the Washington Wizards. Both siblings played at St. Frances Academy in Baltimore and at Maryland.

    Sources: University of Maryland bio: Julian Reese; NBA.com player record: Julian Reese

    Jordan Hawkins, cousin. Won the 2023 NCAA title at UConn, the same year Reese was Most Outstanding Player of the women's Final Four with LSU. Signed a two-way deal with the Chicago Bulls on Oct. 6, 2026.

    Sources: UConn Athletics release: national championship, April 3, 2023; Chicago Bulls release, Oct. 6, 2026

    Gabriela Jaquez and Jaime Jaquez Jr.

    Gabriela Jaquez, Chicago Sky

    Jaime Jaquez Jr., older brother. NBA forward and former UCLA All-American. The Miami Heat traded him to the Milwaukee Bucks on July 6, 2026, in the Giannis Antetokounmpo deal. The Chicago Sky drafted Gabriela fifth overall in 2026.

    Sources: UCLA Athletics bio: Gabriela Jaquez; UCLA Athletics bio: Gabriela Jaquez (basketball); Milwaukee Bucks release, July 6, 2026 (reprinted by Urban Milwaukee)

    Kia Nurse: Donovan McNabb, Darnell Nurse and Sarah Nurse

    Kia Nurse, Toronto Tempo

    Donovan McNabb, uncle by marriage. Six-time Pro Bowl quarterback who played 13 NFL seasons. He is married to Kia's aunt, Raquel Nurse, a former Syracuse point guard.

    Sources: WNBA.com draft profile: Five things to know about Kia Nurse; NHL.com feature on the Nurse family

    Darnell Nurse, older brother. NHL defenseman. The San Jose Sharks acquired him from the Edmonton Oilers on July 1, 2026. Their father, Richard Nurse, was a wide receiver for the CFL's Hamilton Tiger-Cats for six seasons.

    Sources: WNBA.com draft profile: Five things to know about Kia Nurse; NHL.com feature on the Nurse family; San Jose Sharks release, July 1, 2026

    Sarah Nurse, cousin. Three-time Olympic medalist for Canada who set the single-tournament Olympic points record while winning gold in 2022. Forward for the PWHL's Vancouver Goldeneyes.

    Sources: WNBA.com draft profile: Five things to know about Kia Nurse; Canadian Olympic Committee bio: Sarah Nurse; PWHL release: Vancouver signs Sarah Nurse

    Imani McGee-Stafford: JaVale McGee and Pamela McGee

    Imani McGee-Stafford

    JaVale McGee, older half-brother. Longtime NBA center, the 18th pick in 2008, and an Olympic gold medalist with Team USA. They have the same mother and different fathers.

    Sources: Big 12 Conference feature: Imani McGee-Stafford, Feb. 27, 2013; WNBA.com: JaVale McGee through his sister's eyes; Team USA profile: JaVale McGee

    Pamela McGee, mother. Two-time NCAA champion at USC, 1984 Olympic gold medalist and the No. 2 pick in the first WNBA draft. Team USA's profile says JaVale was the first son of a WNBA player to be drafted into the NBA.

    Sources: Big 12 Conference feature: Imani McGee-Stafford, Feb. 27, 2013; WNBA.com: JaVale McGee through his sister's eyes

    Candace Parker and Anthony Parker

    Candace Parker

    Anthony Parker, older brother. Former NBA guard who played for the Toronto Raptors. Orlando Magic general manager since July 2023.

    Sources: Tennessee Athletics Q&A with Candace Parker, Sept. 14, 2007; Candace Parker official site: About; Orlando Magic release, July 5, 2023

    Satou Sabally and Nyara Sabally

    Satou Sabally, New York Liberty

    Nyara Sabally, younger sister. WNBA forward. The Toronto Tempo took her in the 2026 expansion draft. Both played at Oregon and both played for Germany at the 2024 Olympics. They first faced each other in the WNBA on June 11, 2023.

    Sources: WNBA.com bio: Nyara Sabally; WNBA release: 2026 expansion draft results, April 3, 2026; WNBA.com game summary: Wings vs. Liberty, June 11, 2023; Oregon Athletics release: Sabally sisters named to Germany's Olympic roster

    Nneka Ogwumike and Chiney Ogwumike

    Nneka Ogwumike, Los Angeles Sparks

    Chiney Ogwumike, younger sister. No. 1 pick in the 2014 WNBA draft, two-time All-Star and a full-time ESPN analyst since 2018. Nneka went first in 2012 and Chiney went first in 2014. Stanford noted they were the second set of siblings taken first overall in a major American sports draft, after Peyton and Eli Manning.

    Sources: Stanford Athletics bio: Nneka Ogwumike; Stanford Athletics release, May 19, 2014; ESPN Press Room bio: Chiney Ogwumike

    Flau'jae Johnson and Camoflauge (Jason Johnson)

    Flau'jae Johnson, Seattle Storm

    Camoflauge (Jason Johnson), father. Savannah, Georgia rapper who was shot and killed in May 2003, about six months before she was born. She told MTV he named her Flau'jae because he wanted her name to rhyme, per Olympics.com.

    Sources: Olympics.com feature: Meet Flau'jae Johnson; Savannah Morning News report, March 24, 2023 (report)

    Diamond DeShields: Delino DeShields and Delino DeShields Jr.

    Diamond DeShields

    Delino DeShields, father. Played 13 Major League Baseball seasons for Montreal, the Dodgers, St. Louis, Baltimore and the Cubs. Her mother, Tisha, was an All-America heptathlete at Tennessee in 1991.

    Sources: Tennessee Athletics bio: Diamond DeShields

    Delino DeShields Jr., older brother. Drafted eighth overall by the Houston Astros and went on to play in the majors.

    Sources: Tennessee Athletics bio: Diamond DeShields

    Lexie Brown and Dee Brown

    Lexie Brown

    Dee Brown, father. Won the 1991 NBA Slam Dunk Contest. Played for the Celtics (1990-98), Raptors (1998-2000) and Magic (2000-02).

    Sources: Duke Athletics bio: Lexie Brown

    Kalani Brown and P.J. Brown

    Kalani Brown, Las Vegas Aces

    P.J. Brown, father. Played 15 NBA seasons and won the 2008 NBA title with the Boston Celtics in his final season. Her mother, Dee, and her father both played at Louisiana Tech.

    Sources: Baylor Athletics bio: Kalani Brown

    Tamika Catchings and Harvey Catchings

    Tamika Catchings

    Harvey Catchings, father. Played 11 seasons in the NBA, then played professionally overseas. Tamika spent parts of her childhood in Italy during his career there.

    Sources: WNBA.com feature: Tamika Catchings' legacy

    Iliana Rupert and Rayan Rupert

    Iliana Rupert, Golden State Valkyries

    Rayan Rupert, younger brother. NBA guard. Signed a two-way contract with the Philadelphia 76ers on July 8, 2026. Their father, Thierry Rupert, played power forward for the French national team.

    Sources: WNBA.com bio: Iliana Rupert; Philadelphia 76ers release, July 8, 2026

    Janelle Salaün and Tidjane Salaün

    Janelle Salaün, Golden State Valkyries

    Tidjane Salaün, brother. Sixth pick in the 2024 NBA draft by the Charlotte Hornets.

    Sources: WNBA.com bio: Janelle Salaün; Charlotte Hornets release: 2024 NBA draft

    Arike Ogunbowale and Dare Ogunbowale

    Arike Ogunbowale, Dallas Wings

    Dare Ogunbowale, older brother. NFL running back from Wisconsin. The Houston Texans signed him off the Las Vegas Raiders practice squad on Sept. 29, 2026.

    Sources: Tampa Bay Buccaneers feature, April 7, 2019; Houston Texans transactions, Sept. 29, 2026

    Kaela Davis and Antonio Davis

    Kaela Davis

    Antonio Davis, father. Spent 13 seasons in the NBA with the Pacers, Raptors, Bulls and Knicks.

    Sources: South Carolina Athletics bio: Kaela Davis

    Courtney Paris: Bubba Paris and Ashley Paris

    Courtney Paris

    Bubba Paris, father. Offensive tackle on the San Francisco 49ers teams that won Super Bowls XIX, XXIII and XXIV.

    Sources: WNBA.com bio: Courtney Paris; Michigan Athletics: Wolverines in the Super Bowl

    Ashley Paris, twin sister. Played alongside Courtney at Oklahoma. The Los Angeles Sparks drafted her in 2009, then waived her in June 2009 before the season.

    Sources: WNBA.com bio: Courtney Paris; WNBA.com 2009 transactions (archived)

    Veronica Burton and Ron Burton

    Veronica Burton, Golden State Valkyries

    Ron Burton, grandfather. The Boston Patriots' first-ever AFL draft choice (1960) and a College Football Hall of Famer from Northwestern. Her parents, Steve and Ginni, were both Northwestern student-athletes. She is the eighth member of her family to attend the school.

    Sources: Northwestern Athletics feature, April 1, 2019; National Football Foundation: Ron Burton, College Football Hall of Fame

    Te-Hina Paopao: Joe Salave'a and Megan Faraimo

    Te-Hina Paopao, Atlanta Dream

    Joe Salave'a, uncle. Nine-year NFL veteran at defensive tackle. Arizona named him associate head coach in January 2025. He was on Oregon's football staff when she signed with the Ducks, per the Daily Emerald.

    Sources: Daily Emerald (University of Oregon student paper) report (report); Arizona Athletics release, Jan. 9, 2025

    Megan Faraimo, cousin. Pitcher taken third overall by the Utah Talons in the AUSL's inaugural 2025 draft. Won the league's first title with Utah in July 2025.

    Sources: Sporting News report (report); AUSL inaugural draft results; UCLA Athletics release, July 28, 2025

    Naz Hillmon: Jawad Williams and NaSheema Anderson

    Naz Hillmon, Atlanta Dream

    Jawad Williams, uncle. Started on North Carolina's 2005 national championship team and had a brief NBA career. Joined the Cleveland Cavaliers' coaching staff in June 2025.

    Sources: Michigan Athletics feature, March 9, 2020; Cleveland 19 News report, June 18, 2025 (report)

    NaSheema Anderson, mother. Played at Vanderbilt and professionally for the Nashville Noise of the ABL. Naz, her mother, her uncle and her aunts have won eight Ohio state basketball titles between them, per Michigan Athletics.

    Sources: Michigan Athletics feature, March 9, 2020; Nashville Metro Council resolution R98-1304 honoring the Nashville Noise

    Kaila Charles: Ruperta Charles and Afia Charles

    Kaila Charles, Golden State Valkyries

    Ruperta Charles, mother. Ran track at Howard (1980-84), competed at the 1984 Olympic Games and was inducted into Howard's Hall of Fame in 2014.

    Sources: University of Maryland bio: Kaila Charles

    Afia Charles, older sister. Ran track at UCF and competed for Antigua and Barbuda at the 2012 London Olympics.

    Sources: University of Maryland bio: Kaila Charles; Olympics.com athlete record: Afia Charles

    Katie Lou Samuelson and Karlie Samuelson

    Katie Lou Samuelson, Seattle Storm

    Karlie Samuelson, older sister. WNBA guard who played at Stanford. A third sister, Bonnie, also played at Stanford.

    Sources: UConn Athletics bio: Katie Lou Samuelson

    DeWanna Bonner and Erica McCall

    DeWanna Bonner, Atlanta Dream

    Erica McCall, younger sister. Former WNBA forward from Stanford, drafted 17th overall by the Indiana Fever in 2017. Their father, Greg McCall, was Cal State Bakersfield's head women's basketball coach, per Erica's WNBA bio.

    Sources: WNBA.com bio: Erica McCall

    Brionna Jones and Stephanie Jones

    Brionna Jones, Atlanta Dream

    Stephanie Jones, younger sister. Former WNBA forward who played at Maryland. She was on the Atlanta Dream's 2026 training camp roster, Brionna's team.

    Sources: University of Maryland bio: Stephanie Jones; Atlanta Dream release: 2026 training camp roster

    Lauren Jackson and Gary Jackson and Maree Bennie

    Lauren Jackson

    Gary Jackson and Maree Bennie, parents. Both played for Australia. Gary played for the Boomers in 1975, and Maree played for the Opals from 1974 to 1982.

    Sources: Sport Australia Hall of Fame: Lauren Jackson

    Kristi Toliver and George Toliver

    Kristi Toliver

    George Toliver, father. NBA referee.

    Sources: University of Maryland bio: Kristi Toliver

    Frequently asked questions

    How many WNBA players have famous relatives?

    Full Court Buckets has verified 36 famous relatives of 27 current and former WNBA players as of October 19, 2026. Siblings are the most common tie (16), and the NBA is the most common sport (14). The list grows as new family ties are confirmed.

    Who is Angel Reese's brother?

    Julian Reese, her younger brother. NBA forward. Signed a two-way contract with the New Orleans Pelicans on Sept. 24, 2026, after starting 2026 with the Washington Wizards.

    Is Cameron Brink related to Steph Curry?

    Not a blood relative. Curry's parents, Dell and Sonya Curry, are Brink's godparents, and Brink's parents are Stephen's godparents. Brink's mother, Michelle Bain-Brink, was Sonya Curry's college roommate at Virginia Tech, and her father, Greg Brink, was Dell Curry's teammate there.

    Who is Gabriela Jaquez's brother?

    Jaime Jaquez Jr., her older brother. NBA forward and former UCLA All-American. The Miami Heat traded him to the Milwaukee Bucks on July 6, 2026, in the Giannis Antetokounmpo deal.

    Who is Flau'jae Johnson's dad?

    Jason Johnson, the rapper known as Camoflauge. Savannah, Georgia rapper who was shot and killed in May 2003, about six months before she was born.

    Who are the Sabally sisters?

    Satou Sabally and her younger sister, Nyara Sabally. WNBA forward. The Toronto Tempo took her in the 2026 expansion draft. Both played at Oregon and both played for Germany at the 2024 Olympics. They first faced each other in the WNBA on June 11, 2023.

    Who is Candace Parker's brother?

    Anthony Parker, her older brother. Former NBA guard who played for the Toronto Raptors. Orlando Magic general manager since July 2023.

    Is Jordan Hawkins related to Angel Reese?

    Yes. They are cousins. Won the 2023 NCAA title at UConn, the same year Reese was Most Outstanding Player of the women's Final Four with LSU. Signed a two-way deal with the Chicago Bulls on Oct. 6, 2026.

    Who is Veronica Burton's grandfather?

    Ron Burton. The Boston Patriots' first-ever AFL draft choice (1960) and a College Football Hall of Famer from Northwestern.

    Who is Lexie Brown's dad?

    Dee Brown. Won the 1991 NBA Slam Dunk Contest. Played for the Celtics (1990-98), Raptors (1998-2000) and Magic (2000-02).
