    Steph Curry, Karl Malone and Donovan McNabb top our list of 84 WNBA players' famous relatives

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 19, 2026

    Steph Curry is Cameron Brink's godbrother, Karl Malone is Cheryl Ford's father, Donovan McNabb is Kia Nurse's uncle by marriage, and JaVale McGee is Imani McGee-Stafford's older half-brother. Angel Reese's younger brother, Julian, signed with the New Orleans Pelicans on Sept. 24, 2026. Full Court Buckets has checked 84 famous relatives of 66 current and former WNBA players against team, league and school records. The NBA accounts for 27 of them, and 37 of the players are on 2026 rosters.

    Updated November 10, 2026 · 66 WNBA players · 84 famous relatives · New family ties are added as they are verified.

    The two 2026 WNBA Finals teams have 10 of the players: 5 with the Atlanta Dream and 5 with the Golden State Valkyries.

    By the numbers

    This count is Full Court Buckets' own. Every row was checked against an official record or, where none exists, a labeled news report.

    • NBA is the most common link: 27 of 84 relatives (32%).
    • Siblings lead the relations: 29 siblings and 33 parents.
    • WNBA families: 12 relatives also played in the WNBA, so the league has its own families: the Sabally, Ogwumike, Samuelson, Jones and Paris sisters, Bonner and McCall, and Pamela McGee and her daughter.
    • Spouses: 9 spouses are notable in their own right, 3 of them WNBA players. Only spouses with a public career and an official team, league or school record of the marriage are listed. Private spouses are left out.
    • Primary records: 78 of 84 rows rest only on official records. The other 6 also cite a news report, labeled as one.
    • Photos: 43 pairs have freely licensed photos of both people.

    How many WNBA players have famous relatives?

    Not a blood relative. Curry's parents, Dell and Sonya Curry, are Brink's godparents, and Brink's parents are Stephen's godparents. Brink's mother, Michelle Bain-Brink, was Sonya Curry's college roommate at Virginia Tech, and her father, Greg Brink, was Dell Curry's teammate there.

    Jaime Jaquez Jr., her older brother. NBA forward and former UCLA All-American. The Miami Heat traded him to the Milwaukee Bucks on July 6, 2026, in the Giannis Antetokounmpo deal.

    Jason Johnson, the rapper known as Camoflauge. Savannah, Georgia rapper who was shot and killed in May 2003, about six months before she was born.

    Satou Sabally and her younger sister, Nyara Sabally. WNBA forward. The Toronto Tempo took her in the 2026 expansion draft. Both played at Oregon and both played for Germany at the 2024 Olympics. They first faced each other in the WNBA on June 11, 2023.

    Who is Candace Parker's brother?

    Anthony Parker, her older brother. Former NBA guard who played for the Toronto Raptors. Orlando Magic general manager since July 2023.

    Is Jordan Hawkins related to Angel Reese?

    Yes. They are cousins. Won the 2023 NCAA title at UConn, the same year Reese was Most Outstanding Player of the women's Final Four with LSU. Signed a two-way deal with the Chicago Bulls on Oct. 6, 2026.

    Ron Burton. The Boston Patriots' first-ever AFL draft choice (1960) and a College Football Hall of Famer from Northwestern.

    Who is Lexie Brown's dad?

    Dee Brown. Won the 1991 NBA Slam Dunk Contest. Played for the Celtics (1990-98), Raptors (1998-2000) and Magic (2000-02).

    Who is Breanna Stewart's wife?

    Marta Xargay. Spanish guard who won a 2016 Olympic silver medal with Spain and played three WNBA seasons with the Phoenix Mercury. They married in 2021 after meeting as teammates at Dynamo Kursk in Russia.

    Penny Taylor. Three-time WNBA champion with the Phoenix Mercury (2007, 2009, 2014), three-time Olympian for Australia and 2006 World Championship MVP. Later a Mercury assistant coach.
