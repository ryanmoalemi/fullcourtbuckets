Valkyries Blow Out Wings 104-80 in Game 1

VALKYRIES 104, WINGS 80. A 37-11 SECOND QUARTER AND 18 THREES IN THE FIRST REAL HOME PLAYOFF GAME AT CHASE CENTER.

The Golden State Valkyries finally got a real home playoff game, and they made it a party. Cecilia Zandalasini scored 22 points, Tiffany Hayes added 21 off the bench, Gabby Williams had 20, and the No. 2 Valkyries ran away from the No. 7 Dallas Wings 104-80 on Sunday night at Chase Center.

According to the AP, it was the most points the second-year Valkyries have ever scored in a game. It was also their first playoff game at Chase Center after last year's home playoff game had to be played in San Jose.

The second quarter that ended it

The first quarter was close. Golden State led just 21-20 after one. Then the Valkyries caught fire. Kayla Thornton's three with 7:23 left in the second quarter made it 32-24 and forced a Dallas timeout, and it only got worse for the Wings from there.

Golden State won the second quarter 37-11. The Valkyries made 10 of 20 threes in the first half and went into the locker room up 58-31. The lead reached 36 in the second half. For the game, Golden State hit 18 of 36 from three and turned it over just five times.

Top performers

  • Cecilia Zandalasini, Valkyries: 22 points in 16 minutes, 7-11 FG, 4-5 from three.
  • Tiffany Hayes, Valkyries: 21 points off the bench, 7-10 FG, 3-5 from three, 4 assists.
  • Gabby Williams, Valkyries: 20 points, 4 assists.
  • Veronica Burton, Valkyries: 10 points, 5 assists.
  • Kayla Thornton, Valkyries: 10 points, 5 rebounds.
  • Paige Bueckers, Wings: 21 points on 9-10 shooting, 6 rebounds.
  • Kitija Laksa, Wings: 11 points, 3-4 from three.
  • Maddy Siegrist, Wings: 11 points.
  • Arike Ogunbowale, Wings: 10 points on 2-7 shooting.

Bueckers needed help

Bueckers was excellent. She made 7 of 8 shots in the first half and had 17 points at the break. The problem was everyone else. Dallas went 6 for 20 from three, and Jessica Shepard went 0-for-7. The Wings had won seven of their last eight games to grab the seventh seed, but Golden State has now beaten them in all four meetings this year, per the AP.

"When we're awake we're ferocious on defense," Valkyries coach Natalie Nakase said.

What's next

Golden State leads the series 1-0. Game 2 is Wednesday, September 30 at 9 p.m. ET at College Park Center in Arlington, Texas, on ESPN. A Valkyries win ends it. A Wings win sends the series back to Chase Center for Game 3 on Friday.

Dallas needs a lot more than one hot hand. If the Wings can't slow down Golden State's three-point shooting, their season ends Wednesday.
