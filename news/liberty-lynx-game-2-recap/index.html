Johannes Hits the Dagger Three, Liberty Sweep the Lynx 87-71

By Ryan Moalemi

LIBERTY 87, LYNX 71. JOHANNES HITS THE DAGGER THREE AND NEW YORK SWEEPS THE NO. 1 SEED.

Minnesota was still hanging around at 71-60 Liberty when Breanna Stewart hit a 14-footer to make it 73-60 Liberty. With 6:48 left, Marine Johannes stepped back from 24 feet and knocked it down, and it was 76-60 Liberty. Barclays got loud. Thirty-eight seconds later Jonquel Jones finished a layup and the free throw, and it was 79-60 Liberty. That stretch ended the argument. New York closed it out 87-71 and swept the No. 1 seed Lynx in two.

Liberty never trailed. Not once. Jones opened the night with the first five points, including a 23-footer, and New York led 26-21 after one and 49-37 at the half. The paint told the story all night. New York scored 44 points inside to Minnesota's 28. Jones finished with 21 points and 13 boards. Breanna Stewart matched her with 21 and went a perfect 9-of-9 at the line. Sabrina Ionescu added 18 points and seven assists, including four threes.

Minnesota's best look came early in the fourth. Johannes hit another one earlier in the quarter from 23. Courtney Williams hit back-to-back pullups, and for a minute it was 71-60 Liberty with life. Then Johannes hit that three. Jones kept eating on the glass. Stewart's 14-foot pullup with 1:38 left made it 87-68, and Kayla McBride's late three was just noise.

Williams led the Lynx with 18 points, eight rebounds, and five assists. Napheesa Collier had 14 and seven but picked up five fouls. McBride added 14. Minnesota shot 38 percent from the field and never found a lead. After a 91-75 Game 1 loss at home, this one in Brooklyn went the same way.

The No. 8 seed just knocked out the No. 1 seed in a sweep. Liberty moves on. Minnesota goes home early. Game 1 was Stewart's night. Game 2 belonged to the whole group, and Johannes' late threes were the ones that made sure there was no Game 3.

Box score: ESPN.
