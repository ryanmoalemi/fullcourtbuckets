    2027 WNBA draft lottery odds for every pick: Houston's most likely landing spot is No. 3

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 29, 2026

    Houston and Toronto each have a 32.9% chance at the No. 1 pick in the 2027 WNBA draft lottery on Nov. 15. Houston's single most likely result, though, is No. 3.

    2027 WNBA draft lottery odds for every pick

    The WNBA published each team's share of the 1,000 lottery combinations on Oct. 28, which only settles the odds for No. 1. Full Court Buckets used those combinations to work out every team's chance at every pick from 1 to 7.

    Why Houston is more likely to pick third than first

    Only two picks are drawn. Houston and Toronto each win one of those two draws 61.7% of the time. When neither wins, both fall to the next spots by record, and Houston has the worse record.

    Houston and Toronto both finished at .250, so they split the top share at 329 combinations each. The league broke the tie with the first tiebreaker, the 2026 head-to-head series, which Toronto won 2-1. That puts Houston ahead of Toronto when the undrawn picks are handed out, so Houston can do no worse than No. 3 and Toronto can do no worse than No. 4.

    The two picks go to Houston and Toronto in either order 32.3% of the time. In 8.8% of lotteries, neither of them lands in the top two.

    How the 2027 WNBA lottery works

    Six teams hold the seven lottery picks this year. Fourteen numbered balls produce 1,001 four-number combinations, with 1,000 assigned by record. The first combination drawn wins No. 1, and a second drawing decides No. 2. The rest of the lottery teams then pick 3 through 7 from worst record to best.

    Most teams' odds use their combined 2025 and 2026 records. Portland and Toronto joined the league in 2026, so the WNBA used only their 2026 records.

    Houston's spot comes from the Connecticut Sun. The WNBA and NBA approved the team's sale to Tilman Fertitta and its move to Houston for the 2027 season in May (WNBA). It will play as the Houston Comets at Toyota Center (Houston Comets).

    Washington holds Chicago's pick through a swap

    The Washington Mystics, who made the 2026 playoffs and played Atlanta in the postseason, hold the third-best odds. Washington got the rights to swap 2027 first-round picks with the Chicago Sky in the Feb. 23, 2025 trade that sent Ariel Atkins to Chicago. The same deal gave Washington the No. 3 pick in the 2025 draft, which became Sonia Citron, and Chicago's 2027 second-rounder (Mystics, Sky).

    Chicago went 26-62 over the last two seasons, which gives that pick 161 combinations. Washington takes that lottery pick, and Chicago gets Washington's own first-rounder, which falls outside the lottery. The pick has a 35.0% chance to land in the top two and can fall no lower than No. 5.

    Los Angeles has two tickets

    The Los Angeles Sparks hold their own pick and Phoenix's. They got the Phoenix Mercury pick on Aug. 2, 2026, when they traded Kelsey Plum to Phoenix for Monique Akoa Makani, Phoenix's 2027 first-rounder and its 2028 second-rounder (Los Angeles Sparks release).

    Together the two picks hold 36 of the 1,000 combinations. Los Angeles has a 3.6% chance to win No. 1 and an 8.4% chance to come away with at least one top-two pick. Most likely, it picks sixth and seventh.

    Seattle and Portland

    The Seattle Storm have 93 combinations and a 21.1% chance at a top-two pick. Their most likely spot is No. 4, at 60.7%. Portland, at 17-27, gets 52 combinations. The Fire's most likely result is No. 5, at 80.0%.

    When and how to watch

    The lottery is Sunday, Nov. 15, from 7:30 to 8 p.m. ET (4:30 p.m. PT) on ESPN, right after UConn vs. Louisville. It is also on TSN in Canada. Bethany Donaphin, the WNBA's head of league operations, will reveal the results (WNBA). To stream it, use the ESPN app with ESPN Unlimited or a TV provider login (Disney+ Help Center).

    This is the 26th WNBA draft lottery and the 15th straight on ESPN networks. A representative of Ernst & Young oversees the drawing. The 2027 WNBA Draft is Monday, April 12, 2027 (WNBA).

    Who the top picks could be

    The AP, ESPN and USA Today all name USC's JuJu Watkins, Texas's Madison Booker and Notre Dame's Hannah Hidalgo among the top prospects (AP, ESPN via ABC7, USA Today). Those rankings are projections, not a draft order.
