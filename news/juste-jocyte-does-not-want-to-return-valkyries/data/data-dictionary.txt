Universe
Every No. 1 through No. 5 pick in the WNBA Draft from 2008 through 2026.
Names and order are the WNBA.com draft page for that year.
2008-2014: the numbered results in the page description at wnba.com/draft/<year>.
2015: the FIRST ROUND RESULTS list on wnba.com/draft/2015.
2016-2026: the pick order on wnba.com/draft/<year>/board.
2012 No. 1 is printed "Nnemkadi Ogwumike" on the 2012 page. The FCB log name is Nneka Ogwumike.
2015 No. 5 is printed "Cheyenne Parker." The FCB log name is Cheyenne Parker-Tyus.
2019 No. 2 is printed "Asia Durr." The FCB log name is AD Durr.
2025 No. 5 is printed "Justė Jocytė." The FCB log name is Juste Jocyte.
2026 No. 3 is printed "Awa Fam Thiam." The FCB log name is Awa Fam.

First season
The earliest regular-season line (season_type 2) with at least one game.
A player drafted in one year and first appearing later is kept.
Her first season is the year she played, not the draft year.
seasons_after_draft is first_season minus draft_year. Zero means she played in the draft year.

Minutes
The log stores minutes per game to one decimal, not a minute total.
estimated_minutes is that per-game figure times games, summed across teams if the first season has more than one stint, then rounded to one decimal.
It is not a sum of official box-score minutes.
A one-decimal average can move the product by a few minutes.
Ranks that are close can change if the underlying seconds differ.
Jocyte's 129.6 is 8.1 times 16. Stories that say 129 minutes are rounding that product.
This file does not use 129.

mpg and ppg
Weighted from the same per-game figures times games, then rounded to one decimal.
They match the stored per-game number when the first season is one stint.
