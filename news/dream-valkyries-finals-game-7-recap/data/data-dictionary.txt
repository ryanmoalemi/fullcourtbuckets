Data through Game 7, Oct. 31, 2026.

game: Finals game number. The 1-7 row is the series sum, not a single game.
date: Game date. The 1-7 row is the span of the series.
site: Arena.
final_dream, final_valkyries: Points. The 1-7 row is the series point total.
margin: Absolute final margin. The 1-7 row is Dream points minus Valkyries points (28).
halftime_dream, halftime_valkyries: Score after two quarters. The 1-7 row counts how many games the halftime leader won (6 of 7) and how many the trailer won (1 of 7).
halftime_leader_won: yes if the team ahead at halftime won the game.
pace: Average of the two teams' estimated possessions. Possessions = FGA + 0.44 x FTA - offensive rebounds + turnovers. Player rebounds and player turnovers only. Regulation is 40 minutes, so pace is possessions per 40 minutes. The 1-7 pace is the average of the seven game paces' underlying possessions, (546.84 + 537.60) / 2 / 7 = 77.5.
dream_fgm, dream_fga, dream_fg3m, dream_fg3a: Dream field goals and threes, makes then attempts.
valkyries_fgm, valkyries_fga, valkyries_fg3m, valkyries_fg3a: Same for the Valkyries.
scoring_leader, leader_team, leader_points: Player with the most points in that game. The 1-7 leader_points cell is Gabby Williams' series point total (137), not a single-game high.
