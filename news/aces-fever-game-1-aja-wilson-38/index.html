A'ja Wilson Drops 38 as Aces Roll Past Fever in Game 1

By Ryan Moalemi

ACES 102, FEVER 85. WILSON GOES 15-FOR-19 AND LAS VEGAS TAKES A 1-0 SERIES LEAD.

The Indiana Fever came to Las Vegas hoping to steal Game 1. A'ja Wilson had other plans. Wilson scored 38 points, grabbed 16 rebounds and blocked four shots as the No. 3 Aces beat the No. 6 Fever 102-85 on Sunday to open their best-of-three first-round series.

Wilson was nearly perfect. She made 15 of her 19 shots, both of her threes and 6 of 7 free throws in 35 minutes. According to the AP, the 38 points matched her career playoff high, and it came on the same day she was named AP WNBA Player of the Year for the third straight season.

The run that broke it open

For most of the first half, this was a real game. Indiana led 16-14 late in the first quarter, and Caitlin Clark's three with 8:13 left in the second quarter tied it at 31. When Raven Johnson scored inside with 7:20 to go before halftime, it was tied again at 36.

Then Las Vegas took over. Wilson scored back-to-back buckets, Jackie Young and Jewell Loyd got going, and the Aces ripped off a 13-2 run to go up 49-38. They kept pushing all the way to the break, outscoring Indiana 26-11 over the last 7:20 of the half to lead 62-47. Wilson had 22 points by halftime. Loyd's three with 23 seconds left in the half capped the stretch.

The Fever never got closer than 12 in the second half. The Aces led by as many as 27, and even though Indiana won the fourth quarter 20-16, the lead never dropped below 15. Turnovers hurt Indiana all night: the Fever gave it away 17 times, and the AP reported Las Vegas turned those into 28 points.

Top performers

  • A'ja Wilson, Aces: 38 points, 16 rebounds, 4 blocks, 2 steals, 15-19 FG.
  • Jackie Young, Aces: 21 points, 10 assists, 3 steals, 8-14 FG.
  • Chelsea Gray, Aces: 14 points, 8 assists. Per the AP, she passed Courtney Vandersloot for second on the WNBA's all-time playoff assists list.
  • Aliyah Boston, Fever: 18 points, 8 rebounds, 3-6 from three, playing through what the AP described as a lower leg injury.
  • Caitlin Clark, Fever: 15 points, 10 assists, 6-17 FG, 3-10 from three, 6 turnovers.
  • Raven Johnson, Fever: 14 points on 6-7 shooting off the bench.
  • Kelsey Mitchell, Fever: 12 points on 4-16 shooting.

What they said

Aces coach Becky Hammon kept it simple: "She was a clinic tonight." Clark was blunt about Indiana's night. "I thought our actions were sloppy," she said, per the AP. Fever coach Stephanie White gave the Aces their due: "They're defending champions for a reason."

What's next

Las Vegas leads the series 1-0. Game 2 is Tuesday, September 29 at 6:30 p.m. ET at Gainbridge Fieldhouse in Indianapolis on Prime Video. A Fever win forces Game 3 back in Las Vegas. An Aces win ends Indiana's season.

For Indiana, the math is simple. The Fever need cleaner possessions, a healthier night from Boston and a lot more from Mitchell, who has been their go-to scorer all year. And they need an answer for Wilson, which nobody has found yet.
