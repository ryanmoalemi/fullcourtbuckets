    Valkyries Close Out the Wings 77-73, Win Series 2-1

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 3, 2026

    Dallas led 44-37 at half. Golden State won the fourth quarter 23-14, finished 77-73, and took the series 2-1.

    The Dallas Wings led 19-14 after the first quarter and 44-37 at halftime at Chase Center. The Golden State Valkyries outscored them 17-15 in the third and 23-14 in the fourth. The final was 77-73. The first-round series was tied 1-1. Golden State wins it 2-1 and advances to the semifinals.

    How the quarters swung

    Dallas scored 19, 25, 15, and 14. Golden State scored 14, 23, 17, and 23. Dallas led by 5 after the first quarter. The second quarter was 25-23 Dallas, so the lead at halftime was 7. The third was 17-15 Golden State. The fourth was 23-14 Golden State, and that was the closeout. Dallas's largest lead was 16. Golden State's largest lead was 4.

    The shooting split

    Dallas made 26 of 59 shots (44 percent). Golden State made 27 of 77 (35 percent). The Wings shot a higher percentage. The Valkyries took 18 more shots. From three, both teams shot 31 percent: Dallas 5 of 16, Golden State 9 of 29.

    Golden State had 40 rebounds, 14 of them offensive. Dallas had 36 rebounds and 6 offensive. Points in the paint were 34 for Golden State and 32 for Dallas. Fast-break points were 10 for Golden State and 2 for Dallas. Dallas turned the ball over 11 times. Golden State turned it over 6 times. Golden State had 17 assists and 5 steals. Dallas had 13 assists and 4 steals. Each team had 5 blocks.

    Dallas went 16 of 19 at the line (84 percent). Golden State went 14 of 18 (78 percent). Dallas was called for 23 fouls, including one flagrant. Golden State was called for 18 fouls.

    Series, and the semifinals

    Golden State won Game 1, 104-80, at Chase Center. Dallas won Game 2, 108-100, in overtime, and Arike Ogunbowale scored 45. Game 3 was winner-take-all. The Valkyries won it at home, 77-73. There is no Game 4. The first round is a best-of-three, and Golden State wins it 2-1.

    The Valkyries advance to the semifinals. ESPN lists the opponent as the Las Vegas Aces, in a five-game series. Game 1 is Sunday, October 4, at Chase Center, at 4:00 p.m. ET (1:00 p.m. PT).
