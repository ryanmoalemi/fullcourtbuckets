    Sarah Strong scores career-high 39 as UConn beats Iowa: where it ranks in UConn history

    Sunday's game

    The official box is the final at the M&T Bank Center at Gampel Pavilion. Tip was 2 p.m. Attendance was 10,244. Both teams moved to 2-1. UConn shot 34-for-62 (54.8 percent) and 11-for-21 from three (52.4 percent). The quarters were 20-14, 32-23, 20-14, 19-28. Halftime was 52-37. UConn's biggest lead was 27, at 4:47 of the third quarter. The officials were Maj Forsberg, Fatou Cissoko-Stephens, and Teresa Stuck.

    Blanca Quiñonez scored 21 on 7-for-10, including 4-for-5 from three, with 5 steals. The UConn recap calls the 21 a match of her career high and the 5 steals a career high. KK Arnold scored 14. Jovana Popovic had 10 points and 6 assists. Olivia Vukosa had 10 rebounds. Iowa's Chazadi Wright scored 23. UConn won the rebounding 38-22.

    The Nov. 7 notes call Strong the reigning national player of the year. UConn's April 2, 2026 release says she won the AP award for 2026, after the Naismith, Wade, and USBWA awards.

    Thursday's loss was 77-66 at No. 5 Michigan, in the Nov. 5 recap. Strong's box line that night was 13 points and 8 rebounds in 27:15, with 5 fouls. The recap says nine rebounds. KK Arnold, asked on the quotes page about the 72 hours after Michigan, said the team had talked through the film and moved on.

    Geno Auriemma, on that same quotes page, on how Strong bounced back: "I would say pretty good, right? I think she was really upset about what happened and how it happened and the way the whole thing evolved and she had a different look about her at practice and the way she was talking to the players and the example that she was setting as these couple of days went by and how aggressive she's capable of being. I think that was evident today."

    Strong, on the same page: "I don't know, I just felt good. If my team is gonna give me the ball and I'm open, I'm gonna shoot it. That's what Coach has been harping off for the last two years I've been here."

    On the Michigan game, Strong said: "I just think after the game, you just had to do a lot of self-reflecting. Personally, me, I just, I know I messed up in every aspect of the Michigan game. And Coach told me that and I just know how to be better for my team."

    Iowa coach Jan Jensen, on the same page: "She's the most versatile big-guard, guard-big."

    Before the game, UConn inducted Azzi Fudd into the Huskies of Honor. The Nov. 7 notes say that honor is for a first-team All-American. The recap says Gabby Williams and Tiffany Hayes, the 2026 WNBA champions, were back, and that national champions Tina Charles and Kalana Greene were in the building. Williams and Hayes are on the Golden State Valkyries pages. The series that ended with the title is the Game 7 recap.

    The Nov. 7 notes list Sunday's Iowa game as TV: FS1, with radio on the UConn Sports Network. They do not name a streaming service. The Nov. 8 recap lists the next two games as Thursday at No. 18 Ohio State, 6:30 p.m., on FS1, and Sunday at No. 7 Louisville, 5:30 p.m., on ESPN. That recap does not name a stream either. Those ranks are the same preseason AP poll.

    USA Today's game story says Strong had three steals. The official box lists zero steals and three assists.
