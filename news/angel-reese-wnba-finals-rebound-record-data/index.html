    Angel Reese's 78 rebounds set WNBA Finals record; Sylvia Fowles still leads per game

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 31, 2026

    Angel Reese has 78 rebounds in the 2026 WNBA Finals, the most by any player in one Finals series since at least 2002. Sylvia Fowles had 75 in 2017, in one fewer game, and her 15.0 a game is still the best rate.

    Most rebounds in one WNBA Finals series since 2002

    Seven players have grabbed 50 or more rebounds in a Finals series, eight times in all, with Fowles doing it twice. Only Reese and Fowles have reached 70. Every row links to the official box score for each game.

    Reese had six games. Fowles had five

    This is the first WNBA Finals to reach a sixth game. The best-of-seven format started in 2025; that series ended in four. After five games, she had 64 rebounds, 11 behind Fowles' 2017 pace. She passed 75 in Game 6 on Thursday and finished it with 14.

    Fowles' 20 in the 2017 clincher was the first 20-rebound game in Finals history, according to the WNBA's game story, and it broke her own record of 17 from Game 2 (WNBA). It is still the Finals high in our data. Minnesota won the title that night, 85-76.

    The two series also line up in other ways. Both had 21 offensive rebounds. Only Erlana Larkins had more, with 22 in four games for Indiana in 2012. Fowles had a double-double in all five games. Reese has four in six, the second most in one Finals since 2002.

    What Game 7 would take

    Reese can keep the record without a rebound tonight. Matching Fowles' 15.0 a game would take 27 in Game 7, 7 more than anyone has had in a Finals game since at least 2002.

    The other team has a more reachable mark. Kiah Stokes of the Golden State Valkyries has 60, third on the list. Sixteen in Game 7 would give her 76 and move her past Fowles into second. She had exactly 16 in Game 4 on Oct. 24, Golden State's 67-62 win in Atlanta.

    Her 30 and 16 in Game 2

    Reese's best rebounding game of the series was Game 2 on Oct. 19, with 30 points and 16 rebounds in a 79-65 win at Chase Center. Since 2002, three players have reached 30 points and 15 rebounds in a Finals game:

    • Jonquel Jones, 32 and 18, 2019 Game 2 (box score)
    • Breanna Stewart, 37 and 15, 2020 Game 1 (box score)
    • Reese, 30 and 16, 2026 Game 2 (box score)

    In Game 6 she had 15 points, 14 rebounds and 6 assists. That is the 12th Finals game since 2002 with at least 15, 10 and 5, and Reese is the sixth player to do it. Alyssa Thomas has six of them. Candace Parker has two, and Deanna Nolan, Elena Delle Donne and A'ja Wilson have one each.

    The turnovers came with it

    Reese also has 23 turnovers, the most by any player in one Finals series since 2002. Thomas had 20 in four games for Phoenix in 2025. Reese had six in Game 4 and six more in Game 6.

    The sixth game affects that number, too. At 3.8 a game, Reese ranks third among players with at least four Finals games, behind Thomas' 5.0 in 2025 and Chelsea Gray's 4.0 in 2022. Both totals are in the CSV.

    Her shooting has swung game to game. She made 10 of 16 in Game 2 and 2 of 12 in Game 4, and she is 31 of 77 (40.3%) for the series with 95 points and 20 assists.

    How we built the data

    Full Court Buckets pulled the player box scores for all 99 WNBA Finals games from 2002 through Oct. 29, 2026 from WNBA.com and added every player's games by series. That gives 527 player series and 1,841 player games, all in the two CSV files. We checked the leaders against each game's official box score and against the WNBA's 2017 Finals story on Fowles. We read coverage from the Sporting News, the AP in the Washington Post, USA Today, Sports Illustrated, ClutchPoints, Bleacher Report, EssentiallySports and the Idaho Statesman before building the table. Both CSV files are free to use and republish (CC0).
