JuJu Watkins' 1/1 Just Sold for $24,404: 10 Women's College Basketball Cards to Buy Before They Go Pro

By Ryan Moalemi

A 1/1 JuJu Watkins SuperFractor autograph, PSA 10, sold for $24,404 at Goldin on September 26, 2026. I would buy these 10 women's college basketball cards now, before these players reach the WNBA, while that sale is still this far ahead of the rest. One card on the list sold for $19.97.

1. JuJu Watkins, USC

The Big Ten named Watkins its 2026–27 preseason player of the year after she missed 2025–26 through injury. This 1/1 SuperFractor autograph, PSA 10, is the $24,404 Goldin sale from September 26, 2026, and every card below is measured against it. I would buy her women's college basketball cards before she reaches the WNBA.

2. Sarah Strong, UConn

Strong's UConn résumé includes the 2025 national championship and the 2026 Naismith, Wooden and AP player-of-the-year awards. Her highest verified sale is the 1/1 patch SuperFractor, PSA 7, at $1,220 on Goldin on September 27, 2026. That is $23,184 under Watkins' 1/1, which is why I would buy it before she goes pro.

3. Hannah Hidalgo, Notre Dame

Notre Dame's biography lists 25.3 points and 5.6 steals in 2025–26, plus ACC player of the year and defensive player of the year. Her top verified sale is a PSA 10 aqua refractor autograph at $150 on Fanatics Collect on February 9, 2026, $24,254 under the Watkins 1/1. I would buy that women's college basketball card before she reaches the WNBA.

4. Madison Booker, Texas

Texas lists Booker as a three-time Cheryl Miller small-forward-of-the-year winner, with 2025–26 AP first-team All-America honors and SEC tournament MVP. Her top verified sale is an ungraded prospect autograph at $25.41 on SportsCardsPro on July 6, 2026. That is $24,378.59 under Watkins' $24,404, so I would buy before she goes pro.

5. Mikayla Blakes, Vanderbilt

Vanderbilt records that Blakes led the nation with 27.0 points per game in 2025–26 and won SEC player of the year, and she scored 55 at Auburn on February 16, 2025. Her top verified sale is a PSA 10 Sapphire prospect autograph at $178.50 on SportsCardsPro on September 13, 2025. That is $24,225.50 under the Watkins 1/1, which is why I would buy it before the WNBA.

6. Joyce Edwards, South Carolina

Edwards averaged 19.2 points and 6.8 rebounds in 2025–26, made AP second-team All-America, and set South Carolina's single-season scoring record with 768 points. Her top verified sale is an ungraded red autograph numbered to 5 at $245 on SportsCardsPro on March 15, 2025, $24,159 under Watkins' 1/1. I would buy that women's college basketball card before she goes pro.

7. MiLaysia Fulwiley, LSU

LSU records 14.6 points per game and a second consecutive SEC Sixth Woman of the Year award in 2025–26. Her top verified sale is an ungraded gold wave autograph numbered to 50 at $170 on SportsCardsPro on April 1, 2024. That is $24,234 under the Watkins 1/1, so I would buy before she reaches the WNBA.

8. Mikaylah Williams, LSU

Williams' official biography lists first-team All-SEC honors in both 2025 and 2026, with 2025–26 averages of 14.2 points, 5.3 rebounds and 3.6 assists. Her top verified sale is a gold Sapphire numbered to 50 at $19.97 on SportsCardsPro on July 18, 2026. That is $24,384.03 under Watkins' $24,404, and it is the card on this list that sold for $19.97.

9. Audi Crooks, Oklahoma State

Crooks is now at Oklahoma State after three seasons at Iowa State, and that biography credits her with 25.8 points per game and 64.9 percent shooting in 2025–26. Her top verified sale is a gold patch autograph numbered to 50, CGC Authentic, at $28.80 on Fanatics Collect on April 6, 2026. That is $24,375.20 under the Watkins 1/1, so I would buy before she goes pro.

10. Jaloni Cambridge, Ohio State

The Big Ten's 2026–27 preseason announcement places Cambridge on both all-conference teams, with unanimous recognition from the media panel. Her top verified sale is an ungraded red autograph numbered to 5 at $79.99 on SportsCardsPro on April 10, 2025, $24,324.01 under Watkins' 1/1. I would buy that women's college basketball card before she reaches the WNBA.
