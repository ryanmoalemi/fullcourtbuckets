    Wings and Tempo each send 6 players to Unrivaled Season 3 rosters

    By Ryan MoalemiNovember 5, 2026

    The Dallas Wings and the Toronto Tempo each have 6 of the 48 players on Unrivaled's 2027 rosters. The Golden State Valkyries, the 2026 WNBA champions, have 5, and no two of them are on the same club. Unrivaled named the eight clubs on Nov. 4, 2026.

    Jessica Shepard and Maddy Siegrist are the only Wings teammates on the same club, both on Laces. Paige Bueckers is on Breeze, Arike Ogunbowale on Mist, Li Yueru on Phantom and Aziaha James on Rose.

    Tempo has two pairs. Marina Mabrey and Temi Fágbénlé are on Lunar Owls. Kiki Rice and Isabelle Harrison are on Mist. Brittney Sykes is on Rose. Aneesah Morrow is on Vinyl.

    Three Atlanta Dream players are on Vinyl: Allisha Gray, Rhyne Howard and Jordin Canada. Naz Hillmon is the Dream player on Hive. Hive also has Cameron Brink and Rae Burrell, the two Sparks players on Hive. Dearica Hamby is the Sparks player on Vinyl.

    Sporting News wrote that the 48 players were assigned to six teams. The Nov. 4 release lists eight clubs, six players each. The same Sporting News headline is right about the Valkyries: Gabby Williams and four teammates.

    Kelsey Plum was a Los Angeles All-Star in the July 7 release. The WNBA.com trade-deadline story says the Sparks traded her to Phoenix on Aug. 2. The Mercury roster on Nov. 5 lists her, which is why the table says Mercury. She and Kahleah Copper are Mercury teammates on Phantom and Breeze.

    Who is back on the same club

    20 of the 48 are on the club where they played the most games in 2026. Lunar Owls, Mist, Phantom and Rose each have 3. Breeze, Hive, Laces and Vinyl each have 2. Twelve played for a different club. Sixteen are on none of the 2026 stats pages.

    The Nov. 4 release says half the league is returning to a club from season one or season two, and that more than 75 percent are on multiyear deals. The 20 above are the players whose 2026 stats page matches the club they are on now.

    The Oct. 28 roster update says the league signed Kaitlyn Chen and Isabelle Harrison to one-year deals. Neither has a 2026 stats line.

    Mist beat Phantom 80-74 on March 4, 2026, for its first title, in the league recap. Breanna Stewart scored 32 and was the Championship MVP. She is back, with Arike Ogunbowale and Veronica Burton. Allisha Gray scored 12 in that game and is on Vinyl. Alanna Smith played 14 games for Mist in 2026 and is not one of the 48. Plum scored 40 in that game and is back with Phantom.

    Breeze coach Jarell Christian, in the Nov. 4 release: "I'm excited to welcome Paige and Dom back for their second season with the Breeze."

    Phantom coach Roneeka Hodges, in the same release: "I welcome the professionalism, skill and energy KMac, Li and Aaliyah will add to the Ghost Gang!" Kayla McBride, Li Yueru and Aaliyah Edwards are the players she welcomes. Plum, Natasha Cloud and Tiffany Hayes played for Phantom in 2026.

    All-Stars, the rookie, and who is not here

    14 of the 48 were 2026 All-Stars. Copper is in that 14 because the commissioner named her on July 22 to replace the injured Plum. Plum had already been selected, so she stays in the 14 too. Rose is the only club with no 2026 All-Star. Chelsea Gray is on Rose. The Oct. 28 reveal announcement calls her the 2026 Unrivaled MVP and an inaugural champion.

    Olivia Miles received all 74 Rookie of the Year votes. She is on Hive with Sonia Citron. Gabby Williams is the Finals MVP, on Laces with Jackie Young. The roster release calls Williams an Unrivaled newcomer, and she has no 2026 club stats line. The WNBA.com Game 7 blog says she scored 30 points in Game 7 and averaged 19.6 points and 2.6 steals in the series. Our recap is Game 7.

    Six of the 10 All-WNBA players are on a roster. Miles and Stewart are first team. Shakira Austin, Bueckers, Rhyne Howard and Young are second team. The other four are Caitlin Clark, Kelsey Mitchell, A'ja Wilson and Angel Reese. Wilson won the 2026 MVP. She is not on a club.

    Also selected as All-Stars and not on a club: Natasha Howard, Kiki Iriafen, Jonquel Jones and Nneka Ogwumike. Iriafen played 14 games for Phantom in 2026. Sabrina Ionescu is on the Liberty roster page and is not one of the 48. Brittney Griner played 13 games for Vinyl in 2026 and is not one of the 48. The Sun roster page lists her.

    Aliyah Boston was an All-Star starter and is out for 2027. The Oct. 28 update says she is recovering from a procedure the Fever had already announced, and that she is expected back in 2028. The reveal announcement that day says Rickea Jackson and Ezi Magbegor are out for 2027 with injuries. The same update says Unrivaled and Kate Martin ended her contract.

    The Nov. 4 release says the rosters include four 2026 WNBA champions. The Valkyries roster page lists five of the 48: Burton, Chen, Hayes, Thornton and Williams.

    Four players are marked rookies on a WNBA roster page: Miles, Rice, Flau'jae Johnson and Sydney Taylor.
