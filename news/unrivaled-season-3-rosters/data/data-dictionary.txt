Data through Nov. 5, 2026.

unrivaled-season-3-players.csv
  One row per player named in Unrivaled's Nov. 4, 2026 roster release.
  unrivaled_club: the 2027 club (Unrivaled's third season, tipping off Jan. 8, 2027).
  position: Guard, Wing, or Forward, the label on that club's roster page at unrivaled.basketball.
  wnba_team_2026: the team on the WNBA.com roster page for that club on Nov. 5, 2026.
  wnba_honors_2026: 2026 WNBA honors only. Blank if none of the honors below apply.
    Champion: the player is on the Golden State Valkyries 2026 roster page, and the WNBA.com Game 7 blog says Golden State won the 2026 title.
    Finals MVP: Gabby Williams, WNBA.com Game 7 blog.
    All-Star: July 2 starters, July 7 reserves, or the July 22 replacement (Kahleah Copper, for the injured Kelsey Plum). Plum was selected and is still marked All-Star.
    All-WNBA first / second: WNBA.com video titles dated Oct. 24, 2026. The Dream's Oct. 24 release also names Reese and Howard to the second team.
    ROY: Olivia Miles, unanimous, WNBA.com release of Sept. 28, 2026 (74 of 74 votes).
  unrivaled_club_2026: the club whose 2026 player-stats page lists the most games for that player. This is Unrivaled's second season. Blank if the player is on none of the eight 2026 stats pages.
  games_2026: games on that 2026 stats page. Blank if none.
  other_2026_club: a second 2026 stats page, with the game count. Courtney Williams has 4 games on Breeze besides 10 on Vinyl. Aziaha James has 1 game on Lunar Owls besides 2 on Hive.
  college: the College field on the WNBA.com roster page. Aaliyah Edwards' college is the one on the Phantom roster page at unrivaled.basketball, because the Sun roster table does not list a college. Blank if the roster page has no college (Marine Johannès, Dominique Malonga, Li Yueru).
  fcb_player_page: the Full Court Buckets player page.

unrivaled-season-3-by-wnba-team.csv
  One row per WNBA team that has at least one player on the Nov. 4 release.
  players: count of those 48.
  unrivaled_clubs: 2027 clubs, with a count when more than one player from that WNBA team is on the club.
  player_names: the 48, in release order within the counts above.

Not in this file
  Protected, drafted, or player-pool status. The Nov. 4 release does not label rows that way.
  Season 1 (2025) club. The 2026 column is season 2 only.
  The release's figures for multiyear deals, combined All-Star selections, and top-10 draft picks. They are the league's sentence, not a recount.
  WNBA season statistics.
