    Angel Reese scores 23 as Dream take 2-0 lead over Liberty

    Angel Reese scored a postseason-high 23 points in Atlanta's 101-98 overtime win over New York, including six of the Dream's 13 points in the extra period. She made all six of her free throws, the last two with 4.3 seconds remaining.

    Six of the 13 overtime points

    Atlanta scored 13 points in overtime. Reese had 6 of them. A 2-foot layup at 3:37, off a DeWanna Bonner assist, put Atlanta ahead 93-88. With 44.3 seconds left she scored again, a layup from Naz Hillmon, and Atlanta led 97-96. Breanna Stewart put New York ahead 98-97 with 33.8 seconds left. Hillmon's layup at 20.8 made it 99-98. With 4.3 seconds left, Reese made both free throws, and the final was 101-98.

    She had already made both free throws with 14.5 seconds left in regulation, which put Atlanta up 88-85. She finished 6-of-6 at the line. The only three was a 24-footer at 8:32 of the second quarter. She missed the other, from 25 feet at 6:16. Five of her 7 rebounds were offensive. The other seven overtime points came from Rhyne Howard (3), Jordin Canada (2), and Hillmon (2). Times are from the play-by-play.
