    Dream Rally From 11 Down to Beat Mystics in Game 1

    By Ryan MoalemiSeptember 28, 2026

    DREAM 92, MYSTICS 77. A 29-12 THIRD QUARTER AND 16 STEALS FLIP THE GAME.

    For a half, the Washington Mystics looked ready to steal one on the road. Then the Atlanta Dream turned up the pressure. Rhyne Howard scored 26 points with five steals, and the No. 4 Dream came back from 11 down to beat the No. 5 Mystics 92-77 on Sunday night in Game 1 of their first-round series.

    Angel Reese added 13 points and 12 rebounds in her first career playoff game. Allisha Gray scored 22, and Jordin Canada ran the show with 10 points and 10 assists.

    The turning point

    Washington came out swinging. Howard hit a three 22 seconds in, but the Mystics answered with an 8-0 run and built a lead as big as 11. Kiki Iriafen and Shakira Austin owned the boards, and Washington led 41-36 at halftime.

    The third quarter changed everything. Atlanta opened the half on a 7-0 run, capped by a Gray three that gave the Dream their first lead since the opening minute. Then DeWanna Bonner took over, scoring 11 of her 13 points during an 18-5 run that made it 61-48 with 2:29 left in the quarter. Atlanta won the third quarter 29-12, and the Mystics trailed by at least seven the rest of the way.

    The key was defense. Atlanta finished with 16 steals, a franchise playoff record according to the AP. Washington turned it over 22 times, and the Dream scored 27 points off those mistakes. Atlanta also lived at the free throw line, making 25 of 26. Howard went 10-for-10.

    Top performers

    • Rhyne Howard, Dream: 26 points, 5 steals, 7-18 FG, 10-10 FT.
    • Allisha Gray, Dream: 22 points, 8-16 FG, 5-5 FT.
    • Angel Reese, Dream: 13 points, 12 rebounds, 2 steals in her playoff debut, though she shot 4-15.
    • DeWanna Bonner, Dream: 13 points, 4 rebounds, 2 steals.
    • Jordin Canada, Dream: 10 points, 10 assists, 2 steals.
    • Sonia Citron, Mystics: 25 points, 8-15 FG, 7-9 FT.
    • Kiki Iriafen, Mystics: 15 points, 14 rebounds (9 offensive).
    • Shakira Austin, Mystics: 12 points, 16 rebounds.

    The Mystics actually won the rebounding battle 50-31. They just couldn't hang on to the ball long enough for it to matter.

    What they said

    "This is just round one," Dream coach Karl Smesko said, per the AP. Citron pointed to the third quarter: "We just let some of our discipline slip." Iriafen said the young Mystics now know what playoff basketball feels like: "We'll be better prepared for our game on Wednesday."

    What's next

    Atlanta leads the series 1-0. Game 2 is Wednesday, September 30 at 7 p.m. ET at CareFirst Arena in Washington on ESPN. A Dream win closes it out. A Mystics win sends the series back to Atlanta for Game 3.

    Washington is in the playoffs for the first time since 2023 and is still chasing its first postseason win since the 2019 title run. If the Mystics can cut down the turnovers, all those offensive rebounds could make Game 2 a very different story.
