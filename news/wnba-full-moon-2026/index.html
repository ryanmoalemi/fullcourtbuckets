    Home teams won 5 of 8 full-moon games, and that is not a moon effect

    By Ryan MoalemiNovember 3, 2026

    Home teams won 5 of 8 WNBA games on a full-moon date in 2026, 62.5%, against 190 of 345 on every other game, 55.1%. The Atlanta Dream were 1-2 on those dates and 37-16 otherwise. The one-day window, and the rituals players have described, are below.

    The eight games

    Every game has a winner, so the league's own win percentage on these dates is 50% by definition. The home rate is the number that can move. It moved by one game. Five home wins, where the rest of the season would put the count at 4.4.

    Four of the eight were on Friday, Aug. 28. June 29 and Sept. 26 were full moons with no WNBA game at all. Seven of the eight tipped in the evening, local time. The exception is May 31 at Chase Center, Aces at Valkyries, which tipped at 12:30 p.m. Pacific. That is still the Eastern calendar date of the full moon. It is not a night game.

    Aug. 28 at 12:18 a.m. Eastern, and Oct. 26 at 12:12 a.m. Eastern, were the previous evening in the Pacific time zone. The Las Vegas, Los Angeles, and San Francisco games on those Eastern dates were played the next local calendar day: 7 p.m. Pacific on Aug. 28 in Las Vegas and Los Angeles, and 5 p.m. Pacific on Oct. 26 at Chase Center. The +/-1 column is the check for that, and for the ordinary fact that the moon looks full the night before and the night after. Oct. 25 and Oct. 27 had no games, so Finals Game 5 has no neighbor in the window.

    The Dream's three full-moon games were a road win at Dallas, 82-81, on July 29, a home loss to Portland, 83-101, on Aug. 28, and a road loss in Finals Game 5, 55-64, on Oct. 26. That is 1-2. Their other 53 games were 37-16. Add one day on either side and the only extra Dream game is Sept. 27, a 92-77 home win over Washington in the first round. The window record is 2-2. Three games made the slump. One more game erased it.

    Las Vegas was 2-0 on the exact dates and 6-0 in the window, against 31-17 on their other regular-season and playoff games. Six wins for a team that won 31 of its other 48 games is not a moon result. The 6-0 also leaves out the Commissioner's Cup final on June 30, the day after the June full moon, which the Aces lost 85-93 at New York. That game is not in the regular-season or playoff counts.

    Pregame rituals the players described

    None of this says what a player does in the locker room. These five lines are from interviews the players gave. Bustle edited and condensed each one. None of the five mentioned the moon. A routine that did not come from the player's own interview is not here.

    PlayerWhat she saidInterview
    A'ja WilsonShe wears a tattoo of her grandmother's name on her left hand and rubs it before she plays. She puts her uniform on the same way every time, left to right.Bustle, May 22, 2025
    Sabrina Ionescu"I always have to take a pregame nap and a shower." After a good game, or a win, she tries to do the same thing again. She sits on the right side of the bus, and she said the warmup order has to stay the same.Bustle, April 21, 2025
    Cameron Brink"I always put my left sock on first. That's my one thing. I have no idea how it started, but I've been doing it from a young age."Bustle, April 10, 2026
    Kate MartinThe Valkyries play "YMCA" in the locker room before every game, she said, "because it's really been working for us." She gets dressed at the same time and eats the same thing, chicken and rice or chicken and noodles. "I don't like to change my routine, but I will if things aren't going my way."Bustle, Sept. 25, 2025
    Gabby WilliamsAsked if she has a good luck charm or a superstition, she said, "I don't." She also said that if she finds a song and plays well, she may keep listening to it. "Maybe that is a little superstitious."Bustle, July 28, 2026

    A sock, a nap, a song, or a tattoo can matter to the person who keeps it. Eight games cannot show that the moon mattered to the score.
