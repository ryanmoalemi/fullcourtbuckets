2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup

THIS IS THE OLYMPICS OF THE WNBA

The WNBA season stops after today. No games again until September 17. The league’s best players are headed to Berlin for the FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup. For the next two weeks, this is the Olympics of the WNBA.

Team USA’s roster has 58 combined WNBA All-Star selections, 16 WNBA championships and seven Rookie of the Year awards. Caitlin Clark. Angel Reese. Paige Bueckers. A’ja Wilson. Breanna Stewart. Napheesa Collier. Kelsey Plum. Rhyne Howard. Chelsea Gray. Jackie Young. Aliyah Boston. Kahleah Copper.

Caitlin Clark and Angel Reese are playing together for the first time. Clark can hit Reese running the floor before the defense gets set, find her on rolls to the basket and draw help defenders away from the paint. Reese gives Clark an elite offensive rebounder who can recover missed shots and generate second-chance possessions. Clark creates space. Reese attacks the space and the glass. Now we get to see how that combination works on the same team.

Kara Lawson will coach Team USA. She’s the head coach at Duke and won Olympic gold as a player in 2008. Her assistants are Golden State Valkyries head coach Natalie Nakase, Phoenix Mercury head coach Nate Tibbetts and Indiana Fever head coach Stephanie White.

Team USA opens against China on September 4, then plays Italy on September 6 and Czechia on September 7. Those three games make up the group stage before the tournament moves into elimination rounds.

The World Cup champion earns automatic qualification for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. The United States is already qualified because it’s the host country.

The United States is trying to become the first country to win five consecutive Women’s World Cups. Team USA has won the last four, hasn’t lost a World Cup game since 2006 and enters Berlin on a 30-game World Cup winning streak.

Like Steve Kerr said in Paris about the men’s Team USA, expectations are so high that anything short of gold is a disappointment. For the women at the 2026 World Cup, the standard is the same. Silver will be a disappointment.

And here’s how you watch it. All 36 World Cup games will stream live on HBO Max. Team USA’s group games against China, Italy and Czechia will also air on TNT, with the China and Czechia games also on truTV.
