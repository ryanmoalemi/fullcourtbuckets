    How this is calculated

    R = pre-series benchmark (92,599). C = combined reported attendance of completed 2026 Finals games (pending reports count as unknown, not zero). g = remaining future games in the selected series length. To tie: R − C. To exceed: R − C + 1, the exact required total. Approximate average across g games: (R − C + 1) ÷ g, shown to two decimals. A uniform whole-number target rounds that up, which is sufficient but not the minimum possible average. With no remaining games the result is final; no per-game target is shown.

    Methods, data & sources Dataset v1 · released Oct. 16, 2026

    Attendance is the figure published in the cited game record. Series totals add the game-level figures and do not measure unique individuals, ticket revenue or independently counted turnstile entries. Future-game assumptions are shown separately from reported results. Missing values remain visible with their status.

    Averages are calculated from unrounded totals and rounded to the nearest whole number, with .5 rounded up. Required combined-attendance thresholds are exact; approximate averages and uniform targets are labeled separately.

    Full Court Buckets compiled this data from official box scores. Every game row links to its published game record. Finals archive: through 2025. 2026 playoffs: through semifinals. 2026 Finals: through Game 3. The database has 96 games: the 2002–2025 archive plus 2026 Finals games played so far (1997–2001 not yet included). 2020 Finals games were played without fans; no attendance is shown for them, and they are excluded from sums and averages. Overtime is shown as regulation, OT, multiple OT or unknown. Lead changes are kept in the CSV exactly as reported by the source and are not shown or ranked on this page until reviewed game by game. Corrections: hello@fullcourtbuckets.com.
