    Four 2026 WNBA Finals games are set, and every viewer count is still pending

    Fast Facts for Media & Citations

    • 51 regular-season telecasts exceeded 1 million viewers in 2026, up from 19 in 2025 (about 2.7 times as many).
    • Games without the Fever averaged 984,000 viewers, up 24%. The 10-game first round averaged 1.3 million, up 37%.
    • October 2026 US search estimates: Atlanta Dream 1.22 million, Angel Reese 1.0 million, Golden State Valkyries 550,000.
    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 14, 2026

    The WNBA set four 2026 Finals games on Oct. 13, and not one of them has a released audience.

    Game 1 is Saturday, Oct. 17, at 3:30 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock, with the Atlanta Dream at the Golden State Valkyries. Games 5, 6 and 7 are if necessary, and the Oct. 13 release does not list them.

    Game 3 is Wednesday, Oct. 21, at State Farm Arena, at 8:00 p.m. ET on USA, CNBC and Peacock. That is the schedule to cite while the viewer cells stay empty.
