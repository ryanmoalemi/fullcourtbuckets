Stewart Scores 35, and the Dream Still Wins Game 2 in Overtime

By Ryan MoalemiOctober 7, 2026

This is the Game 2 recap from the WNBA semifinals, the New York Liberty at the Atlanta Dream. Breanna Stewart finished with 35 points and 11 rebounds, New York shot 35-of-64 from the field, and the Liberty still lost 101-98 in overtime (ESPN box score). New York even led by three with 2:01 left in the extra period.

The finish

Jordin Canada gained possession on the opening tip of overtime (ESPN play-by-play). Rhyne Howard scored a driving layup at 4:05. New York challenged the play, the replay supported the call, and the Liberty were charged a timeout. Howard made the free throw, and it was 91-88 Atlanta. Angel Reese then scored a 2-foot layup off a DeWanna Bonner assist at 3:37, and the Dream led 93-88 (play-by-play).

Marine Fauthoux hit a 26-foot three at 3:13. Leonie Fiebich tied it with a layup at 2:37. Jonquel Jones hit a 24-foot three at 2:01, assisted by Fauthoux, and New York led 96-93. Fourteen seconds later, Canada scored a 7-foot turnaround and it was 96-95. Reese made a layup off a Naz Hillmon assist with 44.3 seconds left, and Atlanta led 97-96. Sabrina Ionescu found Stewart for a 1-foot layup at 33.8, and New York led 98-97. Hillmon scored off a Howard assist at 20.8 to make it 99-98. With 4.3 seconds left, Fauthoux fouled Reese, and Reese made both free throws. Stewart missed a 32-foot three with 1.1 seconds left. Final: Dream 101, Liberty 98 (play-by-play).

How it got there

Game 2 was at State Farm Arena in Atlanta. ESPN lists the attendance at 9,574 (ESPN game page).

The first quarter ended 26-26. New York's biggest lead was 14-7 at 5:30, on a 4-foot Stewart shot. Canada's three with 23.3 seconds left, off a Reese assist, put Atlanta ahead 26-24. Stewart's 9-foot turnaround at 2.9 tied it (play-by-play).

Atlanta won the second quarter 30-28 and led 56-54 at halftime (game page). New York won the third quarter 20-19 and the fourth 14-13, and regulation still ended 88-88. Isobel Borlase's 24-foot three at 1:59 of the third made it 75-67, Atlanta's largest lead of the night. Fauthoux's 24-foot bank shot with 6.2 seconds left in the quarter cut it to 75-74 (play-by-play).

Howard's layup and free throw at 1:55 of the fourth made it 86-80 Atlanta. Ionescu hit a 24-foot three at 1:40. Stewart scored a layup with 26.5 seconds left. Reese made two free throws with 14.5 seconds left, and Atlanta led 88-85. Fauthoux then hit a 24-foot three with 10.3 seconds left, assisted by Stewart. Neither team scored again before the buzzer (play-by-play).

Stewart's 35

Stewart played 43 minutes and shot 11-of-21 from the field, 2-of-4 from three, and 11-of-13 at the line, with 6 assists, 2 blocks, and 1 turnover (box score). Jones had 21 points on 8-of-11 shooting, 8 rebounds, and 4 blocks, along with 6 turnovers. Fauthoux came off the bench for 20 points on 8-of-10 shooting, including 4-of-6 from three, in 31 minutes. Ionescu had 10 points on 3-of-8, went 2-of-6 at the line, and had 7 assists. Fiebich played 43 minutes and scored 4 points.

New York shot 12-of-25 from three and 16-of-23 at the line. Atlanta shot 34-of-84 from the field, 12-of-30 from three, and 21-of-23 at the line. New York had 36 rebounds to Atlanta's 35, but Atlanta had 11 offensive rebounds to New York's 5. New York committed 16 turnovers to Atlanta's 7. ESPN also lists 17 total turnovers for New York and 9 for Atlanta once team turnovers are included (box score). There were 17 lead changes. Atlanta led for 67 percent of the game, and New York led for 20 percent. Points in the paint were 44-42 New York. Fast-break points were 11-8 Atlanta.

What Atlanta got

Reese had 23 points on 8-of-16 shooting, made all 6 of her free throws, and grabbed 7 rebounds, 5 of them offensive, with 2 blocks in 39 minutes. She was plus-11. Canada had 23 points on 9-of-15, including 3-of-6 from three, and 8 assists in 39 minutes. Howard played 44 minutes, shot 5-of-20, and scored 18 because she made all 6 of her free throws, with 7 rebounds and 4 assists. Hillmon also played 44 minutes, with 12 points, 8 rebounds, 5 assists, and no turnovers. Borlase scored 11 points on 4-of-9, including 3-of-5 from three, in 14 minutes (box score).

Allisha Gray did not play. ESPN reported that she was ruled out of Game 2 with a finger injury and is day-to-day (ESPN).

Game 3

Atlanta leads the five-game series 2-0 (ESPN game page). Game 1 was a 92-82 Dream win (our Game 1 recap). Game 3 is Friday, Oct. 9, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, with Atlanta visiting New York. ESPN lists the tip at 7:30 p.m. ET, which is 4:30 p.m. PT (ESPN game page).
