    Ogunbowale Scores 45, Wings Beat Valkyries 108-100 in OT

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 2, 2026

    Wings 108, Valkyries 100 in overtime. Ogunbowale scores 45, and the first-round series is tied 1-1.

    The Dallas Wings were down 93-90 with 14.6 seconds left in regulation. Arike Ogunbowale had just fouled Gabby Williams, and Williams made both free throws. Dallas called timeout. Paige Bueckers found Ogunbowale, and Ogunbowale hit a 23-foot three with 4.1 seconds left to tie it 93-93. The Golden State Valkyries called timeout. Nobody scored again before the buzzer. Overtime was 15-7 Dallas. The Wings won 108-100 at College Park Center in Arlington, and a series Golden State led 1-0 after Game 1 is tied 1-1.

    How the quarters swung

    Golden State led 26-23 after one. Dallas took the second quarter 27-19 and led 50-45 at half. The Valkyries won the third 29-22 and took a 74-72 lead into the fourth. The fourth was 21-19 Dallas, so regulation ended 93-93. Overtime was 15-7 Dallas.

    The lead changed 15 times. Golden State led for 62 percent of the game. Dallas led for 28 percent. Neither team led by more than 8. The team that spent most of the night in front still needed overtime, and then lost it.

    The second quarter is where Dallas first grabbed the game. Veronica Burton's layup with 4:07 left in the half put Golden State up 41-38. From there to the break, Dallas outscored the Valkyries 12-4. Alysha Clark tied it with a 25-foot bank at 3:13. Odyssey Sims put Dallas ahead with a jumper at 2:32. Clark's 25-foot three with 29 seconds left made it 50-43, and Janelle Salaun scored with 3.3 seconds left. Halftime was 50-45 Wings.

    Kaila Charles scored the last basket of the third, a driving layup with 0.9 seconds left, and Golden State led 74-72. Ogunbowale scored 11 in the fourth. Williams' free throws at 14.6 seconds made it 93-90. The three at 4.1 seconds sent it to overtime.

    The shooting split

    Dallas shot 32 of 64 from the field (50 percent) and 12 of 23 from three (52 percent). Golden State shot 34 of 73 (47 percent) and 12 of 27 from three (44 percent). Both teams made 12 threes. The rebounding was dead even, 29-29, with Dallas getting 7 offensive boards and Golden State 6. Points in the paint were 34-32 Dallas. Golden State had 16 points off turnovers to Dallas's 13, and 10 fast-break points to 7. Dallas turned it over 13 times. Golden State turned it over 11 times, including one team turnover.

    The free throws were the gap. Dallas went 32 of 37 (86 percent). Golden State went 20 of 23 (87 percent). Dallas attempted 14 more free throws and made 12 more points at the line. Golden State committed 28 fouls. Dallas committed 22. Each team was called for one flagrant foul.

    Who scored

    • Arike Ogunbowale, Wings: 45 points, 12 of 23 from the field, 5 of 8 from three, 16 of 18 at the line, 7 rebounds, 4 assists, 3 steals, 5 turnovers, 42 minutes. Eleven of the points came in the fourth quarter and 11 in overtime.
    • Gabby Williams, Valkyries: 36 points, 12 of 23 from the field, 5 of 12 from three, 7 of 7 at the line, 6 rebounds, 7 assists, 3 steals, 37 minutes.
    • Alysha Clark, Wings: 21 points off the bench, 6 of 7 from the field, 3 of 4 from three, 6 of 6 at the line, 31 minutes, plus-15.
    • Aziaha James, Wings: 13 points in 18 minutes, 4 of 5 from the field, 2 of 3 from three, plus-19.
    • Kaila Charles, Valkyries: 14 points off the bench, 5 of 7 from the field, 23 minutes.
    • Kayla Thornton, Valkyries: 13 points, 4 of 8 from the field, 3 of 4 from three, 6 rebounds.
    • Veronica Burton, Valkyries: 11 points, 5 assists, 3 steals in 37 minutes.
    • Janelle Salaun, Valkyries: 11 points on 5 of 14 shooting, 0 of 3 from three, 3 assists in 30 minutes.
    • Paige Bueckers, Wings: 10 points on 3 of 13 shooting, 1 of 4 from three, 4 rebounds, 4 assists in 43 minutes. She assisted the tying three and the bank three that opened overtime.
    • Jessica Shepard, Wings: 4 points, 8 rebounds, 6 assists in 41 minutes.
    • Tiffany Hayes, Valkyries: 6 points, all at the line, and 3 turnovers in 15 minutes.
    • Kiah Stokes, Valkyries: 0 points on 0 of 1 shooting, 5 rebounds in 25 minutes.

    Overtime

    Ogunbowale opened the extra period with a 28-foot bank three at 4:32, again assisted by Bueckers, and Dallas led 96-93. Williams answered with a driving layup. James grabbed an offensive rebound and scored at 3:47 to make it 98-95. Williams then hit a 26-foot three, assisted by Burton, and it was 98-98 with 2:26 left.

    The play log at that same clock lists a flagrant foul on Tiffany Hayes, a referee-initiated review marked call overturned, a flagrant foul on Ogunbowale, and an Ogunbowale offensive-foul turnover. Ogunbowale made both flagrant free throws. Hayes made both of hers. The score was 100-100.

    Ogunbowale's 24-foot step-back three at 1:46 made it 103-100. Clark made two free throws at 1:28, and it was 105-100. Golden State did not score again. With 12.1 seconds left, a foul on James was overturned on a Wings coach's challenge, and Dallas kept the timeout. Kaitlyn Chen missed a pull-up, then fouled with 9.1 seconds left. Ogunbowale made both free throws. Williams missed a 29-foot three at the end. Final: Wings 108, Valkyries 100.

    Series, and Game 3

    Golden State won the regular-season series 3-0: 91-80 on June 17, 94-76 on August 7, and 78-70 on August 17. The No. 2 Valkyries (32-12) then beat the No. 7 Wings (27-17) 104-80 in Game 1 at Chase Center. Wednesday night was Dallas's first win against Golden State this year. Attendance was 6,251. The game was on ESPN.

    Game 3 is Friday, October 2, at 9 p.m. ET (6 p.m. PT) at Chase Center in San Francisco. Dallas is the visitor. It is on ESPN2. The series is tied 1-1 with one game left, so the winner moves on. ESPN Game 3 page.
