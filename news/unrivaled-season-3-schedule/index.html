    Unrivaled Season 3 schedule: Breeze plays six tour cities, Rose plays two

    By Ryan MoalemiNovember 7, 2026

    Every club plays 14 games, and every pairing is scheduled twice. Breeze is in six of the eight tour cities. Rose is in two, Los Angeles and San Francisco. Unrivaled posted the clubs on Nov. 6, on dates that were already on sale. Who plays in her own city is in the homecoming table. The player list is the Season 3 rosters.

    Opening night, and the rematch

    The Nov. 6 release puts the first game at TD Garden at 7 p.m. ET. Veronica Burton and Mist play Tiffany Hayes and Phantom, a rematch of last season's final. Mist beat Phantom 80-74 on March 4, 2026, in the league recap. At 8:30 p.m. ET, Paige Bueckers and Breeze play Flau'jae Johnson and Vinyl. The release calls that club Breeze BC presented by Thorne.

    The same night at Intuit Dome, Rose plays Lunar Owls at 7 p.m. PT, which is 10 p.m. ET. The release calls Napheesa Collier and Chelsea Gray league MVPs. At 8:30 p.m. PT, Hive plays Laces. The release says four Notre Dame alums are in that game: Sonia Citron and Olivia Miles for Hive, Jackie Young and Jessica Shepard for Laces. Miles' college on the rosters file is TCU. The release calls her a Notre Dame alum in this game. Cameron Brink and Rae Burrell are the other Hive players the release names for Los Angeles. The release calls Hive "Hive BC presented by Chegg."

    The other tour stops

    The Detroit stop, presented by Ally, is Jan. 11 at Little Caesars Arena. At 7 p.m. ET, Bueckers and Kaitlyn Chen on Breeze play Breanna Stewart and Mist. The release calls Bueckers and Chen 2025 NCAA champions and UConn alums, and it calls Chen "a 2026 WNBA" without the rest of that title. Chen is marked a 2026 champion on the rosters file because she is on the Valkyries roster page. At 8:30 p.m. ET, Kelsey Plum and Phantom play Rhyne Howard, Allisha Gray, and Jordin Canada of Vinyl. The release says all three are coming off the 2026 WNBA Finals.

    San Francisco the same day is Hive against Rose at 7 p.m. PT, then Laces against Lunar Owls at 8:30 p.m. PT. The release says Brink, a 2021 NCAA champion at Stanford, returns to the Bay Area to face Lexie Hull and Kayla Thornton of Rose. It says Gabby Williams, the 2026 Finals MVP, returns to Chase Center with Laces, against Sydney Taylor, Skylar Diggins, and Lunar Owls. Her 30-point Game 7 is in the Game 7 recap.

    The New York stop, presented by Maker's Mark, is Jan. 22 at Barclays Center, 7:30 and 9 p.m. ET. Lunar Owls play Breeze, then Hive plays Mist. The release calls Marina Mabrey a New Jersey native, Taylor a Long Island native, Miles a New Jersey native, and Citron a New York native. Those hometowns are not rows in the homecoming table. That table uses a bio that names the town, or a WNBA home arena. Miles is on the table for Target Center, not for New Jersey. The release also says Stewart, Burton, and Arike Ogunbowale return to the court where Mist reached the 2026 championship game.

    Minneapolis is Jan. 24 at Target Center. Mist plays Lunar Owls at 1 p.m. CT. Breeze plays Hive at 2:30 p.m. CT. The release says co-founders Collier and Stewart play each other, and that Miles and Bueckers are the two most recent WNBA Rookies of the Year.

    Houston is Feb. 12 at Toyota Center. Phantom plays Breeze at 6:30 p.m. CT. Vinyl plays Laces at 8 p.m. CT. The release says Phantom coach Roneeka Hodges returns to the city where she began her WNBA career with the Houston Comets in 2005. It says Howard, Allisha Gray, and Canada play Williams after the 2026 Finals.

    The Philadelphia stop, presented by Xfinity, is Feb. 15. Phantom plays Vinyl at 7:30 p.m. ET. Breeze plays Laces at 9 p.m. ET. The release says Natasha Cloud and Kahleah Copper play in their hometown at those two times, Cloud first. Cloud and Phantom play Aneesah Morrow and Vinyl. Copper and Breeze play Maddy Siegrist and Laces. The Inquirer put the second game at 9:30 p.m. The release and the schedule page say 9 p.m.

    Back in Miami, the release says Jan. 19 is the Mist championship banner ceremony. Vinyl plays Mist at 7:30 p.m. ET. Lunar Owls play Hive at 9 p.m. ET.

    Tickets went on sale before the clubs were named

    The Sept. 8 Boston announcement put tickets on sale that day and said the clubs would be named later. It set the start at 7:30 p.m. ET and said the time was subject to change. The Nov. 6 release and the schedule page put that game at 7 p.m. ET. The Oct. 1 Los Angeles announcement said opening weekend would start at 7 p.m. local time on both coasts. The Oct. 28 reveal announcement said tickets for the tour stops and for Sephora Arena were on sale, and that the matchups would come out Nov. 6.

    The tour page on Nov. 7 still said "TBD vs TBD" on the city cards. Boston's card there still said 7:30 and 9:00 p.m. ET, and so did Detroit's. The HTML of that page had no 7:00 p.m. ET tip. The schedule page and the Nov. 6 release put both cities at 7:00 and 8:30 p.m. ET.

    1-on-1, presented by Sprite

    The Nov. 6 release calls it the third annual 1-on-1 Tournament presented by Sprite, at Sephora Arena from Thursday, Feb. 18, through Saturday, Feb. 20. Round 1 is Thursday at 7 p.m. ET. Round 2 and the quarterfinals are Friday at 7:30 p.m. ET. The semifinals and final are Saturday at 6 p.m. ET. The schedule page says the bracket is still to be announced.

    Season 2 had two tour stops

    The March 10, 2026 league release says Season 2 added tour stops in Philadelphia and Brooklyn. It reports 21,490 in Philadelphia and 18,261 in Brooklyn. Season 3 has eight tour cities. Philadelphia and New York are back. Boston, Los Angeles, Detroit, San Francisco, Minneapolis, and Houston are the other six.

    That release says Season 2 games aired on TNT, truTV, and HBO Max. It also says Sephora Arena's capacity was increased, and it does not print the new number. The Nov. 13, 2024 facility announcement described a building capacity of 850. The arena page checked Nov. 7 does not print a capacity. This page does not treat 850 as the current figure.

    The Sept. 8, Sept. 17, Sept. 21, Sept. 28, and Oct. 1 tour announcements said those nights would be simulcast on TNT, truTV, and HBO Max, with the time subject to change. The Nov. 6 release does not name a network. The schedule page checked Nov. 7 says television is TBD and shows an HBO Max mark.

    How the rows were counted

    Club games are the 56 games with two clubs. Each club has 14, and each of the 28 pairings is on the schedule twice. Tour-stop games are the 16 games at the eight road arenas, two per stop. Miami games with a set tip are the rest, except the four March 5 games, which are counted in the flex column and in the 14. The 1-on-1 nights are on the game file and not in the club totals.
