    Liberty fire Chris DeMarco: only 3 WNBA coaches have lasted more than two seasons

    By Ryan MoalemiNovember 4, 2026

    The New York Liberty will not keep Chris DeMarco. Only 3 of 15 WNBA teams still have a head coach who has lasted more than two seasons.

    Liberty head coaches, 2022 through 2026

    What the Liberty said

    On Nov. 3, 2026, the Liberty account posted "Thank you, Coach DeMarco." The graphic reads "THANK YOU" and "CHRIS DEMARCO." It does not add a paragraph. The post is here. The same morning, Alexa Philippou of ESPN reported that sources said the team will not retain him. That story's publish time is 1:03 p.m. UTC, which is 5:03 a.m. Pacific. The Liberty news index opened for this story had no matching written release, and the team staff page still listed him. The WNBA.com team page does not.

    The hiring release, published Dec. 3, 2025, says the Liberty hired DeMarco, a Golden State Warriors assistant, that day. General manager Jonathan Kolb is the person quoted. Whether the deal ran through 2027 is not in that release.

    In 2026 the Liberty went 26-18 and were the No. 8 seed. They beat the No. 1 Lynx, 2-0, in the first round. The 2026 playoff bracket shows that series as NYL wins 2-0. The 2024 bracket and the 2025 bracket show the No. 1 seed winning the 1-vs-8 series. The 2022 bracket page did not open, and the 2023 bracket lists the winners with both teams marked TBD, so this page does not call New York the first No. 8 seed to beat a No. 1. Atlanta, the No. 4 seed, then swept them. The scores on the WNBA.com recaps are 92-82, 101-98 in overtime, and 85-83. Our recaps are Game 1, Game 2, and Game 3. The first-round games against Minnesota are Game 1 and Game 2.

    The Game 2 recap says the Liberty did not use their reset to advance the ball on the last play. In that same recap, 4.3 seconds is the clock on Atlanta's free throws after an overthrow by Sabrina Ionescu. It is not a separate note that New York was down 3 and still holding a timeout. That version is left out.

    On June 30, 2026, New York beat Las Vegas 93-85 in the Commissioner's Cup final at Barclays Center, on the WNBA.com game page. DeMarco was the head coach. The WNBA.com history page names the winning teams from 2021 through 2025 and does not name the coaches, so this page does not say he is the first cup-winning coach not to return.

    Breanna Stewart, Sabrina Ionescu and Jonquel Jones are on the roster page. What they said about the coaching change, and which contracts run into 2027 or 2028, is not in a team transcript or team release opened for this story. Satou Sabally and Leonie Fiebich are in the same gap. The offseason roster piece is five moves after the sweep. The service-time file is 2027 free agents. The league table is the standings.

    The other opening

    The Sparks parted ways with Lynne Roberts on Sept. 25, 2026, effective that day. Special advisor Farhan Zaidi's quote on the release thanks her through "our last game last night." The hiring story, dated Nov. 20, 2024, says the team announced it Tuesday, which was Nov. 19. Her two seasons were 21-23 in 2025 and 16-28 in 2026, 37-51, .420. The playoff ranks were 9 and 12, so both seasons are outside the eight-team field used above.

    Eight hires had a first season in 2025: Nakase (Oct. 10, 2024), White (Nov. 1), Marsh (Nov. 3), Smesko (Nov. 13), Roberts (Nov. 19), Meziane (Dec. 4), Johnson (Dec. 23) and Chris Koclanes in Dallas (Dec. 23, 2024). Koclanes was not the 2026 coach. Fernandez's Oct. 27, 2025 release names Fernandez as the next Wings coach.

    Five 2026 hires have a date on a release opened for this page: Sarama (Oct. 17, 2025), Fernandez (Oct. 27, 2025), Raman (Oct. 28, 2025), Brondello (Nov. 4, 2025) and DeMarco (Dec. 3, 2025). The Fire release is Portland Fire Names Alex Sarama As Head Coach, dated Oct. 17, 2025 on the team's news list.

    Nakase's Valkyries won the 2026 title. The AP recap of Game 7 says Golden State beat Atlanta 68-63. Our recap is Game 7.
