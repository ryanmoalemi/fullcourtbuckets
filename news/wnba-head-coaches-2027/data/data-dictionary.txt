WNBA head coaches, data through Nov. 4, 2026. Free to use (CC0).

wnba-head-coaches.csv
team: WNBA team.
head_coach: Name on the WNBA.com team page on Nov. 4, 2026. Blank when that page lists no head coach.
hire_date: Dateline or publish date of the linked team or WNBA.com release. For New York and Los Angeles, the date is the day the job opened. Portland is Oct. 17, 2025, the date on the Fire news list for the Sarama release.
seasons_with_team: WNBA regular seasons in the current uninterrupted stint. White's row is 2025 and 2026. The Fever hiring release says she also coached Indiana in 2015 and 2016, a 37-31 overall record and a 6-6 playoff record. Those seasons are a previous stint.
regular_season_w / regular_season_l: Sum of that team's wins and losses on WNBA.com standings for the seasons in the stint. Blank when the job is vacant.
win_pct: Wins divided by wins plus losses, three decimals.
playoff_w_l_2026: 2026 playoff wins and losses only. Missed means the team played no 2026 playoff game. Blank when the job is vacant.
status: In place, or vacant since the linked post or release.

liberty-head-coaches.csv
season: WNBA season.
coach: Brondello for 2022-2025 from the Toronto Tempo release. DeMarco for 2026 from the Liberty hiring release of Dec. 3, 2025.
regular_season_w / regular_season_l / win_pct: WNBA.com standings.
seed: League playoff seed, not a conference place. In 2023 the Liberty were first in the East and the No. 2 seed.
playoff_result: 2026 series from the WNBA.com bracket. 2025 series from the WNBA.com bracket (Phoenix wins 2-1). 2022, 2023 and 2024 from the Liberty team history page.
playoff_source: Page used for that playoff result.
coach_source: Release used for the coach's name.
