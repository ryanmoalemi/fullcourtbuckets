wnba-head-coaches.csv
One row per 2027 franchise, including open jobs. Data-through is the snapshot date, not a confirmation of a 2027 contract.
team: Franchise name entering 2027.
team_2026: Name used in the 2026 WNBA standings. Houston played as the Connecticut Sun.
head_coach: Current incumbent. Blank for vacancies.
hire_date / hire_source: Date and URL of the public team or WNBA hiring announcement, or the team biography for Reeve. Blank for vacancies.
first_season: First WNBA regular season of the current uninterrupted stint with the franchise.
seasons_with_team: Seasons from first_season through 2026, inclusive. Blank for vacancies. Stephanie White's earlier 2015-16 Indiana stint is separate. Rachid Meziane's seasons in Connecticut carry over to Houston.
regular_season_w / regular_season_l: Team wins and losses summed across the seasons in this stint. These are franchise standings records, including games an acting head coach may have handled.
win_pct: Team regular-season wins divided by games in the stint, rounded to three decimals.
playoff_w_2026 / playoff_l_2026: Team wins and losses in the 2026 playoffs. 0 and 0 mean the team missed the playoffs. Blank when no current incumbent exists.
playoff_wl_in_this_file: Text display of the same 2026 postseason totals. Earlier versions used this column for mixed periods.
status: In place or vacant as of the snapshot.
vacancy_date / vacancy_source: Public announcement date and URL marking an open job. Blank for occupied jobs.
data_through: Snapshot date, YYYY-MM-DD.

liberty-head-coaches.csv
One row per completed season from 2022 through 2026.
season / coach: Season year and head coach.
regular_season_w / regular_season_l / win_pct: New York regular-season standings record and winning percentage.
seed: Overall WNBA playoff seed.
playoff_result: Final series outcome. The 2026 row also includes the first-round upset.
coach_source / standings_source / playoff_source: Links for the head coach, season standings and postseason result.

Reproduce the tenure comparison: Select rows with a head_coach. Sort seasons_with_team ascending and sum the first ten values (1+1+1+1+2+2+2+2+2+2 = 16). Compare with Minnesota (17). The seventh value in the ordered set of 13 is the median (2). For the expansion adjustment, exclude Golden State, Portland and Toronto; seven of the ten remaining incumbents have at most two seasons.
