WNBA head coaches, data through Nov. 4, 2026. Free to use (CC0).

wnba-head-coaches.csv
team: WNBA team.
head_coach: Name on the WNBA.com team page on Nov. 4, 2026. Blank when that page lists no head coach.
hire_date: Dateline or publish date of the linked team or WNBA.com release. Blank for Portland because no Fire release with a date was opened.
seasons_with_team: WNBA regular seasons the coach was that team's head coach. White's row is the current stint, 2025 and 2026, not 2015-16.
regular_season_w / regular_season_l: Sum of that team's wins and losses on WNBA.com standings for those seasons. Blank when the job is vacant.
win_pct: Wins divided by wins plus losses, three decimals.
playoff_wl_in_this_file: Playoff games confirmed on WNBA.com recaps for this page. A note in the cell means earlier postseasons are omitted, not that the coach was 0-0.
status: In place, or vacant since the linked post or release.

liberty-head-coaches.csv
season: WNBA season.
coach: Brondello for 2022-2025 from the Toronto Tempo release. DeMarco for 2026 from the Liberty hiring release of Dec. 3, 2025.
regular_season_w / regular_season_l / win_pct / seed: WNBA.com standings.
playoff_result: 2026 series from WNBA.com recaps. 2024 title from the Tempo release. Other seasons are not in this file.
