series-team-games.csv
  One row per team per game, Games 1-6.
  shot_att = estimated shooting opportunities, FGA + 0.44 * FTA. A weighted estimate, not a literal count of shots.
  poss = estimated possessions from the play-by-play file (not used in the article tables)
  pts_off_to_official = points off turnovers on the WNBA.com box score
  turnovers = team total including team turnovers, counted from the play-by-play and checked against the official box
  first_half / second_half = sum of that team's quarter scores

series-player-lines.csv
  One row per player who played, Games 1-6. Plus-minus is the box-score figure.
  G6 minutes for Zandalasini, Canada and Hillmon differ by 1 from the official box. Other Game 6 totals used here match WNBA.com.

series-margin-games-1-6.csv
  One row per game. running_atl_margin is the cumulative Atlanta points minus Golden State points.

series-patterns-games-1-6.csv
  One row per game. yes/no flags say whether the winner had fewer turnovers, more points off turnovers, or more estimated shooting opportunities (shot_att), and who led at halftime.

series-player-splits-games-1-6.csv
  Win lines are games that player's team won. Atlanta wins: games 2, 3, 6. Golden State wins: games 1, 4, 5.

best-of-seven-finals.csv
  2025 length from the FCB Finals scores file (WNBA.com box scores). 2026 from the FCB game files through Game 6.

Source line: FCB game data, verified against WNBA.com.
Data through Oct. 29, 2026.
