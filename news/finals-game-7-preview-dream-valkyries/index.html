    Dream force Game 7 with 80-69 win, plus-33 margin in tied series

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 29, 2026

    Follow the play-by-play: Dream at Valkyries, Game 7 →

    The Atlanta Dream beat the Golden State Valkyries 80-69 on Thursday at State Farm Arena to tie the WNBA Finals at 3-3. Atlanta has outscored Golden State by 33 points, 437-404. Saturday's matchup will be the first Game 7 since the Finals moved to a best-of-seven format in 2025.

    Atlanta is plus-33 in a 3-3 series

    Golden State's three wins came by 4, 5 and 9, while Atlanta's came by 14, 26 and 11. The table below shows how that margin built game by game. The series scoring margin is 6 points in the first half (Atlanta 218, Golden State 212) and 27 after halftime (219-192).

    Thursday's quarters were Atlanta 32-14-17-17 and Golden State 15-11-21-22. The Dream led 46-26 at halftime in front of a sellout of 17,099. Allisha Gray scored 23 on 5 of 7 threes. Rhyne Howard had 21. Angel Reese had 15 points, 14 rebounds and 6 turnovers on 5 of 18 shooting. Gabby Williams scored 26 on 8 of 22 for Golden State, and Tiffany Hayes had 15.

    The WNBA had moved Game 6 to 12:30 p.m. ET at State Farm Arena, which seats more than 17,000, instead of Gateway Center, because the Hawks played in the building that night (WNBA). Dream ownership later said a midday Thursday "is not what we would have chosen and not what we fought for" (CBS Sports). After Game 5, Veronica Burton told Golden State fans, "Call in sick. I recommend it" (USA Today, Oct. 27).

    The ten top scorers in Game 6

    These are the five leading scorers on each side Thursday, not the series leaders.

    Atlanta made 6 of 8 threes in the first quarter. That matches the 6 threes the Dream made in all of Game 5. Gray and Howard combined to shoot 9 of 13 from three and scored 44 of Atlanta's 80 points. Gray's last basket was a three with 56 seconds left that made the score 80-69.

    Atlanta led 6-5 after a Gabby Williams free throw, then scored the next 13 points: a Gray floater, a Reese layup and free throw, a Canada three, a Gray three, and a Canada layup. Jordin Canada had 7 of her 10 points in that first half. Naz Hillmon scored all 7 of hers before halftime, on 3 of 4 shooting, and finished plus-15, the best plus-minus on the floor. Reese made a three. In this series she has made at least one three in each of Atlanta's three wins and has not made one in any of the three losses.

    Williams scored 4 points before halftime and 22 of her 26 after it. Hayes had the opposite split: 11 of her 15 points came in the first half, and she picked up her fifth foul with 5:01 left in the fourth quarter and Atlanta up 74-57. She did not score after that. Cecilia Zandalasini had 7 of her 9 points in the first half and finished 3 of 12 from the field. Kaila Charles scored all 6 of her points after halftime. Burton shot 2 of 13, and 0 of 6 from three, with 4 assists and 3 steals in 36 minutes.

    What has decided the six games

The team with fewer turnovers is 5-1. Game 6 broke that rule, with Atlanta winning despite committing 15 turnovers to Golden State's 11. The team with more shot attempts is 4-2. That edge failed in Games 5 and 6. The table below summarizes those patterns game by game.

Golden State has not scored more than 71 in any game of the series.

    The first Game 7 of the best-of-seven Finals

    The WNBA moved the Finals to a best-of-seven in 2025, replacing best-of-five, with the higher seed hosting Games 1, 2, 5 and 7 (WNBA). In the FCB Finals database, which includes every Finals game since 2002, no series reached a seventh game before this one. The longest series in that file is five games. The 2025 Finals, Las Vegas over Phoenix, ended in four.

    Splits inside the tie

    Angel Reese has 66 points on 22 of 46 shooting in Atlanta's wins and 29 points on 9 of 31 in its losses. Her rebounds stayed up either way, 42 in the wins and 36 in the losses. She has 10 turnovers in the wins and 13 in the losses. Allisha Gray is 8 of 17 from three in the wins (49 points) and 2 of 16 in the losses (34 points). Atlanta as a team shot 29 of 77 (37.7 percent) from three in its wins and 14 of 74 (18.9 percent) in its losses.

    Tiffany Hayes is minus-56 for the series, the worst plus-minus on either roster: 0, minus-18, minus-17, minus-17, plus-5, minus-9. She scored more in Golden State's losses (43 points) than in its wins (28). Kiah Stokes is plus-5 in Golden State's wins (26 points, 31 rebounds) and minus-46 in its losses (2 points, 29 rebounds). Naz Hillmon is plus-40, the best mark on either team. Gray is plus-35 and Reese plus-32. Williams, the series scoring leader at 107 points, is minus-15.

    Keys to victory for Game 7

    Atlanta Dream

    Score more points off turnovers

    6-0. The team with more points off turnovers has won every game. Winners lead that count 118-54. Atlanta can win that battle while losing the turnover count, which is what happened Thursday (18-12 in points off turnovers, 15 turnovers to 11).

    Lead at the half

    6-0. The halftime leader has won every game. Three of those leads were by 3 points or fewer, so a small edge has been enough. Atlanta's two largest halftime leads, 46-40 in Game 3 and 46-26 in Game 6, both became wins.

    Make the threes that showed up in the wins

    37.7 percent, then 18.9. Atlanta shot 29 of 77 from three in its three wins and 14 of 74 in its three losses. Gray was 8 of 17 from three in the wins and 2 of 16 in the losses, including 5 of 7 on Thursday.

    Win the second half

    219-192. Atlanta has outscored Golden State by 27 points after halftime. Golden State won the second half in Games 1 (26-25), 5 (39-33) and 6 (43-34). It won Games 1 and 5 but lost Game 6 after trailing 46-26 at halftime. Atlanta won the other three second halves.

    Golden State Valkyries

    Keep the score low

    3-0 at 132 or fewer, 0-3 at 144 or more. Golden State won the games with combined totals of 132, 129 and 119 points and lost the ones that finished 144, 168 and 149. It has not scored more than 71. Atlanta is 0-3 when it scores 64 or fewer and 3-0 when it scores 79 or more.

    Make Stokes's minutes count the way they did in the wins

    Plus-5, then minus-46. Stokes has 26 points and 31 rebounds and a plus-5 in Golden State's three wins. In the three losses she has 2 points, 29 rebounds and a minus-46. The rebounds traveled. The points and the plus-minus did not.

    Get Hayes out of the minus

    Minus-56. That is the worst plus-minus on either team, across six games: 0, minus-18, minus-17, minus-17, plus-5 and minus-9. She has still scored 71 points. The scoring and the plus-minus are telling different stories, and only the plus-minus lines up with the wins.

    Gray's hand

    Gray told the AP, in a story published while Atlanta led the series 2-1, "I am literally playing with eight fingers," and "It's knowing I am not 100% 'Lish,' the one that can hit shots and things like that" (AP). She then scored 23 points on 5 of 7 threes in Game 6. No Game 7 injury report was posted as of Thursday afternoon PT. The league report will be available here when it is posted (WNBA injury report).

    Six sellouts, and a building that has been full

    The Finals have drawn 105,489 fans through six games, and every one was a sellout: 18,064 at Chase Center for Games 1, 2 and 5, and 17,099 at State Farm Arena for Games 3, 4 and 6. That is already past the 92,599 the 2024 Finals drew, the high in our file since 2002. Our Finals attendance tracker has every game. A full Chase Center on Saturday would be 18,064 again. That crowd is not in yet.
