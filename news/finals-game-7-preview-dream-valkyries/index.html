    WNBA Finals Game 7: Atlanta's shooting vs. Golden State's defense

    By Ryan MoalemiOctober 29, 2026

    Follow the play-by-play: Dream at Valkyries, Game 7 →

    The Atlanta Dream have outscored the Golden State Valkyries by 33 points, 437-404, through six games. The series is still tied. On Saturday at Chase Center, the WNBA championship comes down to whether the Valkyries can contain Atlanta's offense or the Dream can reproduce the shooting that forced Game 7.

    Atlanta is plus-33 in a tied series

    Golden State's three wins came by 4, 5 and 9 points. Atlanta's came by 14, 26 and 11. The margin is 6 points in the first half (Atlanta 218, Golden State 212) and 27 after halftime (219-192).

    Where to watch

    • When: Saturday, Oct. 31 (Halloween), at 5 p.m. PT (8 p.m. ET). Pregame is 7 p.m. ET on USA Network.
    • Where: Chase Center, San Francisco. Golden State is the higher seed and hosts Game 7.
    • TV: USA Network (simulcast on CNBC). Streaming: Peacock. (USA Sports)
    • Series: tied 3-3. The winner takes the first WNBA title in either franchise's history (WNBA).
    • Canada: listings show TSN (Sports Media Watch).

    The Chase Center event page lists the game for Oct. 31 (Chase Center). NBC's how-to-watch note matches the USA Network, CNBC and Peacock lineup (NBC).

    Atlanta made 6 of 8 threes in the opening quarter of its 80-69 Game 6 win, matching its total from all of Game 5. That contrast captures the series: Golden State has won the three lowest-scoring games, while Atlanta has won the three highest-scoring games. Those games finished at combined totals of 132, 129 and 119 for Golden State, and 144, 168 and 149 for Atlanta. Atlanta is 3-0 when it scores 79 or more and 0-3 when it scores 64 or fewer. Golden State has not scored more than 71 in any game of the series.

    How Atlanta forced Game 7

    Atlanta led 6-5, then scored the next 13 points, and it led 46-26 at halftime. Allisha Gray and Rhyne Howard combined to shoot 9 of 13 from three and scored 44 of Atlanta's 80 points. Golden State answered after the break: Gabby Williams scored 22 of her 26 points in the second half, and the Valkyries outscored Atlanta 43-34 after halftime without erasing the 20-point lead. The basket-by-basket sequence is in the Game 6 recap.

    Jordin Canada had 7 of her 10 points in the first half. Naz Hillmon scored all 7 of hers before halftime, on 3 of 4 shooting, and finished plus-15. Tiffany Hayes had 11 of her 15 points in the first half, picked up her fifth foul with 5:01 left and Atlanta up 74-57, and did not score after that. Cecilia Zandalasini had 7 of her 9 points in the first half. Veronica Burton had 4 assists in 36 minutes.

    The WNBA had moved Game 6 to a 12:30 p.m. ET tip at State Farm Arena, which seats more than 17,000, instead of Gateway Center because the Hawks played in the building that night (WNBA). Dream ownership later said a midday Thursday "is not what we would have chosen and not what we fought for" (CBS Sports). After Game 5, Burton told Golden State fans, "Call in sick. I recommend it" (USA Today, Oct. 27).

    The patterns through six games

    Committing turnovers and allowing points off them are different counts. The two teams are tied 86-86 in turnovers, while the winners have 118 points off turnovers and the losers 54. In each of these six games, the team that scored more points off turnovers won. That is a description of the series so far. It does not prove those points caused the wins.

    Atlanta won Game 6 after committing more turnovers, 15 to Golden State's 11, because it scored more points from Golden State's mistakes, 18-12. The team that committed fewer turnovers won the other five games.

    The winner had more estimated shooting opportunities in Games 1 through 4. Games 5 and 6 went the other way. The halftime leader also won all six games. Leading at the half is where those games stood. It is not a separate way to win Game 7.

    Home teams are 4-2. Each side has won once on the other's floor, and each team is 2-1 at home.

    The first Game 7 of the best-of-seven Finals

    The WNBA moved the Finals from best-of-five to best-of-seven in 2025, with the higher seed hosting Games 1, 2, 5 and 7 (WNBA). In the FCB Finals database, which includes every Finals game since 2002, no series had reached a seventh game before this one. The longest series in that file is five games. The 2025 Finals, Las Vegas over Phoenix, ended in four.

    The players who could decide Game 7

    Angel Reese has 66 points on 22 of 46 shooting in Atlanta's wins and 29 points on 9 of 31 in its losses. Her rebounds stayed up either way, 42 in the wins and 36 in the losses. She has 10 turnovers in the wins and 13 in the losses. She has made at least one three in each of Atlanta's three wins and has not made one in any of the three losses. She went 2 of 8 from three in Game 6. The Dream are 16-0 this season when she makes at least one 3-pointer, 9-0 in the regular season and 7-0 in the playoffs, according to CBS Sports and High Post Hoops. The streak is a 2026 Atlanta number, not a career mark, and it follows the shot she rebuilt with coach Karl Smesko after joining the Dream (CBS Sports).

    Allisha Gray is 8 of 17 from three in the wins (49 points) and 2 of 16 in the losses (34 points). She scored 23 points on 5 of 7 threes in Game 6. Atlanta as a team shot 29 of 77 (37.7 percent) from three in its wins and 14 of 74 (18.9 percent) in its losses. Gray told the AP, in a story published while Atlanta led the series 2-1, "I am literally playing with eight fingers," and "It's knowing I am not 100% 'Lish,' the one that can hit shots and things like that" (AP). The official injury report for Saturday, Oct. 31 at 5:00 a.m. ET (2:00 a.m. PT), does not list her (WNBA injury report). That report lists Brionna Jones out (left leg) and Te-Hina Paopao questionable (left leg). Tiffany Hayes is not on it, which an earlier Saturday note had already reported (Underdog WNBA). ESPN's preview listed Iliana Rupert out (pregnancy). She is not on this report (ESPN).

    Plus-minus is the team's scoring margin during a player's minutes. It does not establish that the player personally caused that result. She scored 28 points in Golden State's wins and 43 in its losses. Kiah Stokes scored 26 points in Golden State's three wins and 2 points in its three losses. Her rebounds were 31 in the wins and 29 in the losses. Her plus-minus was plus-5 in the wins and minus-46 in the losses. Naz Hillmon is plus-40, the best mark on either team. Gray is plus-35 and Reese plus-32. Williams, the series scoring leader at 107 points, is minus-15.

    Keys to victory for Game 7

    Atlanta Dream

    • Create clean perimeter shots for Gray and Howard.
    • Turn Golden State's mistakes into points.
    • Get more efficient scoring from Reese.

    Golden State Valkyries

    • Limit Atlanta's early three-point opportunities.
    • Improve Burton's shooting efficiency. She shot 2 of 13 in Game 6, and 0 of 6 from three.
    • Get more offensive production during Stokes's minutes.

    Golden State's center rotation

    Natalie Nakase split the center minutes in Game 6, with Kiah Stokes and Laeticia Amihere at about 20 minutes each. It was Amihere's first real run since Game 1. ESPN reported that Stokes's legs "weren't moving as well" (ESPN). Who gets those minutes against Reese is the practical version of the Stokes task above.

    What they're saying before Game 7

    Gray, after her 23 points in Game 6: "In Game 7, I can't take my foot off the gas. I feel I can take it up to another level" (ESPN).

    Reese on playing at Chase Center: "I think that the crowd doesn't really get to us much, but I think, obviously, Game 7 is going to be a lot louder... The energy is going to be electric in there" (ESPN). She also pointed to the start: "When we punch first, we have won every single game" (ESPN).

    Burton and Williams on Golden State's slow start in Game 6: "That's unacceptable on our end... Need a sense of urgency" (X).

    Warriors coach Steve Kerr, asked about the series: "There's the pressure that comes with the Game 7 itself and it just turns into a street fight" (95.7 The Game).
