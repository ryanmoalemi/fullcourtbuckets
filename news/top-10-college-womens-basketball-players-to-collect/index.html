Top 10 Women's College Basketball Players to Collect in 2026

By Ryan Moalemi

Ten college players with a 2025-26 or early 2026-27 record you can read on a school or award site, and a card sale you can date.

A great player can still be a poor card buy when the asking price is a guess. Each name below is paired with one card. Where SportsCardsPro (the sports side of PriceCharting) has a completed sale with one price and a date, that sale is in the table. Ryan keeps a public Angel Reese card collection on this site. This piece uses the same habit: read the basketball note, then read the sale.

1. JuJu Watkins, USC

On Oct. 1, 2026, USC announced senior JuJu Watkins as the 2026-27 Big Ten Preseason Player of the Year, from both the coaches and the media. The release puts her 2024-25 line at 23.9 points, 3.4 assists, 2.18 steals, and 1.76 blocks, and her career scoring at 1,709 points, ninth at USC. She had already been Big Ten Player of the Year after that sophomore season. She missed 2025-26 after knee surgery, so this honor is a preseason pick on the way back, and the card is still the 2024-25 Bowman issue.

SportsCardsPro's page for the Purple Lava Refractor #37 (PriceCharting ID 8918037) shows a PSA 10 sale on July 15, 2026, at $42.01. The listing title is "2024 BOWMAN CHROME UNIVERSITY PURPLE LAVA #37 JUJU WATKINS PSA 10." A raw copy of the same parallel sold for $3.85 on Sept. 19, 2026. The page's guide that day was about $4.63 ungraded and $45.50 in PSA 10. The table uses the dated sales, and the guide is only context.

The catch: The PSA 10 sale at $42.01 and the raw sale at $3.85 are both real, and they are different buys. Match the slab in the listing to the grade you mean to pay for. A PSA population page was not opened, so no pop count is used.

2. Sarah Strong, UConn

Strong's cards sit on trophies she already won. The Atlanta Tipoff Club named her the 2026 Naismith Trophy winner on April 1. The next day, UConn said she was also the Associated Press National Player of the Year and the Wade Trophy winner, at 18.6 points, 7.6 rebounds, 3.4 steals, and 1.6 blocks, on 59.4% shooting, plus unanimous Big East Player of the Year and Defensive Player of the Year. The WBCA confirmed the Wade Trophy on April 2.

The photo is the logo-foil College Ticket #8. The sale that matches it is $2.99 on June 16, 2026, and the listing title says "College Ticket Logo Foil." A Nov. 4, 2025 sale on the same page, titled only "2024-25 Panini UCONN Sarah Strong #8," was $0.99.

What can go wrong: The foil and a plain #8 share this checklist page. The $2.99 sale is the row whose title names the logo foil.

3. Hannah Hidalgo, Notre Dame

The Naismith winners page puts Hidalgo's 2025-26 season at ACC Player of the Year and ACC Defensive Player of the Year, an NCAA single-season steals record of 202, and averages of 25.3 points, 6.9 rebounds, 5.6 steals, and 5.2 assists. It also calls her the second player in ACC history to win defensive player of the year three times, after Elizabeth Williams. The ACC's March 3, 2026 release names both awards, and the WBCA named her defensive player of the year on April 2.

Her basketball case is already on those pages. The card price is a separate question. New Waves #NW-16 sold ungraded for $3.49 on May 23, 2026. Two PSA 10 sales on the same page were $6.00 on Dec. 18, 2025, and $3.04 on Dec. 21, 2025.

Price check: Use the May 23 raw sale if you are holding the raw card in the photo. The gem sales on this page are still single digits.

4. Madison Booker, Texas

On April 4, 2026, Texas announced Booker as the Cheryl Miller Small Forward of the Year for the third time. The release calls her a first-team AP All-American and the second three-time winner, with Ashley Joens. It also names her MVP of the SEC Tournament and Most Outstanding Player of the Fort Worth 3 Regional, and lists 18.9 points and 6.7 rebounds on 50.8% shooting, with 738 points. The WBCA coaches' All-America team includes her too.

The photo is base Bowman Chrome Prospects #BCP-15. On July 30, 2026, a listing titled "2025-26 Bowman - Chrome Prospects Madison Booker #BCP-15 (RC)" sold for $1.00. A base-style title sold for $0.99 on Aug. 27, 2026. The same page mixes in other versions: a "/50" sold for $29.99 on July 13, 2026, and a Sapphire title sold for $2.85 on June 9, 2026. A May 10, 2026 "/10" row shows $62.50 and $80.00, two prices, so it stays out of the table.

Before you buy: BCP-15, BPP-15, and CPA-MB are different Booker cards. This section is the base BCP-15 whose title does not add a serial.

5. Mikayla Blakes, Vanderbilt

The card is tied to a night that already happened. On Feb. 16, 2025, Blakes scored 55 points in Vanderbilt's 98-88 overtime win at Auburn. ESPN called it the Division I women's freshman scoring record, one more than Elena Delle Donne's 54. She shot 15 of 28 from the field and 23 of 24 at the line. Topps made Bowman U Now Card 70, print run 2,168. The product page says relic and autograph-relic redemptions can replace the base card, with parallels at /49, /25, /10, /5, and 1/1.

LSU's March 3, 2026 SEC honors release lists Blakes as SEC Player of the Year, and the WBCA coaches' All-America team includes her. A 2025-26 scoring average from Vanderbilt's April award release was not re-opened, so it is not quoted.

Topps states the print run. The sale shows who paid. An ungraded #70 titled "Single-Game Scoring Record" sold for $3.99 on June 18, 2026. A PSA 10 sold for $34.99 on March 14, 2026. A March 14 ungraded title says "Presale" at $9.99 and stays out. Other sold titles on the page include "/2168," which matches the Topps print run.

The limit: $3.99 is the base title. A /10 or a relic redemption is a different card, even when the number on the front is still 70.

6. Joyce Edwards, South Carolina

South Carolina's bio lists 768 points in 2025-26, in 40 games and 40 starts. Dividing those two school figures is 19.2 points a game. Sports-Reference's game log totals the same 768 points and 272 rebounds in 40 games, which is 6.8 rebounds a game. The school page lists All-America recognition, and the WBCA coaches' All-America team includes her. An AP second-team line was not re-checked, so it is not used.

Topps sells her Bowman U Now card at an address ending in card-81. SportsCardsPro lists #80 (PriceCharting ID 9819637) and shows zero sold listings in every grade tab. The page still prints an ungraded guide of $4.99. That guide is an estimate with no completed sale behind it, so she is off the table. This note is the South Carolina forward, a different player from Aaliyah Edwards.

Where it can slip: $4.99 can get repeated as if a buyer paid it. On this checklist, the sold tab is empty.

7. MiLaysia Fulwiley, LSU

Fulwiley started at South Carolina and plays at LSU. On March 3, 2026, LSU announced she was the SEC Sixth Woman of the Year for the second time, with Blakes as SEC Player of the Year on the same release. Her 2025-26 line at LSU is 14.6 points, 3.6 rebounds, 3.5 assists, and 2.8 steals.

The photo is base McDonald's Chrome #29. On Aug. 11, 2026, "2023 Topps Chrome McDonald's All American Milaysia Fulwiley #29" sold for $1.50. On Sept. 24, 2026, a title on the same page that says "RC /75" sold for $6.69. A PSA 10 of #29 sold for $20.00 on Jan. 29, 2026.

The hang-up: The newest sale is the /75 at $6.69. If the card in your hand has no serial, the Aug. 11 base sale is the closer comp.

8. Jazzy Davidson, USC

Davidson is here because the same Oct. 1 USC release spells out her freshman year. She was the USBWA and WBCA National Freshman of the Year. She averaged 17.9 points, 5.9 rebounds, 4.1 assists, 2.0 steals, and 2.0 blocks, started all 32 games, and was the only Division I player to lead her team in points, rebounds, assists, steals, and blocks. USC also said she was the only Division I player, men's or women's, to average at least 2.0 steals and 2.0 blocks. She is on the preseason All-Big Ten team with Watkins.

No card image and no dated sale are attached. A SportsCardsPro page for a specific Davidson card was not opened, so there is no price to print.

Keep in mind: The freshman awards are on USC's site. A card price waits until a completed sale has a date you can cite.

9. Raven Johnson, South Carolina

LSU's March 3 release names Johnson the SEC Defensive Player of the Year. The WBCA's 2026 coaches' All-America team includes her with Edwards, Booker, Blakes, Hidalgo, and Strong. A season scoring line and a card sale were not pulled, so neither is printed.

Watch the player. Check the card before you pay. The first job on a listing is the Edwards test: one completed price, one date, and an empty sold tab does not count.

The caution: Any later bump is something to watch for. It is money you have not seen in a sale yet.

10. Mikaylah Williams, LSU

LSU's 2026-27 bio says Williams started 35 games as a junior in 2025-26 and averaged 14.2 points, 5.3 rebounds, 3.6 assists, and 1.4 steals. She led the Tigers in threes made (50), defensive rebounds (159), assists (127), and minutes (999). She made the All-SEC first team again, earned an Associated Press All-America honorable mention, and was a Cheryl Miller Award finalist. She passed 1,500 career points against Tennessee on Feb. 26.

The photo is Sapphire #4. On Aug. 27, 2026, "2023-24 Bowman University Chrome Sapphire #4 Mikaylah Williams Rookie LSU Tigers" sold for $1.65, the newest single-price raw sale on that page. A $1.99 sale dated Aug. 1, 2026, does not appear on this Sapphire checklist, so it is not used. A PSA 10 of the Sapphire sold for $12.09 on Feb. 13, 2025. An Aug. 1 price on a Jalen Williams Prizm is a different player and a different card.

One last check: The listing title has to say Sapphire. A base Bowman University Chrome #4 is a different sale from the card in the photo.

How to use the list

Start with the player you already follow. If her card is in the table, that dated sale is the comp for that title, and a serial or another grade needs its own row. Edwards, Davidson, and Johnson are here for the basketball note, and the card still needs a sale you can point at. Pick the player, look up a dated sale, and leave the listing if the price is a story you cannot find on the page.
