2027 WNBA coaching tracker: open jobs and vacancy clock

By Ryan Moalemi
Nov. 4, 2026

As of Nov. 4, 2026, two WNBA head coaching jobs are open. The Sparks job has been open for 40 days. The Liberty job has been open for 1 day. No permanent replacement has been named. These are the only two openings.

Day 0 is the date the team publicly announces the departure. Days open are calendar days from that announcement through Nov. 4, 2026. The wait column is calendar days from the coach's last game to the announcement. For a playoff team, the last game is the elimination game. Naming an interim coach would not stop the clock. Neither team has named one.

Open jobs as of Nov. 4, 2026

Team | Coach | Last game | Announced | Exit | Wait | Days open | 2026 season
Los Angeles Sparks | Lynne Roberts | Sept. 24 | Sept. 25 | Fired | 1 | 40 | 16-28, tied for 10th, missed playoffs
New York Liberty | Chris DeMarco | Oct. 9 | Nov. 3 | Fired | 25 | 1 | 26-18, No. 8 seed, lost semifinals 0-3

Two coaches have been fired. Two permanent jobs are open. Zero permanent hires have been announced.

Fired follows national reporting. The Sparks' release says the team parted ways with Roberts, effective that day. The Liberty's public post says "Thank you, Coach DeMarco." Alexa Philippou of ESPN reported that New York would not retain him. Field Level Media and the New York Post reported that he was fired.

On Nov. 3, the day New York moved, the Sparks job had been open for 39 days.

The Sparks are 16 days short of the 56 days they took to hire Roberts. Day 56 of the current search is Nov. 20, 2026. The Liberty's 2025 search reached a reported agreement on day 59 and an official hire on day 71, 12 days later. The same intervals from Nov. 3, 2026 land on Jan. 1, 2027 and Jan. 13, 2027.

Los Angeles Sparks

Status as of Nov. 4: open 40 days. Wait after the last game: 1 day. General manager: Ariana Andonian, named Sept. 4, 2026. Interim: none named.

The Sparks lost their season finale on Thursday night, Sept. 24, at Crypto.com Arena. Golden State won 76-75. The WNBA.com game page, in an Associated Press recap, says Los Angeles led 69-57 with 4:42 left and did not make another field goal until the final second. Golden State closed on a 17-4 run. The same page lists the Sparks' largest lead at 16. Lynne Roberts was called for a technical foul with 10.8 seconds left. The game was Nneka Ogwumike's last. She finished with 17 points and 15 rebounds.

The Sparks announced the move the next morning. The team release, timestamped 10:31 a.m. PT on Sept. 25, says Los Angeles "parted ways with Head Coach Lynne Roberts, effective today." Special advisor Farhan Zaidi said Roberts' commitment "was evident all the way through our last game last night." Sabreena Merchant of The Athletic and Marisa Ingemi of the Los Angeles Times both reported the move as a firing. Before the finale, Roberts told the Times she thought the franchise needed stability: "just give us a goalpost, and we'll march down the field in that way."

Roberts' two seasons were 21-23 in 2025 and 16-28 in 2026, a combined 37-51. That is five wins fewer than 2025. Los Angeles, Chicago, and Phoenix all finished 16-28. Nine teams had a better record, so the Sparks are tied for 10th. They missed the playoffs for a sixth straight season. In September 2024, the Associated Press described Miller's exit as the fourth consecutive miss. The 2025 and 2026 misses make six.

The contract figure is Merchant's, from sources, and the team has not confirmed a payment. Roberts had finished year two of a four-year deal with a team option in year five. The deal was not fully guaranteed. Because she was fired without cause, Merchant reported, the Sparks owe the lesser of one year or the remaining base salary. Year three was scheduled at $950,000 and year four at $975,000, so the reported obligation is $950,000.

Andonian was already the general manager when Roberts was dismissed. The Sparks' hiring release is dated Sept. 4. Merchant wrote on Sept. 25 that Andonian had been working since the start of the month and would be introduced the next week. At her introductory press conference on Sept. 29, David Yapkowitz of ClutchPoints quoted Andonian: "We want to take our time with this process and make sure that we get it right. We want an excellent leader, an excellent communicator, someone who is collaborative and willing to come to work in the W and work with all of us." In the same answer she called the coach she wants "a great developer of talent." Lindsay Gibbs of CBS Sports also reported that the franchise is up for sale.

New York Liberty

Status as of Nov. 4: open 1 day. Wait after the last game: 25 days. General manager: Jonathan Kolb. Interim: none reported.

DeMarco's season ended Friday night, Oct. 9, at Barclays Center. The fourth-seeded Atlanta Dream won 85-83 and completed a three-game semifinal sweep. The Associated Press recap on WNBA.com says Breanna Stewart and Jonquel Jones scored 26 points apiece. New York had swept the top-seeded Minnesota Lynx in the first round, then lost the semifinals. The Liberty finished the regular season 26-18 and were the No. 8 seed, one win fewer than their 27-17 season in 2025.

On June 30 they beat Las Vegas 93-85 at Barclays Center to win the Commissioner's Cup.

On Nov. 3, Philippou reported that the Liberty would not retain DeMarco after one season, and that he had said he expected to return in 2027. The same day, the Liberty account posted "Thank you, Coach DeMarco." Field Level Media reported that DeMarco, who was under contract for 2027, was fired. The New York Post confirmed the firing and said the terms of any buyout were not immediately known. Sabrina Ionescu, in the Field Level Media account: "I don't know what's going to happen," she said. "Regardless, thankful for him coaching us this year and giving us a chance, and we just fell short."

This is New York's third head coach in three seasons. Sandy Brondello coached the 2024 championship team and the 2025 team. On Sept. 23, 2025, the Liberty announced they would not renew her contract after a first-round loss. The Associated Press quoted Kolb thanking her as the first coach to win a title for the franchise. DeMarco's hiring was reported by ESPN on Nov. 21, 2025. The Liberty's official release is dated Dec. 3, 2025, and calls him a four-time NBA champion. Philippou reported that Stewart, Jones, and Ionescu are under contract through 2028. Gibbs reported that Satou Sabally and Leonie Fiebich are under contract for next season. Gibbs also wrote that, at that point, Los Angeles was the only other head coaching vacancy.

Who still has a job is in Full Court Buckets' WNBA head coach guide. Only 3 of 15 coaches have lasted more than two seasons with their current team.

Methodology

The tracker follows WNBA head coaching changes from each team's last game of 2026 through the first tip of the 2027 regular season. This file is the Nov. 4, 2026 edition. Any job still open at the 2027 opener keeps its final day count and is marked open at opening tip.

Three dates are stored separately when they exist: the first report by credentialed media, the team's public announcement, and the team's announcement of a permanent hire. The vacancy clock uses the team's announcement as Day 0 and the team's permanent-hire announcement as the end. A reported agreement before that announcement is a marker on the way, which is why New York's 2025 search is 59 days to the report and 71 days to the official hire.

Dates are calendar dates. Day counts are the difference between two dates. The Nov. 1, 2026 clock change does not affect them.

Day counts in this edition, as of Nov. 4, 2026:

- Sparks days open: Nov. 4 minus Sept. 25 = 40. On Nov. 3 the same subtraction is 39.
- Liberty days open: Nov. 4 minus Nov. 3 = 1.
- Sparks wait: Sept. 25 minus Sept. 24 = 1.
- Liberty wait: Nov. 3 minus Oct. 9 = 25.
- Sparks' 2024 search: Nov. 19, 2024 minus Sept. 24, 2024 = 56.
- Liberty's 2025 search: Nov. 21, 2025 minus Sept. 23, 2025 = 59 to the reported agreement. Dec. 3, 2025 minus Sept. 23, 2025 = 71 to the official hire.

Exits are labeled from the reporting, using five categories: Fired, Contract not renewed, Resigned, Mutual, or Terms unclear. A release that only says "parted ways" does not, by itself, decide the label. Miller's 2024 exit is Mutual because the team said the sides had mutually agreed to part ways. Brondello's 2025 exit is Contract not renewed because the team said it would not renew her contract. Roberts and DeMarco are Fired because that is how the beat and national reports describe those decisions.

An interim coach is an event. The permanent job stays open until a permanent coach is announced. Reported salary and buyout figures stay attached to the reporter who published them. They are not treated as confirmed cash payments. Unknown terms stay blank.

The Sparks' 2024 hire date in the table is Tuesday, Nov. 19, 2024, the day the WNBA.com story says the team announced it. That story's own timestamp is Nov. 20, 2024. Roberts was introduced at a press conference on Nov. 21, 2024. The introduction is not a second hire date.

Data

The tables above are the readable version of three files. The files are free to use and republish (CC0): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- searches_2027.csv, one row per search
- events_2027.csv, one row per dated event
- historical_comparisons.csv, one row per prior franchise search

Data dictionary

searches_2027.csv
- edition: season the new coach would coach. Here, 2027.
- search_id: stable id. las-2026-roberts, nyl-2026-demarco.
- franchise_id: LAS or NYL.
- outgoing_coach: coach who left.
- departure_type: Fired, Contract not renewed, Resigned, Mutual, or Terms unclear.
- record_2026: regular-season wins and losses.
- playoff_result_2026: result, including "missed playoffs" when there was no playoff game.
- final_game: date of the coach's last game, YYYY-MM-DD.
- departure_reported: date a credentialed outlet first reported the move. Blank if the team announcement is the first public record.
- departure_announced: team's public announcement. Day 0.
- deal_reported: date a permanent-hire agreement was reported. Blank while the job is open.
- hire_announced: date the team announced a permanent coach. Blank while the job is open.
- successor: new coach. Blank while the job is open.
- as_of: date used for days_open. 2026-11-04 in this edition.
- final_game_to_announcement_days: announcement date minus last-game date.
- days_open: as_of minus announcement date, or hire date minus announcement date once a permanent coach is announced.
- contract_note: reported terms, with the reporter. Blank when unknown.
- source_keys: letters pointing at the source list.

events_2027.csv
- event_id, search_id, season, franchise_id
- event_type: final_game, departure_reported, departure_confirmed, search_milestone, interim_named, deal_reported, permanent_hire, contract_report
- evidence_status: Reported, or Team confirmed
- subject, event_date, date_public
- source_url, source_attribution, summary

historical_comparisons.csv
- franchise_id, franchise, prior_cycle, outgoing_coach, departure_type
- departure_announced, hire_agreement_reported, hire_announced, successor
- days_to_reported_agreement, announcement_to_announcement_days
- source_keys

Game scores in this edition come from WNBA.com pages and Associated Press recaps on those pages. Coaching-news reports are cited to the reporter who wrote them.

1. Open-jobs table and historical table

Both tables are static HTML, in the first response, with no dependency on JavaScript. Use the columns in the article. Right-align the numeric columns. On a 375px screen, each team becomes a stacked block with the coach's name first, so the two names and the day counts are on the first screen along with the top of the chart.

Departure type is text, not a color alone. "Fired", "Mutual", and "Contract not renewed" are visible labels.

2. Vacancy clock

A horizontal bar chart, one row per open search, drawn as an inline SVG so it is crawlable and prints. The HTML table remains the canonical data. The chart repeats it.

Geometry. ViewBox about 0 0 760 220. A day is 8px. The zero line sits at x=220, which leaves room for New York's 25-day wait (200px) to the left. The scale runs from day 0 to day 80 so a 71-day precedent tick fits. Label the axis at 0, 20, 40, 60, and 80.

Default bars, with the decision-lag control off:
- Sparks: a bar from day 0 to day 40 in Sparks purple #702F8A. Black label above the bar: "Sparks, 40 days open". An open circle, 12px, stroke #702F8A, at the 40-day end.
- Liberty: the 1-day length is 8px, so draw a 14px open circle in Liberty seafoam #6ECEB2 at day 1, with a short bar behind it. Black label: "Liberty, 1 day open". Seafoam text on white fails contrast, so the words stay black. The color is the bar, not the type.

Legend, in text above the chart: open circle means the job is still open. A solid diamond will mean a permanent hire has been announced. A half diamond will mean a reported agreement that the team has not yet announced. Neither diamond is on the Nov. 4 chart. The historical table carries the 2025 half-step (Nov. 21 report, Dec. 3 announcement) so the legend is explaining a mark the page is ready to draw.

Decision-lag control. One checkbox, visible label "Include the wait before the announcement", off by default. CSS only: the lag segments are in the SVG, hidden until the checkbox is checked, using the sibling selector so it works without a script. When on, draw a muted bar to the left of day 0. Sparks: 1 day, 8px, purple at 35% opacity, label "1 day". Liberty: 25 days, 200px, seafoam at 35% opacity, label "25 days". The zero line is labeled "Announced". Keyboard users can tab to the checkbox and toggle it with the space bar. The table already shows the same numbers.

Franchise-precedent control. A second checkbox, label "Show this franchise's last search", off by default, same CSS pattern. When on:
- Sparks row: a 2px vertical tick at day 56, gold #FFC72C, with a black label "2024: 56 days, Roberts".
- Liberty row: a tick at day 59 labeled "2025: 59 days to the report" and a tick at day 71 labeled "71 days to the official hire".

Do not label either tick as a league average.

On a 375px screen, shrink the SVG to the content width. A day then falls to about 3px (the wait plus 80 days has to fit in roughly 340px). Keep the 14px circle so the Liberty row is still visible. Move each label above its bar. The first screen still shows both coaches' names and the start of the chart.

Team color is limited to the bars and the precedent tick. Body text, table text, and links stay in the site's existing colors.
