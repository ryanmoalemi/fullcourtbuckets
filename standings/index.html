How WNBA Standings Work

The eight teams with the best regular-season records qualify for the WNBA Playoffs. Playoff seeding is based on regular-season record rather than conference.

If teams finish with identical records, WNBA tiebreak procedures are used to determine playoff qualification and seeding.

Tiebreakers

  • Better head-to-head record
  • Better winning percentage against teams that finish .500 or better
  • Better head-to-head point differential
  • Better point differential against all opponents
